- ✓ **Enemy roster spellings** (2026-08-03, Taylor): `Taint`, `Shithead`, `Abcde`, `Cuck`, `Shirley`, `Lucky`.
- ✓ **`Tink` is not a separate enemy** — it was `Taint` misheard.
- ✓ **Carl Senior = `Carl A`, Carl Jr. = `Carl B`** from the S09 roster. Same pair, renamed.
- ✓ **Shirley is one creature**, not a matched pair.
- ✓ **Lucky's she/he inconsistency doesn't matter** — reviewed and dropped, don't re-flag it.
- ✓ **S11 villain entrance line** (2026-08-03, Taylor confirmed by listening to `02:05:09`): the transcriber's *"He rests a hand on his head **in a And** a massive thrall appears"* is really *"He rests a hand on his head **and** a massive thrall appears by his side."*

**Standing rules — do not put these on this list again:**
- **DM arithmetic** (gold splits, damage math, party-size divisors) gets recorded exactly as stated. We have an imperfect DM and that's fine.
- **Homebrew with no PHB match** gets filed in [[Homebrew Abilities]] as canon-in-play, no source hunt. Taylor doesn't have access to the DM's homebrew cards.
