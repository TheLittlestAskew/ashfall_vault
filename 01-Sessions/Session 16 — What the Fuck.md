## Summary

The morning after [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] died, the party gets a destination, drives east for two days, finds the tomb — and then stops everything to interrogate one of their own, correctly.

### The morning after

The recording opens cold, mid-conversation. [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] is unloading her grief onto [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]], who has been with the party for about a session and a half: *"we lost one of our party members very— in a very bad way… I don't know where we're going next, what we're doing next, if we're doing anything with her."* Lulu tries Valerian's door and is told to go away; the man himself emerges *"wrapped in his fucking blankets. Still some drool, like, dried in the corner of his mouth"* and tells them to take it down the hall. Zelda kicks his door — *"Because I'm short, I'm not gonna kick it open"* — and leaves.

Lulu tries [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] instead and gets the flattest grief in the campaign so far:

> *"Oh yeah, I heard Val throwing a bitch fit. Yeah, my sister died. What's up?"*

She offers to join properly, on the grounds that her *"last group couldn't hack it,"* and Samothy signs off on it. The DM gives **the entire table inspiration** for the scene.

### Hargraven decodes the tablet

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] reads out the **obsidian tablet** he pocketed in S15 — closing an S15 open flag. Protective wards; **Tharos Emberborn** named as a *pre-vampire hero*; a burial site sacred to *an old fiery deity*; and a riddle: *a mountain shaped like a broken crown, a river of black glass, a cavern mouth marked by three flame sigils, a coffin surrounded by burning chains*, closing on **"the graves lie where the mountain bleeds fire."**

[[Lt. Hargraven]] walks in and solves it on the spot. The broken crown is the **King's Mountain** — *"one big mountain pass at one point, and then a great earthshake came and fractured it, so now it's called the [[Broken Crown Mountains]]"* — east, visible from [[The Dead Zone]]. **Tharos Emberborn** *"was the wielder of the Grieveflame, the Flame Greaves. He was the legendary hero that helped with the Vampire War."* A ritual and burial site is rumoured at the base of the volcano there, once ruled by a fire-worshipping tribe gone over a thousand years.

Then [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asks the question that breaks the setting open: *"doesn't it also say in there that he's a pre-vampire hero? So what was there before the vampires that they needed heroes for?"*

> *"This is the Second Vampire War."*
> *"It's titled The Second Coming."*
> *"A lot of the lore and facts of the first Vampire War were taken and sealed away, and society eventually just forgot that vampires were even a thing outside of like fairy tales and folklore."*

He also mentions **the Book of Varona**, which somebody in the party is already carrying — Samothy thinks it's *"the book that, um, only I can read."* Hargraven rolls on the tablet and finds a **hidden compartment**: a **charred map fragment** (a cave mouth lit by three torches, two beside and one above), a **fire-etched sigil**, and a warning — *"only those who bleed fire may walk the path of the— of Emberborn."* The DM points at [[Vega Bloodroot]] unprompted: *"this ties directly into the sword that Vega has."*

Hargraven asks where the sister is. [[Flux]] opens the bag of holding.

> *"I'm trying to understand as to why you would put her in a bag of holding, but I fear that's just going to bring many more questions that we just— I don't, I don't feel like I want to know the answers to."*
> *"We were hoping to be able to bring her back, but, uh, we don't think that's gonna happen. That's why we had her in here."*

He can't come. He's been **summoned before the council** to answer for the party — *"you destroyed a perfectly good library. Or desecrated a few temples, some graves, things like that."* His parting brief: the mountains are east, no dragons, but **feral vampire beast thralls** are rumoured out there — ones that *"turned on their beast tamer, or their beast master, and ran east… tore him to shreds, and then vanished."*

### Zelda starts suspecting Valerian

Before they leave, Zelda checks on Val and something is wrong: *"he has this look in his eye where he actually looks sleepy."* Two Insight checks both come back that something is off. Samothy proposes **Zone of Truth** and Val instantly threatens **Dispel Magic** — *"Don't fucking cast spells on me."* Zelda casts **Detect Thoughts** twice and is rebuffed twice; his response is the session's signature line, thought at her rather than said:

> *"What the fuck, Richard? Is what they would have heard in my head, but they didn't pass their check."*

[[Flux]] asks about a funeral for Deanna. Nobody knows where to bury her. Nothing is decided.

### Two days east

Day one on the wagon: warming air, scarce wildlife, **massive claw marks on trees and boulders** — a Survival check reads them as thrall marks. They make camp. On watch, [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] spots *"a silhouette moving along the ridgeline, hunched, moving quickly, and it seems to be moving incorrect for a human,"* 300 feet out. She wakes [[Vega Bloodroot]], who is instantly tactical — *"to your left or to your right or towards like above… have they seen you?"*

Zelda casts **Detect Evil and Good** and finds the real problem, invisible to everyone else:

> *"There are bugs near you that are not of the living sort. They are long like worms. Think, think centipedes… they seem to be sapping energy away as everyone is sleeping."*
> *"It looks like they're leeching your aura or your life force away from you."*

She wakes the party — *"Val gets a kick in the ass. Everybody else is gentle."* Lulu, who has never heard of undead insects feeding on sleepers, calls it: **a necromancer in the area.** [[Flux]] hears the ridgeline creature moving *away*. Val blasts one worm with **Produce Flame** and the rest scatter. Vega wants to hunt the thing down — *"It's kind of violating. He didn't even want to fight"* — and gets outvoted.

Then Zelda remembers she has **Tiny Hut**, ritually casts it, and the party sleeps safe and disgusted.

> *"Wait, what? You had Tiny Hut so we didn't have to be fed off of by weird little things?"* — [[Vega Bloodroot]]
> *"Had Val sleeping on the fucking ground and you had a Tiny Hut this whole time?"* — "[[Valerian Hellebore]]"

Day two: blackened ground, sulfur in the air, a **half-eaten elk torn apart with feral brutality**. A Nature check rules out vampires — a predator did this. A History check lands perfectly and ties it to Hargraven's rumour: **the first beast thralls that revolted, killed their tamer and fled east.** The claw marks the whole way here are theirs.

### The volcano

The volcano has been inactive for years, but it smokes, it rains ash, the mountainside *"faintly glow[s] with natural heat,"* and **the river is obsidian, black glass.** Halfway up, the **three torches** from the map, over a cave mouth.

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] uses his smith's tools to work loose black glass rather than smash it, rolls a **d100**, and gets **51 pieces of obsidian** — *"some that are broken into pieces that could be used for like arrowheads or spear tips."* Fifteen gold apiece raw, and craftable into weapons with a bonus for slicing and piercing.

The cave walls are murals, and Religion checks read them in sequence:

> *"The mural in front of you shows vampires descending on the city like a plague… you see Tharos Emberborn, greaves blazing, charging into battle… you see the Sunfire blade piercing the vampire lord atop a stone altar, with the blade left embedded in the altar as a seal… the last one is Emberborn leading the last stand inside this very mountain."*

A second reading names the deity: **a sanctuary of a fire deity whose name was Emberos**, later repurposed as a tomb. Deeper in: claw marks gouged into the walls, scattered bones, *"a low distant growling echo that never seems to come from the same direction twice."* [[Flux]] rolls a **natural 20** Insight and learns they are being watched from somewhere unfindable, and that *"the air around you feels heavy with anger… like violence."*

### The trap floor

A room with two facing doors, chains on the walls, a great sun figure in the centre, and a floor of **glyphs and pressure plates** with small stepping stones between them. No way around.

[[Vega Bloodroot]] volunteers to go first. She **rages and holds the rage**; Lulu casts **Pass Without Trace**. She steps on a plate — *"you feel your foot sink down into the floor a little more than normal, and radiant flame comes shooting out, but that you narrowly avoid."* Then she works the cracks. Burning her inspiration and taking a **+4 from Samothy's Flash of Genius**, she **identifies over three-quarters of the pressure plates**, marks the path, walks back to brief the party, and takes everyone across safely.

> *"I'll explain to everybody what I see so that they can tell, um, themselves also where the pressure plates are, or they could, um, follow my foot— follow my path if they need to."*

⚠️ The DM ruled **Pass Without Trace does not apply to saves**, only Stealth checks.

### The chain room, and the interrogation

The next room's **chains whip back and forth, reach out, grab things, and are red hot.** The party never crosses it. Instead, in the doorway, they spring the trap they have been planning by private message all session:

**Lulu casts Entangle. [[Barrett Grimmskar]] grapples him. [[Vega Bloodroot]] takes him and holds him down single-handedly. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] casts Zone of Truth.** He makes his save — and [[Flux]] burns **Silvery Barbs** to make him reroll it lower. **He fails.**

What follows is forty minutes of a man who cannot deliberately lie and does not need to. He works out the loophole immediately and says so:

> *"It just guards from deception… If everyone's lying, I don't have to answer shit."*
> *"It's cute that you guys all planned this together."*

He answers questions with questions, reflects them back, and never once states a falsehood. *"When did you see Val for the first time?"* — *"When I— the same day I looked into a mirror for the first time."* *"What are you?"* — *"Alive? What the fuck kind of question is that."* *"What creature are you?"* — *"Do I not fucking look like an elf to you?"*

The pressure escalates badly. [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] holds a **silver-tipped dagger** against him. Lulu casts **Pins and Needles** and shuts down his left arm, then a leg. [[Barrett Grimmskar]] presses a **silver bullet to his skin** — the DM initially rules on the cloak, then, once it's established the bullet touched *skin*, **there is a reaction.** Val is reduced to pleading:

> *"You're freaking me out. Can you just please get off of me? Like, I cannot. I'm like about to have a panic attack."*

[[Vega Bloodroot]] is the only one uncomfortable with it: *"I was told we were just gonna restrain you."*

Then it breaks. Zelda lands **Detect Thoughts** on the third attempt. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] uses **Hunter's Sense**:

> *"He's a changeling."* — *"Genetically altered changeling."*

Zelda deep-dives his memories: *"you come across a tower And a tied-up Val. Those are the last 2 images."* A History check: **the tower is very familiar. She saw it recently.**

He stops pretending. He stomps and casts **Depth Charge**; Zelda **Counterspells at 4th level**, he'd cast at 6th, and it goes off — DC 17 Con, **27 damage** to anyone who failed. He **Wild Shapes into a fly** and runs for it, and [[Vega Bloodroot]] catches him **out of the air on a natural 20**, closes her hand without crushing him, and re-grapples him the moment he drops the shape.

> *"I'm literally restraining him single-handedly, might I add."* — [[Vega Bloodroot]]
> *"And she caught him as a fly in midair, which was amazing."* — Taylor (DM)

Cornered, he gives them everything except the location:

> *"I've been with you for weeks."* — *"And none of you noticed."*
> *"What's your goal here?"* — ***"Chaos."***
> *"You think I'm some just mindless zombie who follows master's orders? No. **He pays me well.**"* — *"**He made me.**"*
> *"You really should have figured this out, oh, I don't know, when I was steering you away from whatever the fuck that was in the woods, but sure."*
> *"You caught me after 3 weeks… Imagine what I saw. Imagine what I did. Imagine the chaos I sowed."*

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] shoots out both kneecaps and offers to drag him on a rope. [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] tries to **outbid his master**. Zelda argues for keeping him alive; Flux and Lulu argue for killing him. His pockets are empty.

They stop there, by agreement — *"We pause where we're at… it's also a good pause point."* **S17 opens inside this standoff.**

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Grief with nowhere to put it.** The session opens on three separate people failing to process Deanna in three different registers — Zelda by talking too much, Samothy by deadpan, "Val" by hostility. Nobody buries her. She stays in the bag all session, and the party keeps making jokes about her that get progressively worse.
- **The forgetting is the horror.** The biggest reveal is not that there was a first Vampire War — it's that the records *"were taken and sealed away"* until vampires became folklore. Someone chose that. The setting's defiant hope now has an active adversary in the historical record itself.
- **Defiant hope, literalised.** The session's central image is a hero charging in *with greaves blazing* and a blade left in an altar to keep the dark shut. The party is walking into that story to pick up the pieces of it.
- **The party finally noticed — and it took weeks.** *"And none of you noticed"* is the cruellest line of the night, and it is true. The tell that broke it was mundane: elves don't sleep, and he slept.
- **Vega holds him and disagrees with holding him.** She is the physical instrument of an interrogation she never endorsed, saying *"I was told we were just gonna restrain you"* into a room that doesn't answer. When she wins — catching him blind out of the air — she closes her hand and does not crush. Both halves are hers again: the discipline and the surrender.
- **Cruelty arrives fast and casually.** Needles, a hole, a silver bullet, an offer to remove kneecaps and drag him on a rope — inside forty minutes, against someone the party still half-believed was their friend. Only one PC flinched.
- **The table teaches itself rules, again.** The elf-sleep argument ran on actual 2024 PHB text, was argued well on both sides (*"the can and if there make it a possibility rather than a mandatory"*), and was correctly deferred to the DM rather than won.
