## Summary

The party goes looking for a map, and finds a man who is afraid of the outside and better at describing it than anyone who has walked it.

Down a narrow spiral staircase beyond the war room, past walls that grow colder with each step and scraps of parchment shoved into the cracks "as if someone tried to keep the darkness from seeping through," they reach a door reinforced with iron bands and barricaded from the inside. A thin voice calls through it: ***"If you're enthralled, you can't come in. If you're not enthralled, prove it."*** After a pause the barricade shifts, a single ink-stained eye appears, and the party is let into a cramped workshop overflowing with scrolls, quills and half-finished maps — which is not a shop at all, but the man's living room. He never leaves it.

**[[Marrowin Quill]]** is wiry and pale, his fingers stained black, his hair sticking out "as though he's been arguing with it." He slides three locks home behind them. He is agoraphobic to the point of never going outdoors, and he is a savant: he can map almost anything **without ever having seen it**. The walls of his home are covered in his work, and in phrases he has written over and over — *the walls lie, the veins speak true.*

He knows why they have come. *"You're looking for the tunnels beneath the city, the veins. Dangerous business, foolish business, necessary business."* He cannot map the whole catacombs — *"they change, they breathe, they remember"* — but he can give them the part that doesn't lie, and he begins to draw, narrating in "a low rhythmic murmur, half explanation, half confession":

- **The [[Ossuary Approach]]** — *"old kings built this, lined the walls with skulls so their enemies would feel watched."*
- **The [[Lantern Spine]]** — *"Vampires added that. Blue flame, cold flame. Don't trust it. It burns without heat, but it hungers."*
- **The [[Flooded Gallery]]** — *"Blood run off from the upper floors. Don't fall in. Things float in there that shouldn't."*
- **The [[Broken Descent]]** — his quill hesitates. *"Beast thralls patrol it. They smell fear, they smell hope, they smell everything."*
- And below it all, circled in thick dark ink: **the veins**, the tunnels beneath the city, *"the arteries of the vampire empire."*

He also marks a faint bypass near the Ossuary Approach — *"hidden, narrow, useful if you're being hunted"* — and warns that the map tells truths but not all of them: *"Sometimes lines are older than others. Some symbols are warnings, some are apologies."* [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] gives him a potion from **Charlotte**, which visibly moves him, and asks for a second map of the western mountains; he has none, but promises one within a day. He would rather they did not linger: *"It's not that I don't like you, but I don't like strangers in my house for longer than they have to be in here."*

The party briefly debates waiting for the second map and votes it down — [[Vega Bloodroot]] settling it with *"I say this with all due respect to the women in this group, y'all being a bunch of pussies, let's just go in the fucking catacombs."*

Through a sewer grate in the castle, down a service ladder, and into the old catacombs. The stairway is *"carved by hands that feared what they were creating"*; the walls carry jagged symbols of protection, warding and desperation, **some scratched out as if someone tried to undo their own magic**. Cold drafts carry whispers that never resolve into words — and the DM is explicit that **everyone hears them**, and that nobody says so, because no one wants to be the one who admits it.

[[Valerian Hellebore]] spots a hidden alcove (Perception 16) holding a rusted lantern with a still-burning ember of **cold flame**. Reaching for it, a door snaps shut on his arm; he panics, can't pull free, and then it pops open (Constitution 19) and the torch comes loose. It sheds a 25-foot circle of light through the vampires' magical darkness — the one thing ordinary darkvision cannot penetrate.

The **[[Ossuary Approach]]** opens ahead: rows of skulls in wall alcoves, hollow eyes reflecting the torchlight, the silence *"not empty, but expectant."* Every row is perfectly symmetrical **except one skull, turned slightly toward the stairway**. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] goes to investigate it. As the party passes, a few skulls exhale faintly — not words, but mimicry: their own footsteps, breaths, heartbeats, played back. Turning the marked skull cracks its jaw open onto a pressure plate, and the bypass tunnel Marrowin drew hisses open.

They have disturbed too many alcoves. **Two [[Bonewraith|Bonewraiths]]** form out of swirling dust, and release a *memory echo* — the sound of ancient workers arguing about the horrors below, moments before their deaths — which hits like a blast wave. Everyone makes the Constitution save except [[Valerian Hellebore]].

**The fight lasts one round of the party's turns.** [[Flux]] opens with the ritual **Rite of the Dawning Radiance**, granting six willing creatures **Sunward Blessing**: resistance to necrotic damage, advantage on saves against charm, fear and vampiric domination, and +1d6 radiant on weapon attacks. [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] lands a 5th-level **Inflict Wounds** for 17 and follows with **Finger Guns** into the second wraith. [[Barrett Grimmskar]] puts two revolver rounds downrange. [[Mr. Cat]] rolls a natural 1, connects anyway for 6, and then his leg locks mid-swing and he falls prone. [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] runs in dual-wielding her fire-and-cold daggers, stabs Wraith A from both sides of the neck, and **kills it** — *"not today, you spooky bitch"* — then marks the survivor with **Slayer's Prey**. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] hits twice with a **Ballistic Smite** for thunder damage and reports the target at 13 HP via a passive feature. [[Vega Bloodroot]] charges in with the **Staff of Flames** — 27 to hit — and finishes it for 20.

Both wraiths crumble to dust; the tattered clothes they wore fall, hit the ground, and vanish.

The party is left standing between the narrow bypass — which only [[Mr. Cat]], [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]], [[Valerian Hellebore]] and [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] can fit through — and the main passage. Deanna squeezes in on an acrobatics roll the DM describes as *"a chicken with its head cut off trying to fit through this hole."* [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] floats blasting the opening wider and is reminded there is a castle sitting directly above them. **The [[Lantern Spine]] is next.** Play stops there.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **A man who maps what he cannot face.** Marrowin is the session's whole thesis: total knowledge of a world he will not step into. He draws the catacombs perfectly and locks three bolts behind his guests.
- **The dungeon remembers.** Every environmental beat is about memory rather than threat — walls that *"change, they breathe, they remember,"* carvings scratched out by their own makers, skulls that play back your heartbeat, and a wraith whose weapon is a recording of men dying.
- **Warnings and apologies.** Marrowin's line that some symbols on his map are warnings and *"some are apologies"* is the best single line of the night, and nothing in the session explains it yet.
- **Nobody says they hear it.** The DM's ruling that the whispers are audible to everyone *and* that no one will admit it is a small, excellent piece of table psychology.

## Logs

### Encounters

**The Bonewraiths of the Ossuary Approach.** Two wraiths, raised by disturbing too many skull alcoves. Opened with a party-wide Constitution save against a *memory echo*; only [[Valerian Hellebore]] failed. Resolved inside a single round of party turns with no PC brought low.

### Encounter Summary

The party's action economy simply outran the encounter. **Sunward Blessing** landing first meant every subsequent weapon attack carried +1d6 radiant against undead, and the two wraiths — who between them landed one meaningful hit each, 19 damage on [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] and a strike on [[Vega Bloodroot]] — never got a second turn. The fight's real function was to prove the Ossuary Approach is not scenery: **disturbing the alcoves has a cost**, and the party disturbed exactly one, twenty minutes of table time before it mattered.

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **The campaign now has a dungeon with a named route.** Marrowin's map converts a vague "catacombs" into five discrete, described stages plus a goal. That is a structural change: sessions 14+ have a spine.
- **Environmental storytelling is doing the work.** No NPC explains the catacombs' history; the walls, the scratched-out wardings and the mimicking skulls do. Expect the Lantern Spine's cold flame to behave the same way.
- **Undead are the enemy type now**, and [[Flux]]'s Sunward Blessing is built precisely for it — necrotic resistance, anti-charm/fear/domination, radiant damage. The party's kit has begun to specialise against vampires.
