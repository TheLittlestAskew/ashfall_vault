## POV Journal

> *[[Vega Bloodroot]] — Half-Orc Barbarian, Path of the Wild Heart*

The thing wearing Val's face wanted us to watch it enjoy itself. So I stopped letting it.

I had it by the arms. I asked the others what they wanted from me and nobody could say it plain, so I decided for them. I reached around its front, put one fingernail against its throat, and drew a line — shallow, the way you score bark before you split it. Then I told it about death by a thousand cuts, and I said: *one.* I told it my counting isn't good. Barbarian, no schooling. We might have to start over a few times.

It cried. It had taken Deanna's face by then, and it cried, and I put the second cut exactly along the track the tear made. I want to be honest in this book, so: I am not sorry. I am something, but it isn't sorry.

Samothy never blinked. Not once. Whatever the thing was reaching for in him, it didn't find it — and later, when it was down and bound, he rolled it over and took its legs away for good. *He changed his face to my dead sister.* That was all he said about it. I asked, once, if it could be fixed later. I don't know what I wanted the answer to be.

We carried it through the chains like luggage. Zelda cracked it across the skull with the hilt of her sword and for a while it was quiet, and I remember being glad of the quiet more than I was glad of anything else.

Then the fire asked me a question.

An echo of a man long dead stood up out of the flame and asked whether I carry fire without fear, whether I'd stand against the night the way he did. I didn't have a clever answer. I said I don't know much about you, my guy, but I could try. And then I gave him the only true thing I had: I vowed to get Val back. His body or his soul, whichever one is left to get, no matter what it takes. The others put their hands in after mine — bow, sword, fist, gun, whip — and the echo bowed his head and let us pass.

I meant it. That's the part I need written down. I have been the muscle so long I forgot I could be the one who promises.

The tomb gave up its gauntlets and crumbled to ash with a sound like relief, and then the dead came in through the tunnel with no eyes and a great deal of hunger, and I finally got to swing the new sword.

It bit me back. Second time it's done that — the same pull I felt dragging it out of the stone. It clamped onto my hand and would not let go, and my palm opened up, and it is still bleeding as I write this, because I have no way to set it down. There are monks somewhere who can take it off me. I don't know where yet. I'll find out.

We killed the thralls. The thing wearing Val's face got torn apart by one of them while it lay there helpless, and screamed, and Zelda — Zelda, who it had been whispering inside of all night — stopped it dying. Then thought better of it, and let it go.

I couldn't do it myself. I'll put that here too. I looked at it and I saw my friend's face and my hand would not move.

When it finally went, something came out of it. A wind that smelled like moss and pine and salt and wet earth, and it went through every one of us like it was looking for somebody. It didn't find him. So it left us each a crown of flowers and went up into the rock.

That was Val's magic, they say. All of it, leaving at once, because it had nowhere left to live.

He's still alive up in that tower. I swore it in front of the fire and I'll swear it here: we are coming.

I'm keeping the flowers.

## Summary

### Where we left off

The party is mid-standoff in the chain room of the volcano tomb, holding the captured impostor. The DM's one-line recap at 00:12:02: *"last we left, uh, Val was held prisoner against his will."* The table immediately corrects him — *"Fake Val. Fake Val."* — and [[Barrett Grimmskar]] notes the real one is *"locked in a tower somewhere"* and has been gone **three weeks** in-fiction.

### The interrogation, and Deanna's face

The impostor makes its play for [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]: *"I'll enjoy it more than dear Deanna did"* — and shapechanges into [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]], Samothy's dead sister, pleading *"Samothy, please, you're hurting me!"*

[[Vega Bloodroot]] answers it in kind. Holding it from behind, she draws a shallow line across its throat with a fingernail, whispers *"you ever heard about death by a thousand cuts?"*, and counts **one**. When the thing cries, she places cut number two along the exact path of the tear. Samothy makes his CON save (13) and simply **deadpan stares**, giving it nothing.

The impostor tries to palm Vega's dagger — Sleight of Hand **9** against her Perception **11**. It fails.

### What to do with it

A long table debate over maiming versus killing: cut off its arms and legs *"and turn him into a worm"*, a bone saw Samothy can conjure, Polymorph, a gag, or — Samothy's alternative — *"torture him by just playing my bagpipes for hours on end."* The DM, on the record: *"Table, I don't have anything set up for a torture scene outside of what's here."*

It escapes Vega's grip briefly (she rolls STR 18, DEX 6), and [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] clubs it unconscious with the hilt of her shortsword for **11**. The party gags, hogties and binds it.

Then [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] flips it onto its stomach and **severs its spine**. *"He changed his face to my dead sister. He's not gonna walk for the rest of his fucking life again."* The DM, dryly: *"So maybe don't leave him with Sammy."* From here on it is carried like luggage — the table's own word is **"a handbag."**

### Zelda goes inside its head

Still mind-linked from Detect Thoughts, Zelda probes the unconscious impostor (Insight 16, Arcana 11) and gets three things:

1. **The real [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s location** — a tower she recognises exactly.
2. **The impostor is not a natural changeling.** It was *genetically engineered and modified* by vampires, who lost access to real changelings long ago and *"tried to genetically engineer their own. So this would be the first successful attempt of growing a changeling."*
3. **Its master exists but cannot be seen** — *"you do see a person, but you can't see their face. It's like all— when you look at it, it looks blurred."*

[[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]], on the vampires: *"They were too preoccupied with whether or not they could and never thought to ask whether or not they should."*

Zelda drops the link — the DM rules it fades *"like Internet Explorer loading, and then you close it out and you have to wait 10 minutes"* — and while it lingers she floods it with mental images of every cute animal she has ever met. It answers: *"You spend an awfully long time staring at the asses of cats, don't you, darling?"*

### The chains

The chains were built to bind undead; the bones on the floor are what they caught. Crossing requires DEX saves. [[Flux]] (18) and Vega (18) and Barrett (21) get across clean; Zelda makes it *"just barely"* at 14. Samothy skips the roll entirely using his **Boots of the Winding Path**, teleporting 15 feet at a time and carrying **Mr. Cat**, who the DM rules is *"too small for the chains to recognize him."* Lulu is caught and slammed back against the wall for 1d6 fire — then **wild shapes into a fly** to escape, and spends the next stretch buzzing the impostor's eyes and nose.

### The trap floor and the echo

A warning: *one false stone conceals a 20-foot drop* — not lava, but burning hot rock. [[Barrett Grimmskar]]'s Investigation **16** spots the inconsistencies in texture and reflection. ⚑ The DM volunteers the bypasses afterwards: **casting light, daylight or radiant spells would have disrupted the illusion**, as would a long jump or a rope crossing.

On the far side a **fiery apparition of Tharos Emberborn** manifests — *not hostile but imposing* — and asks one question:

> *"Do you carry the fire without fear?"* … *"Will you stand against the night as I once did?"* … *"Show me your resolve."*

No combat. The DM runs it as pure roleplay: **a bold declaration, a selfless act, a vow, or a symbolic gesture** — and *"if you hesitate, lie, or show cowardice, bad things happen."*

Vega vows **to get Val back — "no matter what it takes, um, to either retrieve his body or free his soul."** Lulu asks if it can be a group pledge, and the table turns it into one, Fellowship-style: *"You have my bow, you have my sword, you have my fist" / "You have my gun" / "You have my whip."* The echo bows its head and dissipates.

⚑ Vega also asks to leave **a meaningful token** behind — a ring — somewhere she will have to come back for. The DM says she can do it in the next room, and that the place she wants is **also where the monks are** who can lift a curse. **He deferred its name and location on the record** (01:33:03): *"I will have to get back to you on their name and location later because I'm not going to go find that note."*

### The tomb

A circular chamber, domed ceiling of black glass, runes glowing faintly, a stone sarcophagus on a raised dais and two braziers burning with smokeless orange flame. Emberborn lies *"perfectly preserved, hands crossed over his chest,"* wearing the **Gauntlets of Burning Grasp**.

The party punches the corpse, boops its nose, and casts Animate Dead at it. Nothing happens. Flux simply **takes the gauntlets** — and the body crumbles:

> *"His skin flakes away like ash. His armor collapses inward. His bones turn to dust. A warm breeze sweeps through the chamber, almost like a sigh of relief. A final whisper echoes: let the fire walk with you."*

### The thralls

At **01:39:46** the tomb shakes and **six feral vampire beast thralls** come in through a tunnel. The DM's briefing: these are the ones who *"turned and killed their beast master guy"*; even regular vampires avoid them. **They have no eyes and hunt by smell**, so Daylight does not blind them — but they are **weak to radiant, thunder and silvered weapons**, and take extra damage every turn in sunlight. Their CR was scaled up to match the party.

Highlights:

- **Vega's new sword bites her.** After her first attacks (34 and 28 damage), a CON save of **8** triggers it: *"It is clamped to your hand. You can't remove it now… And you are bleeding."* 7 slashing per turn. **Remove Curse will not work** — the DM: *"it's story-driven."* Only **the monks** can lift it.
- **The impostor is not out of the fight.** It works out that **Ice Knife** needs no verbal component and only a drop of water — and argues sweat counts. The DM allows it. With its hands tied facing two random directions it rolls a d12 for a target, hits **Flux**, and Zelda **Counterspells**.
- **Thrall B mauls the helpless impostor.** Prone, bound and paralysed, it is auto-hit for 12 and 20 and drops to **0 hit points**. *"You guys all of a sudden hear Fake Val screaming bloody murder."*
- **Barrett defends the thing that has been tormenting them**, *"begrudgingly"* — Spellfire Flare at 2nd level, a Heroic Inspiration reroll turning a 10 into a **natural 20**, for 30 radiant.
- **Zelda stabilises it** with Spare the Dying. The impostor: *"Can I refuse?"* Zelda: *"You're not Val, but you are a being."*
- **Lulu ends one thrall with Contagion** (11d8 necrotic) — *"Begone, thot"* — then immediately **Animate Dead**s the corpse as a bonus action. *"He's my little minion now."*
- **Samothy** works through Pistol, a 4th-level **Ballistic Smite** (5d6 acid), **Branding Smite**, and Vex/Sap mastery; **Mr. Cat** takes a swing and misses by one.

### The death of the impostor

With the fight effectively over, the table turns to the thing on the floor. Vega cannot do it — *"Chase, I have this problem in my brain that this character is tied to you and I don't want to kill you."* Samothy wants it left to *"just wiggle around."* Zelda asks to **retroactively not have cast Spare the Dying**; the DM allows it, and she finishes it with Finger Guns for 10.

**The death scene was written and performed by the player**, with the DM's permission:

> *"An arcane mist smelling of moss and pine and sea salt and rain-dampened earth, like a wind, passes around each of you, searching, almost, if wind could search, for something or someone, weaving in between each of you like a large snake made of a soft summer breeze. Seemingly not finding what it searches for, it's coiled around each of you, sweeping up your bodies, leaving behind a crown of flowers on each of your heads before slowly dissipating into the rock above."*

His explanation: that was **Valerian's magic**, stored in the changeling, unable to find its host or its source, dissipating into the ether. *"And that was the last gasps of his magic."*

🛑 **Val himself is alive.** Only his magic is gone. Vega: *"Oh, is Val gonna be just a guy now?"*

⚠️ **The flower crowns may be permanent** — the player's stated intention is that they are, and Vega asks to keep hers. **Not ruled by the DM.**

## Archivist Notes

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **The party solved a roleplay trial with a promise, not a fight.** The Emberborn echo explicitly could not be fought, and the table's answer — a vow that became a group oath — is the emotional spine of the session and the mechanical hook for the next one. **Ashfall's "defiant hope" core ran straight through this scene.**
- **Cruelty has stopped costing them anything.** Between Vega's thousand cuts and Samothy's spine-severing, the party tortured and permanently maimed a bound prisoner, and the debate was entirely logistical. Barrett's grudging defence of the dying impostor is the only counterweight.
- **The DM's plot was overridden by out-of-game coordination**, on the record and without rancour. Worth watching: he had three sessions of swapped items and missing objects prepared, and said *"who's to say things haven't gone missing?"* through the impostor's mouth. **That may not have been a joke.**
- **Two new engines of story were installed this session** — a cursed sword that can only be fixed at an unnamed monastery, and a vow that requires returning to that same place. They point the party in the same direction.
- **A player wrote canon.** The impostor's death text was authored and performed by his own player with the DM's permission, and it established a lore fact (magic stored in the changeling, dissipating without its host). ⚑ **Attribution needs a decision** — see below.
