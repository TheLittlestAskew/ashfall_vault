## Summary

The party goes looking for a relic and comes back with a cursed one, a pile of gems, and a druid who nearly died twice.

### The hidden chamber

At the junction, [[Flux]] notices on passive Perception that one wall "looks off" and warns everyone — *"hopefully it's not another mimic."* It isn't. It dips into a hidden room whose floor, ceiling and pillars are covered in hieroglyphics glowing faintly orange. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] casts Identify: the symbols aren't magical at all, they're **reacting to the heat of the chamber**. A low rhythmic hum runs through it, *"almost like a distant forge."*

The party's dice open catastrophically — **four natural 1s inside three minutes** across History, Arcana, Religion and Investigation. What gets through establishes three things:

- The hieroglyphics identify someone as an **Emberborn**, *a pre-vampire hero*.
- The symbols are **protective wards meant to keep something in, not out**.
- On a Religion check of 15+, this is the **burial site of a sacred old fire deity**.

An Investigation check finds a **hidden compartment** in the altar beneath an **obsidian tablet**, holding a **charred map fragment** showing sewer or tunnel lines the party almost recognises — *"you think you know the area, but it's just out of reach."* Samothy takes the tablet.

[[Valerian Hellebore]] proposes testing the sigils with blood, and specifically with **Lulu's** — *"I don't want to get fucked up by a demon."* Lulu doesn't argue the point; she pulls a dagger and opens her own palm. Nothing happens.

### The choice

Back at the junction: two paths, one under an ominous **red beating light**, one under guttering torches. The party seriously considers splitting up, and then doesn't, for the bluntest reason available:

> *"I vote we stick together because last time Deanna got separated from the group, she died."* — [[Barrett Grimmskar]]
> *"And the last time Lulu was separated from her group, they fucking left her."* — [[Valerian Hellebore]]

They take the red light, on [[Flux]]'s reasoning that the scary one is less likely to be the ambush and [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s that *"nothing fun ever happened taking the safe road."*

⚠️ Valerian suggests, mostly seriously, that **Lulu should walk in first** — *"we have seen the consequences... maybe Lulu should go first."* Chase then calls out his own table for agreeing to it: *"let's make Madi, who just lost a character and had to just make a new one, go first."* They send her in anyway.

### The Boilborn

Beyond the door: a **sword embedded in stone**, glowing ember-orange, with the chamber's red light pouring off the **pedestal** beneath it rather than the blade. And four twitching things covered in pustules.

[[Valerian Hellebore]] rolls a **natural 20 on Nature (29)** and learns nothing — *"these creatures are like nothing you've ever seen or heard of before."* They move jerkily, "like they got a tick," and they stink of decay. The party calls them zombies all night. They are not zombies.

The fight runs roughly thirteen rounds, and the dice are historically bad: **8 natural 1s across 118 d20s**, with three separate players told to change their dice.

[[Vega Bloodroot]] rages in and immediately discovers the mechanic — hitting one **bursts a boil that sprays 2d8 poison back at her**, and a failed Con save leaves her poisoned. She checks whether it'll happen again (Insight says no), swings anyway, and it doesn't. **Valerian casts Call Lightning at 5th level** and re-triggers it every single round for the rest of the fight, which is most of his contribution and most of the damage. **Barrett** swaps his Magnum for his fire revolver and lands a crit for **42**, plus **Gut Shot** — speed halved, disadvantage on attacks. **Samothy** stacks **Slayer's Prey**, later **Hunter's Mark**, and grinds one down with Vex and Sap.

**Zelda gets grappled**, taking 3d6 poison at the start of each of its turns, and the table stops to research whether **Misty Step escapes a grapple**. They work it out correctly and out loud — a grapple sets your speed to 0 but doesn't restrain you, and nothing in the condition prevents teleportation — and she pops free.

🛑 **Lulu goes down twice.** A **Rupture Burst** (DC 16 Con) catches her, Flux and Zelda for 30/30/15 and drops her; a natural 1 on the follow-up Dex save finishes the job. The table's grief is not what it was for Deanna:

> *"She's with Deanna now."* — *"She was only here for a few minutes."* — *"We hardly knew her."* — *"Weeks from now, somebody's gonna go, hey, you guys remember Lulu?"*

Valerian announces he'll *"start picking over her corpse,"* declines to let anyone waste a potion — *"it's fine, it's her time"* — and when [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] heals her anyway, clarifies that he cast Cure Wounds *"with my foot on her."* Zelda burns Bardic Inspiration to boost the healing both times. Lulu, revived, thanks everyone.

[[Vega Bloodroot]] kills the last one, and the DM finally says what they were:

> *"The **Boilborn**. What you're fighting now. They're not zombies... I made this enemy specifically to fight the barbarian."*

He'd built them as an **anti-tank**, aimed squarely at Vega. His own assessment afterwards: *"the tank didn't take as much damage as I thought. **Lulu got demolished.** Flux almost died too."* [[Flux]] confirms she'd be dead without the **Tough** feat.

### The sword

With the room clear, Vega investigates the blade — magic, but she can't tell what kind, and *"nothing seems out of the ordinary."*

> *"Fuck it, I'm at full health. I touch it."*

*"As you grab the hilt, you feel a stabbing pain in your palm. Blood starts to run down the blade and your hand locks to the hilt. You cannot let go."* Three Strength saves, with a barbarian's advantage on all of them: **20, 25, 20.**

*"The sword rips free from the stone. Flames erupt all along the blade. The chamber shakes as the heartbeat light extinguishes."*

She sheathes it immediately and informs the party that none of them can have it. ⚠️ Failure would have cost **3d6 necrotic**, and the DM confirms he had a **separate outcome written for anyone who grabbed it mid-combat** — which nobody tried, and which he never revealed.

### The mine

The torch-lit path leads to an old mineshaft, and the DM is unusually candid about why: *"They are all here for you guys to take. That's why I brought you here."* Raw gem veins line the walls. [[Flux]] uses **Mold Earth** to strip the earth from every vein, giving everyone advantage; [[Valerian Hellebore]] proposes **Stone Shape** to cut a gem door and have Vega kick it off its hinges, then starts working out how many spell slots it would take to refine the whole haul himself.

⚠️ Valerian also notices the obvious: *"Isn't just blasting at earth what fucking got Deanna—"* Flux's answer is that **that** room was cursed and this one isn't.

The haul: **5 star rubies, 5 sapphires, 4 alexandrite, 3 fire opals, 3 blue spinel** — all raw, and worth less until a **jeweler** refines them for a fee. Gold is scarce in this world and refined gems circulate as currency.

Zelda casts **Teleportation Circle** back to base, which triggers the session's last argument — Valerian rounding on her with *"Deanna died and you could have teleported us this whole time?"* until Flux points out you can only teleport to an anchor you've already established, and establishing a new one takes about a year. He apologises. Barrett prompts: *"This is the part where you say thank you, Z."*

The lowest level of the catacombs: the hidden fire-deity shrine, the red-lit relic chamber, and the old mineshaft — then home.

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **The party learned exactly one lesson from Deanna, and applied it immediately.** Nobody split up. It was the first thing said.
- **And they learned the wrong lesson too** — the response to danger was to send the newest, most expendable member first, which Chase named at the table rather than letting it slide.
- **Grief has a hierarchy.** Deanna's death stopped the session cold for twenty minutes. Lulu's got a punchline within seconds. She'd been there about ninety minutes.
- **Vega earned the sword and knows it.** *"For something that was designed to hurt me and for me to come out on the other side the way that I did — definitely deserved that sword."*
- **The table teaches itself rules.** The Misty-Step-versus-grapple question was researched, argued and settled correctly by four people in about ninety seconds.

## Archivist Notes

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **Deanna's death changed party tactics immediately and permanently.** Splitting up was raised and killed in under a minute, by name. That is the single clearest behavioural consequence in the campaign so far.
- **The DM is now building encounters against specific PCs.** The Boilborn were designed to punish Vega's tankiness, and he said so unprompted. Expect more targeted design — and note it didn't work, which he also said.
- **Lulu is load-bearing and fragile.** She is the party's only real healer and she hit 0 HP twice in her second session. The party has no backup if she goes down for good.
- **The party is now wealthy.** Twenty raw gems in a world where gold is scarce and refined gems are currency. That is very likely the 1,000gp Reincarnate funding.
- **Vega is accumulating fire.** Staff of Flames (S13), and now a burning sword. Plus a cursed item nobody understands.
