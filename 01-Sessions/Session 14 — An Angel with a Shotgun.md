## Summary

The party learns that the catacombs kill you for being thorough.

### The Lantern Spine

Picking up where Session 13 stopped, the party enters the **[[Lantern Spine]]** — a long corridor lit by lanterns burning with cold blue flame, where *"the light bends shadows unnaturally, stretching them into shapes that don't match"* their owners. Those who pass the Perception check watch their own shadows **detach, lag behind, and snap back into place**. [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s reaction is immediate and in-character: *"Am I going crazy? Did I just blink? Because it looked like my shadow blinked."*

Touching a lit lantern snuffs it — and the flame **reappears further down the corridor on a lantern that was dark**. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s Detect Magic reads the lanterns and nothing else: pure **illumination magic**, *"their sole purpose must be light."* The walls are not magical at all.

Which makes what Samothy sees next worse. On a **natural 20 Insight (26)** he watches the shadows react to something deeper in, and finds **one section of wall that expands and contracts as if inhaling the cold flame**. [[Flux]] remembers [[Marrowin Quill]] warning them about the walls. [[Valerian Hellebore]] puts his face close to it and feels **actual breath** — a cool gust, no smell but old tunnel.

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] hands Vega **Heroism** (4 temp HP, immunity to fright) and Vega rages and touches the wall. Nothing happens. So she mimes being electrocuted. Everyone screams except Zelda and Samothy, who both make the Constitution save — Zelda's on a **natural 20 (23)**.

Samothy ends the debate by throwing a rock at it. **Roll initiative.**

### Two mimics, one round

Meanwhile [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] is somewhere else entirely. She took the narrow bypass tunnel alone, made her Athletics check (a "dirty 20"), and is working her way through with [[Mr. Cat]] padding along behind her. When the fight starts she can't reach it — so she starts **blasting and hammering at the tunnel walls with her shotgun to widen the entrance**, shouting encouragement back down a passage where nobody is standing: *"Don't worry guys, I almost got this open."*

The mimics do not survive the party's first turn. Zelda opens with **Guiding Bolt** (16 radiant) plus a Finger Guns bonus action for 8 force. Flux's new bow lands 5 + 2d6 with **10 feet of knockback and a 10-foot speed reduction**. Valerian casts **Depth Charge** — *"a pulse of thunder"* audible 300 feet away — for 25, killing Mimic A outright and rattling the whole mine. Barrett burns **Adrenaline Rush** to close 60 feet. Samothy shoots twice, the second on a natural 20, and kills Mimic B.

Behind the wall the mimics were nesting in hangs an enormous vampire-made tapestry: ***The Last Fight of Queen Elandra***. Flux recognises the weapon depicted in it — the **[[Blade of Sunfire]]** — and the DM confirms it.

### The tunnel

Deanna is still chipping. The DM asks for another d20, then a Dex save.

She rolls a **natural 1**.

*"All your blasting and hammering away at things trying to widen the tunnel have caused it to collapse on top of you. You try to reach out for the end of the tunnel but are unsuccessful because you slip on the dirt beneath you... and piece by piece a rock falls."*

**No death saves.** Zelda, sprinting down the hall on a Perception check, hears her. [[Mr. Cat]] makes his own Dex save and bolts clear, *"but it cost him one of his 9 lives."*

Flux uses **Mold Earth** to pull the earth back, and they get her out. The DM does not soften it: her bones have been crushed to dust inside her skin, so *"you go to lift her up and it's like—"* she folds. *"It looks like a mosquito that you smashed against your arm."*

Zelda takes two 150gp diamonds, holds them like defibrillator paddles against Deanna's chest, and casts **Revivify**.

Nothing happens. No magic leaves her. She wipes off her hands, wipes off the diamonds, drags Deanna clear of the passage, and tries again.

Nothing happens.

### Why she stays dead

An Arcana check — Flux rolling, with Zelda feeding her what the failed casting *felt* like as an assist d6 — turns up 17, and the answer.

The bypass was **an old burial ground for cursed people**. Bodies thrown in, never interred, ground to dust over centuries as the earth settled. That dust is what came down on Deanna, and what she breathed in as she was crushed. Samothy's Detect Magic sharpens it: it is **not magic, it is curse** — **seven individual curses, one per body, fused into a single one**. Removing it would mean identifying and peeling each layer. *"Curse is like an onion,"* Samothy offers. *"Yep,"* says the DM.

The party works the problem out loud, in the way people do when they can't stop:

- [[Valerian Hellebore]] can cast **Reincarnate** — **1,000gp of rare oils within 10 days**, and she comes back as *"anything from an aasimar, dragonborn, dwarf, elf, gnome, Goliath, halfling, human, orc, or tiefling."* Probably not human.
- Samothy's **Ritual Caster** would cut both the time and the cost, scaling with how many casters help.
- Samothy's **purity filters** end one charm, curse or disease each. 🛑 **The DM explicitly deferred the ruling** — *"I don't know if it's gonna work on her dead body... I'll ask my other DM friend."* **Still unanswered.**
- Zelda notes she had been choosing between preparing Revivify and **Remove Curse**, and has **Greater Restoration**.

Vega, out of character and repeatedly, asks the DM to simply not do this — *"Why are we doing this? Just let her be alive. This is so unnecessary."* Zelda joins her. The DM doesn't budge: *"Not the player I thought was gonna die tonight."*

They fold her into the **bag of holding** and add her to party inventory, after establishing she isn't bleeding — *"she's so flattened it looks like everything was just expelled out."* Flux takes **both daggers**, which the goddess's blessing left undamaged, including the purple psychic-damage one Deanna had claimed. Barrett hands Samothy her mangled shotgun.

⚠️ In-fiction Samothy is Deanna's **brother**, and Josh said plainly and more than once that he could not roleplay it in the moment — *"I cannot roleplay this at the moment, I'm not gonna lie."* Valerian, who lost his own parents a week ago in-fiction, is the one who names it: *"some people don't process grief right away because they know that they can't."* The party agrees Samothy grieves later, because they have **three days** to reach the bottom and get back out.

### The Old Shrine, and the new druid

At the **[[Old Shrine]]**: a painted stone statue of a bearded man, long hair once blonde, the paint chipped and flaking. **Writing runs the whole way around the base** in something that reads as Old English. Valerian can only pick out a couple of words. ⚠️ **Not translated.**

Circling it, he runs into **[[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]]**.

She is a **Circle of the Land druid** whose element is **lightning**, she is blunt and socially awkward and says so unprompted (*"Sometimes I overshare"*), and she has been trapped down here since her own party turned down a hallway and left without her. Flux checks she isn't a mimic (Insight 21). Flux asks to see her teeth. She likes garlic, which she offers as evidence.

🛑 She admits, without much prompting, that her interest in the **[[Watcher's Pit]]** was to *"take a short nap off a long cliff"* — and that the party turning up changed her mind. Flux redirects her to come with them through the grate instead.

Valerian dislikes her on sight. Chase played the reason out loud: Val is **threatened that she'll replace him**, *"he doesn't like the idea of not being needed or wanted. Especially since he has no parents anymore."* Barrett, checking on him, offers the two options he can see — teach her to be better, *"or we can use her as bait."*

The DM confirms something the party did not know: **there are two ways down to the veins** — through the **Watcher's Pit**, or through **the grate**. They take the grate.

### The Broken Descent

The final stretch is *"a jagged sloping corridor scarred by claw marks and soaked in old blood,"* gouged by beast thralls, air humid and thick with wet fur and copper. Growls come from below that **never repeat the same pattern**, *"as if multiple creatures are listening and responding."* Thrall-scratched symbols mark territorial boundaries. At the bottom sits **a massive iron grate that vibrates and pulses with a heartbeat-like glow.**

Between them and it: a **110-foot rope bridge**, boards missing, two people wide and rated for approximately one. Everyone rolls Dex, Acrobatics and a Con save to cross without looking down.

Valerian **Wild Shapes into a bat** and flies it. Flux **Misty Steps**. Barrett crosses on a 9 and a 14 and arrives badly nauseous. Samothy burns **all three of his Lucky points** on a single Acrobatics check, gets an 11, then rolls a **natural 20** on the Con save. Vega slips and stumbles across, and asks to flavour it as carrying Mr. Cat *"reenacting Sam carrying Frodo up Mount Doom."* Zelda rolls a natural 1 on initiative, rerolls it as a halfling, and gets an 11 — *"Z's just meandering. She is still in her feels."*

At the bottom, a years-old fire pit still burning, and waist-deep water with something moving under it. A **water elemental** rises: they are in its lair.

Barrett opens with two shots for 18. Vega activates her **Eldritch Claw tattoo** for 15 feet of reach and attacks recklessly, landing a natural 20 for 27 and a second hit at 21. Samothy uses his passive to read the creature's exact HP, then lands two pistol shots and a **Ballistic Smite**. **Lulu opens her account with the party** by asking who needs healing and casting **Mass Cure Wounds** — 32 hit points to everyone in a 30-foot radius, which puts Vega back to full.

Valerian gets the DM to formally rule that a creature made of water counts as *"submerged in a liquid"* — *"So essentially he's asking you, is water wet?"* — *"Yeah, water is wet, as a matter of fact."* He then drops **Depth Charge at 4th level** for 32 thunder.

Vega then remembers she forgot her tattoo damage **and** her Brutal Strike d10, and the retroactive 15 kills it. She gives the credit away immediately: *"whoever went before me — you killed it, not me."*

The DM stops there deliberately, to build what comes next properly: *"The going down into the veins is gonna be a whole probably 2 sessions by itself."*

### Themes & Emotional Beats

- **Diligence kills.** Deanna died doing the helpful, conscientious thing — widening a tunnel so her friends could follow — while the fight she was trying to reach was already over.
- **Coping by joke.** The table's response to the death was relentless humour, and they named it while doing it: *"you just killed somebody, so we're coping in the only way we know how — humour."* Two players also policed it: *"Jesus, give her a second,"* and *"it is too soon."*
- **Grief deferred, not skipped.** Samothy's player refusing to perform grief on cue became the in-fiction answer: he can't process it here, so he won't.
- **The replacement problem.** Lulu arrives roughly twenty minutes after Deanna dies, and Valerian's hostility is explicitly about being replaceable — a beat the table found before the DM had to reach for it.
- **Arguing with the DM as mourning.** Vega and Zelda spent several minutes asking, out of character, for the death to simply be undone. It isn't rules-lawyering; it reads as bargaining.

### Party Structure & Subgroups

The party was **split for the entire first act**, and that split is what killed Deanna. She was alone in the bypass tunnel with Mr. Cat while the other six fought the mimics in the Lantern Spine; neither group could see or reach the other, and she was still shouting encouragement to a hallway after the fight had ended. They reunited only to dig her out.

From the Old Shrine on, the party moves as one group of seven (six PCs plus Lulu), carrying Deanna's body.

## Archivist Notes

### Patterns, Progress & Future Implications

- **The campaign now has a death precedent, and it's a harsh one.** Instant death with no death saves, from a failed skill check, in a location whose lethal property was undiscoverable in advance. Worth knowing when weighing whether to split the party again.
- **Splitting the party is now demonstrably dangerous here.** Deanna spent ~40 minutes of session time alone and died in it. Nobody could reach her.
- **Two combats, two single rounds.** At level 10 the party is significantly out-scaling mimics and a lone water elemental. The DM stopping to build the veins encounter properly reads as a direct response to that.
