# Session 18 — I Knew You'd Come

> **⭐ [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s S17 vow is paid.** She swore to the Emberborn echo that she would get [[Valerian Hellebore]] back — *"no matter what it takes… to either retrieve his body or free his soul."* **One session later he is out.** He is alive, his body is whole, and his soul never left. ⚠️ **It was not her arms that carried him out.** She spent the entire fight on the ground floor; [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] made the grab.
> 🛑 **[[Valerian Hellebore]] came back changed.** Different face, different voice with an accent, and he throws **Eldritch Blast** — a Warlock cantrip — from a Circle of the Land druid. He attributes it to *"calling on **Arona**."* Vega's **natural 20 Insight** confirms: *"nothing is wrong with Val other than he looks and sounds different, but he is Val."* He does not know anything is different.
> 🛑 **A new deity is named: [[Lunara, the Nightbringer]].** The ritual crystal is *"a replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]]… but it's the darker version,"* dedicated to her. **First dark-side deity in the campaign.** Do not infer a pantheon.

## POV Journal

> *[[Vega Bloodroot]] — Half-Orc Barbarian, Path of the Wild Heart*

I have carried this sword in my hand for a week now because I cannot put it down. It has started talking to me. Sweet nothings, mostly, about how much better things would be if I were evil. I let it talk. A thing that lives in your palm is going to have opinions.

We walked in instead of arriving. That was the choice we made, and I think it was the right one, because the walk is what told us where we were going. The trees out there twist like broken ribs. The sky pulls back from the tower the way a dog pulls back from a raised hand. And there is a green light in the high window that goes slow and steady, on and off, on and off, like something in there has a heartbeat and it is being kept.

There were screams on the wind the whole way in. I did not ask whose. I already knew.

Flux put a blessing over all of us before we were close enough to be seen — light where there had been half-light, and a brightness that took to the edge of every weapon we carried. Mine drank it up. I said something stupid about how good my sword was about to be. I meant it.

Then the walls came apart and they came down at us, pale and stretched and hungry, and a tall man stepped out onto the top of the tower and told us we were too late.

We were not too late. I want that written down first, before any of the rest of it.

I opened with the bow. That is new for me, and I want to be honest about why: because the thing I wanted to hit was eighty feet up a granite wall and I could not reach it with my fists or my anger. Two arrows, two down. Then the ground came to me and I did not need the bow anymore.

What I remember of the middle is this. A thing with boils on it stood next to me most of the night, and every time somebody hurt it, it burst, and I got a mouthful. Three times I went down on one knee with poison in me. Three times I got back up. It is not bravery. I just did not have anywhere else to be.

Samothy went up. He stepped sideways out of the world and came out at the top of the tower, and the cat went with him, and then — I heard this, I did not see it — the cat threw itself onto the face of the man running the ritual and would not let go. Zelda went invisible and climbed, hand over hand on those hooks of hers, and I lost her.

And I stayed on the floor of the courtyard swinging.

That is the part I keep turning over. I made a promise in front of a fire and a dead man, and I meant every word of it, and when the moment came to reach up and take my friend out of the air, I was two hundred feet below with my boots in the dirt, killing the things that were in everybody else's way.

Maybe that is the job. Maybe the promise was never that I would be the hands. Maybe it was that I would make sure the hands got there.

I am still deciding whether that is a comfort or an excuse.

They brought the tower down from the inside. The chains that were holding him let go all at once, and the crystal cracked open, and the light came out of it and went everywhere, and he fell.

He is different. I saw it before anybody said it. His face is not the face I have been looking at for a year, and his voice has a shape in it that was never there before, and the first thing he did with his hands was throw something out of them that a man like him should not be able to throw. So I looked at him. Properly. The way you look at a track to see whether the animal was running or walking.

He is Val. Whatever else came back with him, he is Val, all the way through, and he does not know a single thing is wrong.

I asked him if he had always had an accent. He looked at me like I had lost my mind.

Then we had to tell him about Deanna, who is folded up in a bag like laundry, and I said the true short version — she got crushed under a lot of rock — and somebody else filled in the part about her bones going to ash inside her, and he got very quiet.

We have been carrying her for weeks and none of us have stopped once to say she's gone.

Three weeks, he said. That's how long he was hanging there. He said it like it was a small thing, like he was correcting a date, and then he asked where we got the flower crowns.

I still have mine. I said we got them off the thing that was wearing his face.

I do not know what to do with a man who has been screaming in a tower for three weeks and comes down asking about flowers. But I know the promise is kept, and I know he is breathing, and tonight that is enough for me to set the sword down.

I cannot set the sword down. You know what I mean.

## Summary

### The march in

The party had teleported back to the home-base castle and — at [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s prompting — camped in the Tiny Hut rather than pressing straight on. The DM opens with the approach:

> *"the journey to the tower is a march through the dead— the dead zone. Uh, the trees twist like broken ribs. The sky dims unnaturally, as if recoiling from the structure ahead, as the tower rises from the cliffside like a rotten tooth. Its stones blackened by centuries of dark magic. A sickly green glow pulses from the highest window, slow, steady, like a heartbeat. The wind carries faint screams."*

Those screams are confirmed as [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s at 00:20:18: *"And those screams you're hearing are Val."*

[[Flux]] casts **Rite of the Dawning Radiance** on the whole party on the approach — resistance to necrotic; advantage on saves against charm, fear and Vampiric Domination; **+1d6 radiant on every weapon attack**; dim light becomes bright within 30 ft and darkness becomes dim within 15 ft; duration one hour. **It is load-bearing for the entire session's damage math**, and the DM rules radiant is **double damage** against these enemies.

[[Vega Bloodroot]]'s reaction: *"Shit, this sword is gonna be so OP."*

### The ambush

> *"The growls begin. Roll tokens. Shadows burst from the ruined battlements. Feral shapes with pale skin stretched tight over their twisted muscles. Beast thralls there, uh, stand before you with animal hunger. Behind them, a towering figure steps into view from the top of the tower. His voice slithers across the stones. **You're too late. His power is already flowing into the circle.** The Beast Master raises his hand and the thralls surge forward in a frenzy."*

🛑 **That is the only use of "the Beast Master" in the session.** From 00:20:38 onward the antagonist is *"the necromancer"* without exception, roughly thirty times, including in both later boxed-text reads. **[[The Beast Master]] died in S10.** See *Continuity Flags*.

Initiative is called at 00:09:57 — *"Everybody except for Val."*

### The tower

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s **History 22** establishes the ground truth:

- **Two levels** — the main floor and the top of the tower.
- A rumour that it is *"supposedly haunted Or controlled by a necromancer, but everyone's too scared to come out here to test that theory."*

The DM's lore, given flat: *"This tower was once a lighthouse that got repurposed into a, uh, fort outpost tower for defense during war. And that's all the lore on the tower."* **Granite** (*"You act like I don't know"*). A big winding staircase all the way up. ⚠️ Height stated as **110 feet** and then used as **120 feet** for the rest of the session without correction — see *Continuity Flags*.

The old door on the party's side is **sealed with solid rock and magically enchanted**. The DM's ruling: *"I would let you try to break through if you rolled **2 nat 20s in a row.**"* The table tries anyway. [[Barrett Grimmskar]]: *"And if my grandmother was born with wheels, she'd be a bike."*

### They levelled up too

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] opens with **Guiding Bolt** for a 15 to hit, and gets the session's thesis statement back:

> *"No, 15 does not hit. Not this time anymore, guys. They leveled up with you guys, so their AC is no longer 15."* … *"You should always beware of level-ups. They come with consequences."*

The board: **Vampire Beast Thrall A and B**, a **[[Boilborn]]**, three on the wall, three in the courtyard, **Beast Thrall C** 42 feet up the tower face, and **feral vampire thralls D, E and F**. ⚑ The DM uses at least four different labels for these across the session — see *Continuity Flags*.

### Samothy goes up the wall, twice

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] spends the session solving the tower as a physics problem. Round one: a **natural 20 Athletics (26)** to fire his **Hookshot Arm Bracers** at an off-angle, swing around the tower, **tackle a thrall off the wall**, shove it away mid-fall for **28 fall damage**, and **teleport out of the drop with his [[Boots of the Winding Path]]** onto a stump — with [[Mr. Cat]] retroactively on his shoulder for all of it. The DM: *"I'm not even gonna roll a DC for you, so yes, you got it."*

Round two he skips the wall entirely — **Vortex Warp at 2nd level**, 80 feet straight to the top. Mr. Cat's passage is settled on a d6 (1–3 yes): he rolls a **3**.

### Val in the circle

> *"you burst into the top of the chamber and the world seems to stop. Val is hanging suspended in a glowing magic circle, chains of shimmering energy wrapped around his arms, his chest, his throat. **Each chain pulses as it siphons away his magic, his immortality, and his very essence.** His skin flickers, mortal one moment, immortal the next, as if it really can't decide what he is anymore. The necromancer is standing over him, robes swirling with dark power, chanting words that scrape against your ears like Broken glass. A massive ritual crystal floats behind him, glowing brighter with every drop of stolen magic."*

The necromancer turns, *"a smile carving across his face"*:

> *"Ah, the rescuers. How touching. **But his power will be mine, and when I'm finished, the vampire lords will kneel before a new master.**"*

⚑ That line is the only statement of his motive on the record, and it puts him **against** the vampire hierarchy, not inside it — which matches the earlier aside that thrall movements are *"not exactly a sanctioned thing by all the vampires."*

**Val is never given a real turn.** The DM excludes him from initiative; the table objects — *"I feel like Val should get to Trying to fight his way out a little bit. He shouldn't get skipped"* — and the compromise is: *"Val can scream in pain on his turns."* Across the fight he takes **five forced Constitution saves and roughly 91 damage** while hanging there, and burns his Heroic Inspiration on one of them.

### Mr. Cat wins the fight

The single highest-leverage action of the session belongs to a cat. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asks whether Mr. Cat can *"jump on this guy's face… like a feral cat when you find like a street cat and you try to pick it up and it just like attacks you."* Acrobatics with advantage — *"I think Mr. Cat should get advantage on acrobatics as well for being a cat"* — **27**.

> *"So Mr. Cat leaps on the necromancer's face."*

That imposes **disadvantage for the rest of the fight**, and it is what makes the next thing land.

### The crystal, thrown

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asks what the ritual crystal weighs. **20 to 25 pounds.** He then burns his **only 4th-level slot on Catapult**, scaled exactly to 20 pounds, and hurls the necromancer's own ritual focus into him.

He first tries to argue a person counts as a surface. The DM rules no — *"we would say a human is not a surface. They are a living being"* — which, as Samothy immediately works out, is **better**, because a struck creature gets a save instead of an automatic stop. Dex save at **disadvantage** thanks to the cat: **15 against a DC of 17**.

**6d8 → 36 bludgeoning, and the necromancer is knocked prone.** A d20 of 10 confirms *"Mr. Cat is safe this time."*

Samothy follows with a shot for 10 and a bonus-action **Branding Smite**.

### Zelda breaks the ritual

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] runs the whole vertical half of the rescue. She casts **Invisibility** on herself and rappels ~53 feet up the tower on her bracers, then hauls herself over the edge and casts **Dispel Magic** on the contraption.

> *"as you cast Dispel Magic, the chains around Val tighten down even more. The circle on the ground starts to sputter."*

The DM's ruling on the state it leaves: *"It is magic-based, but it's got a wind-down… it's done, but it's flickering because it's still got energy being transferred through it"* — *"like a blinker would, flashing on and off as it's losing power."*

She checks the timing (*"How, how much time is between"* — *"Just a couple seconds"*), then commits:

> *"I'm gonna get to the wall and I'm gonna run, jump, grab him, and as the magic kind of flickers out, then the moment it does, I'm gonna just Dimension Door us out."*

**Acrobatics 21.** It works — at a price. *"as you grab hold of him and get to the edge of the circle jumping through with him, the chains crush down on top of him and he takes 25 damage."* Then: *"you Dimension Door, pop, pop, there you are."*

### The Boilborn's turn

At 01:57:11 the DM says *"it's [[Boilborn]]'s turn, and he's got something I've been wanting to try"* — and detonates **Rupture Burst** on [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]], [[Flux]] and [[Vega Bloodroot]] at once. **DC 16 Constitution, 34 damage.** Vega saves on a 17 and takes half; Flux burns Heroic Inspiration and takes the full 34 anyway; Lulu takes 34.

The DM's description, given after the fact when Flux asks what hit them:

> *"just imagine all of a sudden all these boils just started swelling on his skin and, and then they burst open and poison fluid shot everywhere."*

[[Flux]] kills it minutes later with a bow shot plus **Sneak Attack (18)** and a Finger Gun.

### The kill, and the end of the prepared text

With the necromancer at **1 hit point**, the DM calls it:

> *"the necromancer's body hits the floor. The ritual collapses instantly. The runes flicker out. The crystal cracks, splitting light, spilling light across the chamber. The chains shatter, dissolving into drifting sparks. Uh, Val is not on his knees, is in fact probably laying on his back. Gasping and trembling, barely conscious. You rush to him as he struggles to lift his head. He says, **I knew you'd come. Uh, thank you.** His body's weak, his magic is unstable, but he is alive. And somewhere in the distance, far across the land, **something answers the dying pulse of the ritual. A deep resonant tone, a warning, a promise.** The necromancer may be dead, but the power he awakened is That's the end of it, of what I have written."*

⚑ The final clause is **unrecoverable** — the DM runs out of written text mid-sentence. **Whatever the ritual awakened is left explicitly open.**

The surviving beast thralls simply leave: *"Oh, the necromancer's dead. They all ran away. **Their contract is over.**"* [[Valerian Hellebore]] — still on his back, minutes out of the circle — gets the last word of the combat: ***"Before they leave, Eldritch Blast!"*** Three shots, three hits (22, 23, 24), for 9, 14 and 6.

Combat ends at **02:18:36**.

### Val comes back wrong, and it is fine

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] gets to him first — a hug and **Cure Wounds for 26** — and the reunion immediately derails:

> *"You, you sound different."* — Zelda
> *"Have you always had an accent?"* — [[Vega Bloodroot]]
> *"What are you talking about?"* — Val
> *"It's been 3 weeks, darling."*

He can throw **Eldritch Blast**, which a Circle of the Land druid should not have, and he explains it as *"I can call on **Arona**"* — then, pressed on why his magic is different: *"My magic's no different."*

[[Vega Bloodroot]] rolls **Insight — natural 20**:

> *"nothing is wrong with Val other than he looks and sounds different, but he is Val."*

The DM adds that *"he looks like he's genuinely confused, and he's telling the truth, as far as he knows."* ⚠️ **He is not lying and he is not replaced. He also does not know anything happened to him.**

He also asks after the **flower crowns** the party is still wearing — left by his own dissipating magic when [[The Doppelganger]] died in S17. Zelda takes hers off and puts it on his head.

### Deanna, and the offer

The reunion turns hard when Val learns where [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] is:

> *"I'm sorry, you put her body where?"* … *"her body's been in the bag of holding for at least a week."*

The party's case is preservation — *"she's preserved inside the dimensional pocket. There's no decay in there"*, *"It's like Tupperware"* — against Val's *"We don't know that for a fact."* The details that surface: she was **crushed under rock**, she *"breathed in a bunch of cursed"* something, and **her bones turned to ash inside her body**, so she *"kind of got nicely folded up and tucked away"* — *"like a flat sheet, not a fitted sheet."*

Val lands the line the whole arc has been avoiding:

> *"I don't think we've had a moment to properly take in the fact that she died, really, honestly. I don't think we've even really grieved over it."*

[[Flux]] notes she already offered [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] a small funeral before they left, and *"he wasn't ready."*

Then [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] takes Samothy aside and makes the offer:

> *"I have a spell I can do as long as the humanoid has been deceased no longer than 10 days, I can put her soul into a new body. I can reincarnate her."*

A **whole new body** — *"she won't look like Deanna, but her soul will be Deanna"* — with the race rolled on a d10, DM's discretion. Samothy does not answer: *"I don't know if she would want that or not."* Lulu tells him to talk to the others, pats his shoulder and walks off.

🛑 **The ten-day window may already have expired in-world**, and nobody checked. See *Continuity Flags*. The table explicitly defers the whole decision: *"Did y'all want to roleplay discussing what to do about Deanna… tonight or next session?"* — *"Not tonight."*

### The loot, and a new goddess

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] loots the necromancer while the reunion happens below (*"Samothy's still at the top of the tower looting the necromancer at this moment"*). Skulls, incense, jewels, **obsidian rings and silver worth 250 gold together**, the **[[Tattered Robes of the Pale Scholar]]** and the **[[Soulbound Grimoire]]**.

The Grimoire is *"one of the items you were looking for"* — one of **two** commissions the party is filling. The other name is **Bobby**; the second is cut off mid-sentence. **The DM has not assigned a holder:** *"I just have to see who it goes to."* It carries a **corruption** mechanic — *"There's a d6 involved. And there's 3 tiers or 3 checks for it"* — whose effect is *"it turns you into undead,"* and which is **not permanent**: *"there's a ritual you can do to unturn somebody."*

Samothy then asks whether the shattered ritual crystal's shards still hold magic. **Arcana 30.** The DM grants **8 enchantable shards** — and, pulling up his note, names their source:

> *"this crystal is a replica of— oh yeah, it's a replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]], right? But it's the darker version… This crystal is dedicated to **Lunara**, Lunara, who's referred to as **the Nightbringer**."* … *"it's necrotic necromancy that it can be used for."*

🛑 **What the shards actually do is explicitly deferred:** *"I didn't think anybody would ask about getting shards, so I was Not prepared. I'll get to that too, probably tomorrow."* **Open item into S19.**

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] closes the session by teleporting the party — and the horse — back to the castle.

### Encounter Summary

**The real cost was paid by the character who never got a turn.** [[Valerian Hellebore]] took five forced Constitution saves and roughly **91 damage** while suspended, including **25 as the chains crushed down on him during his own rescue** — more damage than any PC took in the fight.

Losing would have meant the necromancer completing a ritual that was siphoning *"his magic, his immortality, and his very essence"*, with the stated aim of making *"the vampire lords kneel before a new master."* Winning collapsed it — and woke something.
