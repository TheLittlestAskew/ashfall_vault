## POV Journal

> Vega Bloodroot — for [[Vega Bloodroot Journal]]. ⚑ No Vega voice guide exists yet (the `⟦FILL: Vega voice guide⟧` token is still open in both instruction files); this entry is written in the grounded register established by the S01–S03 entries.

The doors shut behind us and the quiet outside was worse than the quiet inside had been.

I know the difference now between a place that is empty and a place that is holding its breath. The road out was the second kind. Broken branches at chest height — not mine, I'm taller than that, but chest height for something that walks. Prints in the mud, still soft. Four sets. The changeling read them and said they'd come through recently. Nobody said the obvious thing, which is that recently means *still here*.

Then a voice got inside my head and said **run, I want to see how far you get.**

I have been afraid of things. This wasn't that. This was the particular insult of being spoken to like an animal that's about to be released for sport. I said something stupid back, because that's what I do when I want my hands to stop shaking, and then the shapes came out of the rubble — too many teeth, eyes lit up wrong — and they didn't come at us like beasts. They came at us like a plan. Circling. Flanking. Pushing us where they wanted us.

Six of them. And whoever was driving them never showed his face, not once, the whole time.

So I made it simple. The healer offered me a ward and I took it and went to work.

The first one I took with the axe, and I have to write down what that felt like because I don't think it will happen the same way twice. Something in me went quiet. Not the anger — the *noise around* the anger. Left to right, right to left, and then the axe up over my head and straight down through the middle of him. Clean. It was the closest thing to dancing I have ever done. Somebody shouted at me to save some for the rest of them.

Here is the part I keep turning over. Halfway through, I stopped wanting the axe.

I put it away. I walked over to the next one and I hit him with my hands. Left, right. The knuckles the tinkerer built into my gauntlets, with that shard of the sun sitting in them — every punch landing with a little of the light we crossed a dead city to find. I gave in to it fully. I'm a bear, and a bear does not need a blade, and for a while there I wasn't thinking in words at all. When the last one turned on me I roared at him first. I wanted him to know what was coming. He hit me anyway.

Three of them went down under me by the end. Two of those weren't mine to take. I stole one clean off the little bard after she'd spent the whole night wearing him down — I only meant to throw one more punch, and then he was dead, and I said so out loud because she deserved to hear it from me instead of figuring it out. The druid said he'd have stolen it if I hadn't. That's kind, and it isn't the same as it not happening.

Then it was over, and *he* finally came out. Cloak, dark leathers, and the pleasure of a man watching something he owns perform well. Another thrall walked out of the dark beside him, bigger than the six we'd just put down. He didn't fight. He said we'd led him on a delightful chase and that he couldn't wait until next time, and the fog took him.

He was never hunting us. He was *timing* us.

We walked out of the dead zone and made the castle. There's coin now — enough that I could put it toward something with weight to it. I want a warhammer, two-fisted, and I've told the smith so. And when there's an hour that belongs to me, I'm going to spend it with the metal cat. He keeps stepping in front of people. Somebody should sit with him for a while and let him be something other than a shield.

Next time, he says.

Good. Let him come where I can reach him.

## Summary

**A walk that was never going to be a walk.** The session opens the instant [[Session 10 — Never Truly Alone|S10]] ended: the party steps out of [[The Grand Library of the Veiled Dawn]] into the Dead Zone, bound for [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]]. The DM's cold open is pure dread — *"the wind carries a faint metallic tang, like old blood left to dry… Something has passed through here recently, something low to the ground and fast."* A party-wide Perception check follows. [[Valerian Hellebore]] and [[Barrett Grimmskar]] notice nothing; everyone else sees **broken branches at chest height** (well above [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]'s head) and **prints in the mud running the way the party has to go**. [[Flux]] rolls a natural 20 for 30 and pins it down: **four sets of tracks, laid recently.**

**The voice.** After a stealth sequence past a vampire patrol — one pauses, sniffs, twitches toward them, and moves on — the ambush lands. Twisted shapes burst from the rubble, *"beasts with too many teeth and eyes that glow with unnatural hunger,"* and the DM is explicit that this is not animal behaviour: **"They don't attack like animals. They are moving with a purpose, circling, flanking, driving you into position."** Then a voice brushes the edge of every mind at the table: ***"run, I want to see how far you get."*** Nobody runs. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]: "Who's running? I took on 2 of these guys by myself last time." The voice returns, *"amused and hungry"* — ***"Oh, feeling brave, are you?"*** — and initiative is called ten minutes into the session.

**One encounter, six thralls, eighty-seven percent of the night.** Initiative runs from 00:10:05 to 02:05:50 without a break. Six **[[Beast Thrall]]s**, designated only by letter — **A through F** — with no table-christened joke names this session. The order the DM calls is Zelda → Flux → Barrett → A → Samothy → B → Vega → C → D → E → F → Valerian → Deanna.

**Samothy opens with the session's best stunt.** He asks whether he can trade an action for a second bonus action; the DM says **"Sure."** He then chains the **grappling hook bracers** and his **new boot teleport infusion** into a single sequence: sling toward Thrall B on the castle wall so it thinks he's coming straight in, **teleport behind it**, grapple it, and drop off the wall with it. Athletics 10, Acrobatics 12, Strength 10, then a Dex save of 9 rerolled with a **lucky point** to 18. He lands on top, both prone, and the thrall breaks his fall — *"He cushioned my fall."* / *"Pretty comfy. They're like dogs."* Thrall B then spends **three separate turns** failing to escape the grapple, including a **natural 1**.

**Vega's fight is the spine of the session.** [[Vega Bloodroot]] rages, takes [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s **Protection from Evil and Good** as a hand-slap on the run-past, and opens on Thrall D with the greataxe for 30. In round two she puts together the night's biggest turn: a 20 to hit (*"Dirty. Not natural"*) rerolled with **Savage Attacker** to 16, then **two consecutive natural 20s** — 28 on the crit and 18 on the **Great Weapon Master** bonus attack. **62 damage in one turn; Thrall D dies.** The DM asks her to describe it and she gives the session's signature image: Vega has hit **flow state**, *"which is hilarious because this is really only like the second round"* — she reads it as being outdoors, back in her element, and familiar with these things by now. *"Like translating ballet to fighting… 1 slash, 2 slash, 3 slash,"* the last one bringing the axe over her head and straight down, *"splitting him open a little bit."* [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]: **"damn girl, save some aura for the rest of us."**

**Then she puts the axe away.** Two rounds later, mid-fight, Vega **stows the greataxe and finishes the session bare-knuckle** — *"She doesn't even want to use the axe anymore. She puts it up and she walks over to Beast Thrall F and just starts punching it with her brass knuckles. Left, right."* She names it herself: *"She's given in fully to the beast… she's a bear, so she's just fucking handing it to him. With her fists."* She also asks the DM for a homebrew flourish — left punch, right punch, **bonus-action kick to the chest** — and gets **"Yeah, we could do that. That sounds fun."** And when Thrall F turns on her she tries to **roar him off** with an Intimidation check before he can swing (8 — fails), then eats 15.

**The Sun Shard resurfaces — mechanically, after a full session's absence.** Reminded by [[Flux]] and [[Barrett Grimmskar]] that her knuckles do something, Vega hunts through her DMs for the ruling. The DM: *"purple psychic. Purple is psychic."* [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]: *"I built it into her gauntlets."* [[Valerian Hellebore]] guesses **"Sunny Crystal"** and the DM corrects him on the record — **"Sun Shard"** — with Samothy echoing it. The rider is confirmed as **an extra d6 of radiant damage**. This is the first appearance of the campaign's central relic since [[Session 07 — The Sun Shard|S07]]'s recovery, and it lands as a damage line item rather than a story beat.

**Valerian deletes two thralls with Moonbeam.** [[Valerian Hellebore]] drops **Moonbeam at 5th level** *"because I heard these things that, uh, have like 80 fucking health or some shit,"* announces he was one turn away from polymorphing into a T-Rex instead, and starts cooking Thrall C: **31 radiant doubled to 62**, then **34 doubled to 68** the following round. *"Radiant beams from my holy face, motherfucker."* / *"Fry, motherfucker! Put the fries in the bag, bitch!"* **Thrall C dies.** Radiant damage being doubled against these creatures is the fight's decisive mechanic and is applied consistently by the DM all night.

**New thrall statblock detail, delivered live.** When Thrall C drops below 50 HP the DM says *"it's cool because I've been wanting to try this out"* and triggers a **frenzy**: this is a **"blood trait" that they have. It's basically like a Barbarian's Rage… it gives advantage on hits."* He also rules that beast thralls **deal double damage to structures, doors, and other fortifications**. Later, when [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] lights the field with Flame Blade, he reveals the night's other creature detail: **"you do see that these ones have eyes. The normal ones did not."**

**Barrett's crit range has moved.** [[Barrett Grimmskar]] lands a to-hit of 27 off a **dice roll of 18** and calls it: *"which for me is a— consider it a crit now."* He credits **Piercer** for rolling an additional damage die on crits — *"so instead of 2d8, it is 3d8"* — for 27 total, and applies his **Gut Shot** rider in full: *"The projectile is lodged in the target for 1 minute. Speed is halved, have disadvantage on attack rolls unless they take a full action to dislodge the bullet."* ⚑ The glossary records that rider as a **19**-crit; this is an 18.

**The clean-up.** [[Barrett Grimmskar]] kills **Thrall E** with a Shocking Grasp into a Finger Guns. **Mr. Cat finishes Thrall B** — [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]: *"Oh nice, Cat finishes him off."* Vega kills **Thrall F** with her fists, then runs across the field to [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]], throws one more punch at **Thrall A**, and kills it too — **stealing the kill from Zelda**, who had put roughly **90 points of damage** into that creature over the whole fight. Vega owns it immediately: *"God damn it, I didn't mean to steal a kill from somebody else. I just wanted to throw another punch."* [[Valerian Hellebore]], whose Moonbeam was sitting on the same square: *"That's fine, I was gonna steal it if you didn't."*

**The villain finally steps out — and leaves.** With every thrall dead, the DM narrates: *"from the shadows, a figure steps forward, cloak and dark leathers, eyes gleaming with predatory delight. He rests a hand on his head in a — And a massive thrall appears by his side."* ⚑ *(mid-sentence cut; see Archivist Notes.)* He speaks **into their minds**: ***"you led me on a delightful chase."*** ***"I can't wait till next time."*** Then he and the new, larger thrall **vanish into the fog**. He never entered combat, never named himself, and took no damage. [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s reply to a telepath: *"Sir, you can't do this without consent."*

**Aftermath: money, gems, and a body.** The party clears the Dead Zone and reaches the castle for a **long rest**. **200 gp** found last expedition splits six ways (33.33 each) and the **900 gp party fund** splits seven (128 gp 57 sp), for **161 gp 90 sp per PC** ⚑ *(two different party sizes in the same conversation — see Archivist Notes)*. The DM then drops a new economic rule: **"gems are a currency in this place,"** tradeable by condition — raw versus cut. Party gem inventory is recapped as **3 diamonds, 6 emeralds, 5 rubies**, plus the jade ball from the great-cat skull and the entire restricted section's books and scrolls. The DM, ruefully: *"I didn't plan on you taking all of them, so now I have to come up with stuff for the books."*

**Two downtime projects open.** [[Flux]] confirms the plan is **transformation, not disguise** — *"it's not disguising, it's— I would turn into one."* The DM sets terms: dissect and learn the anatomy, *"especially this one since this one has eyes and the normal ones do not,"* the party can **take a body back to the doctor**, it works *"similar to"* Druid Wild Shape, and **"you keep your normal stats."** [[Valerian Hellebore]] volunteers to help. Meanwhile [[Vega Bloodroot]] commissions a **double fist warhammer** from [[Bobby]], and declares her downtime is to *"cultivate her relationship with [[Mr. Cat|Mr. Cat]]"* — clarified as *"She just wants to pet him a lot."* Shop inventory goes up "probably tomorrow."

### Encounter Summary

A **decisive, one-sided victory** in a fight the party never chose. Six beast thralls — creatures the DM built with ~86 HP each, a below-50-HP **frenzy** trait, and double damage against fortifications — were killed to the last without a single PC dropping, without a death save, and with only two Cure Wounds and two self-cast Healing Words spent on the party side.

The win rests on three things. **Radiant damage doubles against these creatures**, and the party has three reliable sources of it ([[Valerian Hellebore]]'s Moonbeam, [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s Branding Smite, [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s Sun Shard rider) — Valerian alone did **158 radiant** across three Moonbeam ticks. **Control beat damage on the flanks**: Samothy took Thrall B out of the fight entirely for three rounds with a grapple and a hook-pin, and it contributed almost nothing before Mr. Cat put it down. And **[[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s wards** meant the two most-attacked PCs spent most of the fight being attacked at disadvantage.

The costs were negligible and the information gain was real: the party learned the thralls have a **frenzy trait**, that this batch has **eyes** where the standard variant does not, and that **something intelligent is deliberately hunting them for sport**. What they did not get is a name, a face, or a single hit on the actual antagonist — who watched the entire fight, lost six assets, and left in a good mood.
