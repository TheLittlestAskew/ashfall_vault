> 🛑 **This is where [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] died** — not in the corridor itself, but in the bypass tunnel that runs alongside it.

## Description

A long corridor lined on both sides with lanterns burning **cold blue flame** — some lit, some dark.

> "It's a long corridor illuminated by lanterns burning with cold blue flame. The light bends shadows unnaturally, stretching them into shapes that don't match the party's."

Marrowin's warning, given a session earlier:

> "Blue flame, cold flame. Don't trust it. **It burns without heat, but it hungers.**"

## Known Features

- 👤 **The shadows detach.** Anyone who passes the Perception check watches their own shadow **come away from them, lag behind, and snap back into place.** [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s in-character read: *"Am I going crazy? Did I just blink? Because it looked like my shadow blinked."*
- 🔵 **The lanterns move their own fire.** Touching a lit lantern **extinguishes it instantly, and the flame reappears further down the corridor on a lantern that was dark.** Net light in the corridor appears constant.
- ✅ **Detect Magic reads the lanterns as illumination and nothing else.** DM: *"Their sole purpose must be light."* **The walls are not magical at all** — which is what made the breathing wall so alarming.
- 🫁 **The breathing wall (S14).** One section **inhaled and exhaled the cold flame**, pulling the nearest lantern's fire toward it and blowing it back. Found by [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] on a **natural 20 Insight (26)**. [[Valerian Hellebore]] put his face to it and felt **genuine breath** — a cool gust, no smell beyond old tunnel.
  It was a **mimic nest**. Behind it hung a tapestry (below).
- 🖼️ **The tapestry — *The Last Fight of Queen Elandra*.** Enormous, vampire-made, extremely detailed. [[Flux]] identified the weapon depicted in it as the **Blade of Sunfire**, and the DM confirmed. ⚑ **The queen's name is uncertain** — the DM said "Elandra" then immediately "Alandra" and did not settle it. **Not taken.**
- 🕳️ **The bypass tunnel.** Opened from the [[Ossuary Approach]] end in S13 and running parallel to the Spine. Narrow enough to require an Athletics check to move through, and **tightening the deeper it goes**. Mr. Cat was small enough to walk it without rolling.
  🛑 **It is an old burial ground for cursed people** — bodies thrown in, never interred, ground to dust over centuries as the earth settled. That dust carries **seven individual curses fused into one**. See [[Session 14 — An Angel with a Shotgun]].

## Events

- **S13 — approached, not entered.** The party opened the bypass and fought two Bonewraiths in the [[Ossuary Approach]]; the session ended with the Lantern Spine ahead of them.
- **S14 — traversed, at a cost.** The party entered the corridor while [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] took the bypass alone with [[Mr. Cat]], intending to widen it so the others could follow.
  In the corridor: shadows detaching, the lantern trick discovered, [[Vega Bloodroot]] raging and touching the breathing wall (nothing happened) then faking electrocution (everyone screamed except Zelda and Samothy). [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] threw a rock at it and **two mimics** came out. They died inside a single round.
  In the bypass, at the same time, Deanna kept **firing her shotgun at the walls to widen the passage**, shouting encouragement down a corridor where nobody was standing. She failed a Dex save on a **natural 1** and **the tunnel came down and killed her instantly** — no death saves. [[Mr. Cat]] made his save and escaped, *"but it cost him one of his 9 lives."*
  **Revivify failed twice**, because of the cursed dust. [[Flux]] used **Mold Earth** to recover the body; the party carried her out in the **bag of holding**.
  The tunnel is now **collapsed and sealed**.

## Open Threads

- ⚑ **What are the shadows doing?** Detaching and lagging is described in detail and never explained or acted on. It affected nothing mechanically.
- ⚑ **What is the cold flame *for*, and what does it "hunger" for?** Marrowin's warning was specific and the party never tested it.
- ⚑ **The tapestry is still hanging there.** Vampire-made, enormous, depicts **Queen Elandra/Alandra** wielding the **Blade of Sunfire** — a relic the party is nominally hunting. Nobody took it, sketched it, or asked about it further.
- 🛑 **Queen Elandra or Alandra?** The DM said both in one breath. See [[DM Questions — Open]].
- 🛑 **The seven curses in the bypass dust are unidentified**, and identifying them is the precondition for raising [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]].
- ⚑ **Why is a vampire-built corridor lit at all?** Vampires see in magical darkness; the lanterns' *"sole purpose must be light."* For whose benefit?
