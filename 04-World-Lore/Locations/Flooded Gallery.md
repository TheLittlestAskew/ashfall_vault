## What is actually known

One line, from the man who drew the map:

> "Blood run off from the upper floors. Don't fall in. **Things float in there that shouldn't.**"

That is the entire record. Three separate claims inside it, none followed up:

- 🩸 **It is fed by blood running down from the levels above.** ⚑ Which levels, and from what, is unstated — but the party has since walked several of those levels and seen no source.
- ⚠️ **Falling in is called out as the specific hazard**, ahead of anything living in it.
- 🛑 **"Things float in there that shouldn't."** Marrowin's phrasing throughout S13 is precise, and he uses "shouldn't" rather than "don't" — which reads as a statement about the things, not about the water.

## Why it was skipped

The party took the route that suited them rather than the route as drawn. After the [[Lantern Spine]] they reached the [[Old Shrine]], and from there the DM offered **two ways down to the veins** — the [[Watcher's Pit]] or the [[Grate of the Veins]]. They took the grate. **The Flooded Gallery never came up again in S14 or S15.**

⚠️ **Two of Marrowin's six mapped stages have now been bypassed** — this and the [[Watcher's Pit]].
