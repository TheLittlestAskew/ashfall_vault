# Old Shrine

> Named by [[Marrowin Quill]] in [[Session 13 — The Cartographer of the Underkeep]]; reached in [[Session 14 — An Angel with a Shotgun]] and explored properly in [[Session 15 — Only Those Who Bleed Fire]].
> ⭐ **The most lore-dense location the campaign has produced** — and the party looted it and left without answering any of it.

## Description

The visible part is a **statue ring**. The statue: a man with a **big bushy beard** and long hair *"which you assume is blonde since the paint has faded, but you think you see some yellow"* — carved stone that was once **painted**, now chipped and flaking.

🛑 **Writing runs the entire way around the base of the statue.** [[Valerian Hellebore]] could make out *"a couple of what you assume are Old English words, but they seem— they're a little foreign to you."* **It has never been translated.** (Val's own aside: *"I don't speak English. I speak Common."*)

## Known Features

### The hidden chamber (S15)

Found because [[Flux]] noticed on **passive Perception** that one wall *"looks off."* It dips down into a circular chamber, and it is the single richest room in the vault's records:

- 🔥 **Every surface — floor, ceiling and pillars — is covered in hieroglyphics** that glow with a soft orange light. ✅ **Identify confirms they are NOT magical**; they are *"reacting to the heat of the chamber."*
- 🔨 A low **rhythmic hum**, *"almost like a distant forge."*
- 📜 **The hieroglyphics identify an `Emberborn`** — *a pre-vampire hero*. (Read by Flux, Lulu and Samothy.)
- 🛑 **The wards are `protective wards meant to keep something IN, not out`** (Arcana DC 14). ⚠️ **Never resolved what.** Note the [[Boilborn]] were waiting one room away.
- ⛪ **Religion 15+: this is the burial site of a sacred old fire deity.** Unnamed.
- 🗿 **The altar**, holding an **obsidian tablet** over a **hidden compartment** (Investigation DC 16) containing a **charred map fragment** — sewer or tunnel lines the party *almost* recognises: *"you think you know the area, but it's just out of reach."*
- 🩸 **Blood on the sigils does nothing.** [[Valerian Hellebore]] proposed testing them with [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]]'s blood; she cut her own palm without arguing. Nothing happened.

### The relic chamber (S15)

Beyond the shrine, down the **red-lit** of two paths:

- ⚔️ **A sword embedded in a stone pedestal**, glowing ember-orange. ⚠️ **The red light that fills the corridor comes from the PEDESTAL, not the blade.**
- 🪧 The warning: ***"Only those who bleed fire may walk the path of Emberborn."*** — the S15 session title.
- 👹 **Four [[Boilborn]]** guarding it.
- 🛑 **When the sword came free, the pedestal's heartbeat light went out** and the chamber shook.

### The mine (S15)

The **torch-lit** path — the road not taken until after the fight — leads to an **old mineshaft** full of **raw gem veins**. The DM was unusually direct about it: *"They are all here for you guys to take. That's why I brought you here."* Mining is **3 Strength checks at DC 12 per vein**; Mold Earth on the surrounding earth grants advantage.

## Events

- **S14 — the meeting.** [[Valerian Hellebore]], circling the statue, ran into **[[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Ilunid "Lulu" Denulie]]**, a druid abandoned down here by her own party and unable to find the way out. She was cleared of being a mimic (Insight 21) and of being a vampire (she showed her arms and teeth, and volunteered that she likes garlic). The party brought her along and chose **the grate over the [[Watcher's Pit]]**.
- **S15 — the shrine stripped.** The hidden chamber was found and read; **[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] took the obsidian tablet** and the charred map fragment was recovered. The party debated splitting up and refused — *"last time Deanna got separated from the group, she died"* — took the red path, killed four [[Boilborn]] over ~13 rounds, and **[[Vega Bloodroot]] pulled the sword** after three Strength saves with her palm split open and her hand locked to the hilt. They then mined the torch-lit path for **20 raw gems** and [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] teleported everyone back to base.

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **WHAT WERE THE WARDS KEEPING IN?** The single biggest unanswered question here. Built by a pre-vampire culture, still standing, and the party looted the room and left.
- 🛑 **The Old English inscription around the statue base is untranslated.** Nobody in the party reads it. **This is a solvable thread that requires only an NPC or a language.**
- 🛑 **The obsidian tablet's contents were never read aloud.** The DM said twice he would post them to Discord. ⚑ **Check the loot tab.**
- 🛑 **The DM confirmed the tablet is covered in a *different book*** than the one only [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] can read. **A second book exists and the party does not have it.**
- ⚑ **Who is the Emberborn** — an individual, or a title? And **does [[Vega Bloodroot]] now qualify**, having bled on the blade and walked out with it?
- ⚑ **Who is the fire deity?** Unnamed, buried here, honoured by a pre-vampire hero. ⚑ Possible connection to **Aurona**, the goddess whose blessing was on [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s daggers.
- ⚑ **The charred map fragment** shows tunnel lines that feel almost familiar. Unidentified.
- ⚑ **A pre-vampire site, intact, underneath a vampire-built corridor.** The [[Lantern Spine]] above it was *added* by vampires. This place is older than they are.
