## The Mural Corridor

Every surface is murals and hieroglyphics. Religion checks read them **in sequence**:

> *"The mural in front of you shows vampires descending on the city like a plague… you see [[Tharos Emberborn]], greaves blazing, charging into battle… you see the **Sunfire blade** piercing the vampire lord atop a stone altar, with the blade left embedded in the altar as a seal… the last one is Emberborn leading the last stand inside this very mountain."*

A second reading named the deity: ***"a sanctuary of a fire deity whose name was [[Emberos]]"***, later repurposed as a tomb.

Deeper in: **claw marks gouged into the walls**, **scattered bones**, and *"a low distant growling echo that never seems to come from the same direction twice."*

🛑 **Something watches the whole time and never appears.** [[Flux]] rolled a **natural 20** Insight:

> *"You know you're being watched, but you can't figure out from where because the cave is so vast… the air around you feels heavy with anger, you know, like violence."*

## The Glyph Room (trap floor)

Two facing doors, chains on the walls, lit torches, and a **great sun figure in the centre**. The floor is **glyphs and pressure plates** with small stepping stones between them — **none larger than about 2½ feet**. There is no way around.

⭐ **[[Vega Bloodroot]] volunteered to go first**, unprompted. She **raged and held the rage**; [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] cast **Pass Without Trace**.

- She stepped on a plate — *"you feel your foot sink down into the floor a little more than normal, and radiant flame comes shooting out, but that you narrowly avoid."*
- She then **worked the cracks**, burning her inspiration and taking **+4 from [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s Flash of Genius**, and **identified over three-quarters of the pressure plates**.
- She **marked the path, walked back to brief the party, and took everyone across safely** — *"I'll explain to everybody what I see so that they can tell, um, themselves also where the pressure plates are, or they could… follow my path if they need to."*

⚠️ **DM ruling:** **Pass Without Trace does not apply to saves**, only Stealth checks. Filed in [[House Rules & Rulings]].
