## The Mural Corridor

Every surface is murals and hieroglyphics. Religion checks read them **in sequence**:

> *"The mural in front of you shows vampires descending on the city like a plague… you see [[Tharos Emberborn]], greaves blazing, charging into battle… you see the **Sunfire blade** piercing the vampire lord atop a stone altar, with the blade left embedded in the altar as a seal… the last one is Emberborn leading the last stand inside this very mountain."*

A second reading named the deity: ***"a sanctuary of a fire deity whose name was [[Emberos]]"***, later repurposed as a tomb.

Deeper in: **claw marks gouged into the walls**, **scattered bones**, and *"a low distant growling echo that never seems to come from the same direction twice."*

🛑 **Something watches the whole time and never appears.** [[Flux]] rolled a **natural 20** Insight:

> *"You know you're being watched, but you can't figure out from where because the cave is so vast… the air around you feels heavy with anger, you know, like violence."*

## The Glyph Room (trap floor)

Two facing doors, chains on the walls, lit torches, and a **great sun figure in the centre**. The floor is **glyphs and pressure plates** with small stepping stones between them — **none larger than about 2½ feet**. There is no way around.

⭐ **[[Vega Bloodroot]] volunteered to go first**, unprompted. She **raged and held the rage**; [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] cast **Pass Without Trace**.

- She stepped on a plate — *"you feel your foot sink down into the floor a little more than normal, and radiant flame comes shooting out, but that you narrowly avoid."*
- She then **worked the cracks**, burning her inspiration and taking **+4 from [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s Flash of Genius**, and **identified over three-quarters of the pressure plates**.
- She **marked the path, walked back to brief the party, and took everyone across safely** — *"I'll explain to everybody what I see so that they can tell, um, themselves also where the pressure plates are, or they could… follow my path if they need to."*

⚠️ **DM ruling:** **Pass Without Trace does not apply to saves**, only Stealth checks. Filed in [[House Rules & Rulings]].

## The Chain Room

**Magical chains that whip back and forth, reach out, grab things — and are red hot.**

🛑 **Never crossed.** The party stopped in the doorway and sprang their ambush on "[[Valerian Hellebore]]" instead. The entire interrogation of [[The Doppelganger]] happens here, and **the session ends here with him grappled**. **S17 opens in this room.**

## The Trap Floor — illusory tiles (S17)

Beyond the chain room, a span carrying a written warning: ***one false stone conceals a 20-foot drop.*** ⚠️ **Not lava** — the DM was explicit: *"it is not lava, it is just burning hot rock."* Falling means climbing back out of a burning pit.

The fakes betray themselves by **texture and by reflections that don't match the environment**; [[Barrett Grimmskar]] read them on an **Investigation 16** — *"you see how these have dust on them and those don't?"* — and walked the party across.

⚑ **The DM volunteered the bypasses afterwards, unprompted:** *"casting light, daylight, or radiant spells would have disrupted the illusion. A long jump or rope crossing the void also avoided the pit."* He also said **there is another use he declined to name** — *"there is another use, but it doesn't matter. I'm not going to say anything about it."*

## The Echo Chamber (S17)

Past the trap floor, where **a fiery apparition of [[Tharos Emberborn]]** manifests — *"not hostile but imposing."* A **roleplay-only trial** that cannot be fought: the echo asks *"do you carry the fire without fear?"* and demands **a bold declaration, a selfless act, a vow, or a symbolic gesture**, with consequences for hesitation or cowardice. [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s **vow to recover [[Valerian Hellebore]]** satisfied it and the party joined as a group oath; the echo *"bows his head and dissipates, leaving the chamber peacefully."*

⚠️ **Vampires would struggle to cross this stretch** — the DM: *"vampires would not, or would have a really hard time crossing this because of all the fire and light."*

## The Hall of Murals (S17)

A corridor beyond the echo chamber. *"The air grows warmer. The flames on the torches are shining brighter. The murals… glow faintly as if they're watching."* ⚑ Presumed the same murals recorded in S16; **not re-described in detail.**

## The Tomb of [[Tharos Emberborn]] (S17) — the dungeon's end

A **circular chamber** roughly **140–150 ft** across with a **domed ceiling of black glass**, runes glowing faintly along the walls, a **stone sarcophagus on a raised dais**, and **two braziers burning with smokeless orange flame**. High ceilings, statues. *"The air is warm, peaceful, and reverent."*

✅ **The Gauntlets of Burning Grasp were taken here by [[Flux]]**, and the body crumbled to ash — *"let the fire walk with you."* 🛑 **Then six feral vampire beast thralls attacked through a tunnel**, and the session ends with the party still in this room.

## What Lies Beyond

Unreached. From the tablet riddle, the next feature should be **a coffin surrounded by burning chains**, and the objective — ⚑ **the Grieveflame / the Flame Greaves** — is presumed with it.

🛑 **The tomb is untouched. The party gained nothing from it but murals and obsidian.**
