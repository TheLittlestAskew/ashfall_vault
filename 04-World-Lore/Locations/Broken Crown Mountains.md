## Description

[[Lt. Hargraven]] decoded the obsidian tablet's riddle — *a mountain shaped like a broken crown* — on the spot:

> *"one big mountain pass at one point, and then a great earthshake came and fractured it, so now it's called the Broken Crown Mountains."*

So the range **was one mountain**, was called **the King's Mountain**, and was broken by **a great earthshake** into the shape that renamed it. ⚑ **When the earthshake happened is never stated.**

## The Volcano

At the base of the range stands **the volcano** — the destination the whole session was aimed at.

- **Inactive for years**, but it **smokes and rains ash**, and the mountainside *"faintly glow[s] with natural heat."*
- 🛑 **The river is obsidian — black glass.** This is the tablet riddle's *river of black glass*, and it is where [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] worked loose **51 pieces of raw obsidian**.
- Once ruled by a **fire-worshipping tribe** that **vanished over 1,000 years ago**.
- Halfway up the mountainside: a cave mouth lit by **three torches** (two beside, one above), matching the **charred map fragment** exactly. That is the entrance to [[The Volcano Tomb]].

## The Approach (S16)

- **Day one:** warming air, scarce wildlife, and **massive claw marks on trees and boulders** — a Survival check read them as thrall marks. Camp was made in an open clearing (see *Camp on the road east* below).
- **Day two:** blackened ground, sulfur in the air, and a **half-eaten elk torn apart with feral brutality**. A Nature check ruled out vampires — a predator did this. A History check tied it to [[Lt. Hargraven]]'s rumour: **the beast thralls that revolted, killed their tamer and fled east.**

## Camp on the Road East

⚑ *Unnamed site — one night's camp, day one of the journey. Recorded here rather than given its own page.*

- [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] spotted a **silhouette on the ridgeline at 300 feet** — *"hunched, moving quickly, and it seems to be moving incorrect for a human."* She woke [[Vega Bloodroot]]. [[Flux]] later heard it **moving away**. **Never pursued** — and the impostor later admitted he was *"steering you away"* from it.
- [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s **Detect Evil and Good** revealed **[[Undead Centipede-Worms]]** leeching life from every sleeper, invisible to everyone else. One was destroyed by Produce Flame; the rest scattered.
- Zelda then remembered she had **Tiny Hut**, cast it ritually, and the party finished the night safely.

## Lore

🛑 **The tablet's riddle, in full**, as read out by [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]: *a mountain shaped like a broken crown, a river of black glass, a cavern mouth marked by three flame sigils, a coffin surrounded by burning chains* — closing on ***"the graves lie where the mountain bleeds fire."***

**Three of the four clauses were confirmed on sight in S16.** The fourth — the coffin in burning chains — is presumably past the chain room the party never crossed.
