# The Cliffside Tower

> ⚑ **PROVISIONAL PAGE NAME — THE TOWER IS NEVER NAMED IN FICTION.** Across the whole of S18 the DM calls it only *"the tower."* This descriptive title follows the vault's convention for unnamed locations (cf. [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]], [[The Volcano Tomb]]). **Rename on the DM's word.**
> 🛑 **This is the tower [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] saw inside [[The Doppelganger]]'s memory in S16** — the one she found *"very familiar… you saw it just recently."* It held the real [[Valerian Hellebore]] for **three weeks**. It had no page until now because, until S18, there was nothing confirmed to put on one.

## Description

The DM's approach text, given as the party marched in:

> *"the journey to the tower is a march through the dead— the dead zone. Uh, the trees twist like broken ribs. The sky dims unnaturally, as if recoiling from the structure ahead, as the tower rises from the cliffside like a rotten tooth. Its stones blackened by centuries of dark magic. A sickly green glow pulses from the highest window, slow, steady, like a heartbeat. The wind carries faint screams."*

⚑ **The screams were [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s** — confirmed by the DM at 00:20:18: *"And those screams you're hearing are Val."*

**Construction:** granite. The DM, asked: *"Rock, believe it or not."* … *"Granite. You act like I don't know."*

## Known Features (S18)

- **Two levels only** — established by [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s **History 22**. The main floor, and the top.
- **A big winding staircase** runs all the way up.
- **The entrance faces the ocean.** The door on the party's side is **sealed with solid rock and magically enchanted**.
- 🛑 **The sealed door is functionally unopenable.** The DM's ruling: *"I would let you try to break through if you rolled **2 nat 20s in a row.**"* The party tried anyway, including intimidating it. [[Barrett Grimmskar]]: *"And if my grandmother was born with wheels, she'd be a bike."*
- **Part of the wall on the left side is broken down and climbable** — the actual way up, alongside bracers and teleportation.
- **Ruined battlements** the thralls burst out of.
- **A sickly green glow pulses from the highest window**, slow and steady, *"like a heartbeat."*

### The ritual chamber (tower top)

- **Open-topped — no roof.**
- **Runes set into the floor** forming a magic circle that *"does damage"* if crossed. The DM: *"you'd have to run around the circle though."*
- **[[Valerian Hellebore]] suspended inside it**, *"chains of shimmering energy wrapped around his arms, his chest, his throat"* — not dangling but *"floating magically wrapped in some really dope-ass chains."*
- **A massive ritual crystal floating behind the necromancer**, 20–25 lbs, diamond/amethyst-shaped, *"glowing brighter with every drop of stolen magic."*
- **After the kill:** the runes flicker out, the crystal cracks and spills light, the chains shatter into drifting sparks, and the crystal leaves **8 enchantable shards**.

## The Rumour

[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s History check also turned up what the locals say: it is *"supposedly haunted Or controlled by a necromancer, but everyone's too scared to come out here to test that theory."*

⚑ **The rumour was correct**, and the party is the first group on record to test it.

## The Lore, Given Flat

> *"This tower was once a lighthouse that got repurposed into a, uh, fort outpost tower for defense during war. And that's all the lore on the tower."*

⚠️ **The DM explicitly closed the subject.** *"And that's all the lore on the tower"* is a boundary, not an omission — do not expect more without asking.
⚑ **Which war is not stated.** The campaign now has two on the record (the First and Second Vampire Wars, S16) and the tower is not keyed to either.

🛑 **Every distance ruling in the back half of the session hangs on which number the DM had in front of him** — Vortex Warp's range, the bracers' 50-ft limit, Lulu's 60-ft fly speed, Barrett's climbs. **Not resolvable from the transcript.** See [[DM Questions — Open]].

## Events (S18)

- **The march in.** The party, camped overnight in a Tiny Hut at [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]], chose to **walk** rather than teleport. They arrived undetected — *"Are they aware that we're here?"* / *"I'd imagine not."*
- **The ambush.** Thralls burst from the battlements as a towering figure stepped onto the tower top and declared *"You're too late. His power is already flowing into the circle."* 🛑 That figure is named **[[The Beast Master]]** once and never again — see *Open Threads*.
- **One continuous encounter, ~2h10m**, courtyard to tower-top. Initiative 00:09:57, combat ends 02:18:36.
- **Two characters solved the vertical problem.** [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] took a **nat-20 Athletics (26)** bracer swing around the tower face and tackled a thrall off the wall for 28 fall damage, then later skipped the climb entirely with **Vortex Warp** at 2nd level. [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] went **invisible** and rappelled ~53 feet up on her bracers.
- **The ritual was broken from the inside.** [[Mr. Cat]] on the necromancer's face → the ritual crystal thrown at him with **Catapult** → **Dispel Magic** on the circle → [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] grabbing [[Valerian Hellebore]] and **Dimension Door**ing out.
- **The necromancer died on 1 HP**, and *"something answers the dying pulse of the ritual. A deep resonant tone, a warning, a promise."*
- **The tower was looted and left.** [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] stripped the body alone at the top; [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] then teleported the party — and the horse — back to the castle. **The carriage was abandoned outside.**
