# Watcher's Pit

> 🛑 **NEVER VISITED.** Named by [[Marrowin Quill]] in [[Session 13 — The Cartographer of the Underkeep]], offered as a route in [[Session 14 — An Angel with a Shotgun]], and **declined**. This page exists to keep it from being forgotten.
> ⚑ Transcribed once as "the water's pit"; corrected to **Watcher's Pit**, which is how it is rendered six other times by four different speakers.

## What is actually known

Almost nothing, and that is the point of this page. Everything on record is second-hand:

- 🔀 **It is one of two ways to reach the veins.** The DM, in S14: *"there are 2 ways to get to the veins. One of them you go down through the Watcher's Pit, and then the other one you go through the grate."* **The party took the grate.**
- ⚠️ **It has a reputation for danger.** [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] had been trying to get into it before the party found her: *"I'm just interested what's in it. I'm curious. **I've heard there's danger**, and so I thought I'd check it out."*
- 🛑 **It is deep enough to be lethal.** Lulu later admitted her real reason for heading there — she intended to *"take a short nap off a long cliff."* Whatever else it is, **it is a drop.**
- ⚑ **Nobody has said what the "Watcher" is.** The name implies something watching, or something watched. Marrowin listed it without comment.

## Events

- **S13 — named.** Marrowin included it in the route he drew, between the Flooded Gallery and the [[Broken Descent]].
- **S14 — offered and declined.** [[Flux]] initially proposed going through it as a favour to Lulu — *"she wants to go to the Watcher's Pit, so we can go that way to get where we need to go. We can help her, she can help us."* Once Lulu explained why she had actually been going, Flux redirected: *"since you're not going to end it anymore, how about you come with us and we go through the veins instead."*
- **S15 — not revisited.** The party took the red-lit path from the [[Old Shrine]] instead and then returned to base.

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **It is still there, still unvisited, and still a route to the veins.** If the [[Grate of the Veins]] turns out to be sealed or guarded, this is the alternative.
- ⚑ **What is the Watcher?** Unasked.
- ⚑ **The Flooded Gallery** — the other Marrowin-named location the party has never seen (*"Blood run off from the upper floors. Don't fall in. Things float in there that shouldn't"*). ⚑ **It has no page and no encounter.** Two of the six mapped stages have now been skipped entirely.
- ⚑ **Lulu wanted something from it before she wanted to die in it.** Her stated curiosity and her stated intent don't quite match, and nobody pressed her.
