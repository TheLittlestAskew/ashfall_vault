# The Dead Zone

> Named in play from S02 onward and listed as a **Region** in [[Names & Terms]] — but this page was only created at [[Session 11 — A Delightful Chase]], the first session set entirely inside it.
> ⚑ "The Dead Zone" is what the table and the DM call it. Whether that is an in-fiction proper name or party shorthand has never been established.

## Description

Ruined buildings, collapsed walls, shattered stone, ash and mud. Long shadows. **Parts of it are actively on fire** — DM-confirmed when a player asked: *"Some of it's on fire, some of it is not."*

The DM's read-aloud for it is consistently one of held breath rather than emptiness:

> "Outside, the dead zone is silent, almost too silent. The wind carries a faint metallic tang, like old blood left to dry."

> "the dead zone feels alive with eyes."

## Known Features (S11)

- **The broken road** — the route from the library to the castle. Passable on foot; the party crossed it in a single unbroken push after the ambush was cleared.
- **The ambush ground** — collapsed walls, rubble, a **rotted doorway**, and rooftops high enough for a beast thrall to leap from. Thralls **climb the walls** and fight from elevation.
- **A building ruled at 24 feet tall**, from which [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] dropped with a grappled thrall. ⚑ Fall damage was stated as "12," which does not match 24 ft under standard rules.
- **Vampire patrols** move through it — *"drifting through the ruins like hungry ghosts."*
- ⚑ [[Saint Ardas]] stands within or beyond it, per the glossary. The relationship between the two locations has never been drawn in play.

## Events

- **S11 — the ambush.** The party stepped out of [[The Grand Library of the Veiled Dawn]] into the Dead Zone bound for the castle. A party-wide Perception check turned up **broken branches at chest height** and **four sets of fresh prints running the way they had to go** ([[Flux]], natural 20 for 30). They **stealthed past a vampire patrol** — one paused, sniffed toward them, twitched, and moved on without engaging. A branch snapped behind them: *"Sharp, deliberate, not the wind… Whatever follows you is clever. And patient."*
  Then **six [[Beast Thrall]]s** burst from the rubble — flanking, circling and **herding** rather than charging — while **[[The Cloaked Figure]]** spoke into every mind at once: *"run, I want to see how far you get."* Nobody ran.
  **All six thralls were killed. No PC was downed and no death saves were rolled.** With the field clear, the cloaked figure stepped out of the shadows with **[[The Massive Thrall]]** at his side, said *"you led me on a delightful chase… I can't wait till next time,"* and **vanished into the fog** without ever entering combat.
  The party then cleared the Dead Zone and reached [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]] for a long rest.

## Events (S16)

- **The party crossed it eastbound**, outbound from [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]] toward the [[Broken Crown Mountains]]. No encounter — the crossing is not described in detail.
- 🛑 **NEW GEOGRAPHY: the [[Broken Crown Mountains]] are visible from the Dead Zone.** [[Lt. Hargraven]], pointing east: *"I've I'm surprised you haven't seen them so far."* **This is the first time anything has been placed on the horizon beyond it** — the Dead Zone has been a boundary until now, and it turns out to have a far side you can see.
- The mountains lie **two days by wagon** past it.
- ⚠️ Beyond the Dead Zone the land changes: **warming air, scarce wildlife, massive claw marks on trees and boulders** from the revolted [[Beast Thrall]]s, then blackened ground and sulfur as the volcano approaches.
