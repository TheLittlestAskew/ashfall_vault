## Description

Marrowin's warning, and the only line in his whole route recitation where he hesitated:

> "Beast thralls patrol it. They smell fear, they smell hope, they smell everything."

The DM's read-aloud on arrival:

> "a jagged sloping corridor scarred by claw marks and soaked in old blood. The stone floor is cracked with deep gouges carved by what you assume are beast thralls. The air is humid and thick, carrying the scent of wet fur and copper."

## Known Features

- 🐾 **Claw marks and old blood** throughout; deep gouges in the stone floor consistent with [[Beast Thrall]]s.
- 🔊 **Growls from below that never repeat the same pattern** — *"as if multiple creatures are listening and responding."* ⚑ This is described as **communication**, not noise, and nothing came of it in S14. Whatever is making them is still down there.
- 🪧 **Thrall-scratched symbols** on the walls **indicating territorial boundaries.** ⚑ The party has evidence that beast thralls mark and respect territory — new information about the species, unremarked at the table.
- 🌉 **The rope bridge.** **110 feet long**, two people wide but *"you don't know if it can support the weight of 2 people,"* boards missing, rope tattered. Crossing requires a **Dex check, an Acrobatics check and a Constitution save** (the last for looking down).
- 🔥 **The chamber at the base** — a **fire pit in the centre that has been raging for years**, surrounded by **waist-deep water**. Lair of a **water elemental**.
- 🚪 At the bottom: the **[[Grate of the Veins]]** — massive iron, vibrating, pulsing with a heartbeat-like glow.

## Events

- **S14 — the crossing and the elemental.** The party arrived carrying [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s body in the bag of holding, having picked up [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] at the [[Old Shrine]].
  **The bridge:** [[Valerian Hellebore]] **Wild Shaped into a bat** and flew it. [[Flux]] **Misty Stepped**. [[Barrett Grimmskar]] crossed on a 9 and a 14 and arrived badly nauseous. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] burned **all three Lucky points** on one Acrobatics check for an 11, then rolled a **natural 20** on the Con save. [[Vega Bloodroot]] slipped and stumbled across carrying [[Mr. Cat]], flavoured as *"Sam carrying Frodo up Mount Doom."* [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] rolled a natural 1 on initiative, rerolled it as a halfling, and meandered across *"still in her feels."*
  **The elemental:** one **water elemental** rose from the waist-deep water — the party had entered its lair. Barrett opened with two shots for 18; Vega activated her **Eldritch Claw tattoo** for 15 feet of reach and hit recklessly for **27**; Samothy read its exact HP off a passive and landed a **Ballistic Smite**; **Lulu opened her account with the party** casting **Mass Cure Wounds for 32**; [[Valerian Hellebore]] got the DM to formally rule that **water is wet** and dropped **Depth Charge at 4th level for 32 thunder**. Vega then remembered her forgotten tattoo and Brutal Strike damage and the retroactive **15** killed it — credit she immediately gave away.
  **The session ended here**, deliberately, so the DM could build the veins properly: *"going down into the veins is gonna be a whole probably 2 sessions by itself."*

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **Marrowin said beast thralls patrol this and none appeared.** Either they were elsewhere, or they are between the party and the veins right now. The growls from below were never sourced.
- ⚑ **What is making the non-repeating growls, and what are they saying?** Explicitly framed as multiple creatures listening and responding to each other.
- ⚑ **A fire pit that has burned for years, underground, unattended.** Nobody examined it. Note the thematic rhyme with the [[Old Shrine]]'s fire deity and the relic recovered in S15.
- ⚑ **Territorial markings** imply a resident population with borders. Whose border, and what is on the other side?
- ⚑ **The bridge is the only recorded way back up** short of [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]'s Teleportation Circle, which only returns to base.
