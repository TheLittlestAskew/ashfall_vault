## Description

Rows of skulls set in wall alcoves, hollow eyes catching torchlight. The silence is characterised as **expectant rather than empty**.

Marrowin's framing is the key to it — the skulls were placed **to make intruders feel watched**, deliberately, by people who built this a very long time ago.

## Known Features

- 💀 **The alcoves are a trap system.** Disturbing too many of them wakes what is stored there.
- 👁️ **The one turned skull.** Every row sits in perfect symmetry **except one skull, turned slightly toward the stairway**. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] investigated it; the jaw **cracked open onto a pressure plate**, which opened the **narrow bypass tunnel** Marrowin had drawn.
- 🔊 **The skulls mimic.** As the party passed, some exhaled faintly — not words, but **playback**: the party's own footsteps, breaths and heartbeats returned to them.
- 🔦 **A hidden alcove** held a rusted lantern with a still-burning ember of **cold flame**. [[Valerian Hellebore]] spotted it (Perception 16) and pulled it free on a **Constitution check of 19** as a door snapped shut on his arm. It sheds a **25-foot circle of light that penetrates the vampires' magical darkness** — the one thing ordinary darkvision cannot do.
- 🕳️ **The bypass tunnel** runs from here alongside the [[Lantern Spine]]. 🛑 **Now collapsed, with [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s death site inside it.** See [[Lantern Spine]] for what the tunnel actually was.

## Events

- **S13 — first entry and the Bonewraiths.** The party arrived via a sewer grate in the castle and a service ladder into the old catacombs. The stairway down was *"carved by hands that feared what they were creating"*; the walls carried jagged symbols of protection, warding and desperation, **some scratched out as if someone tried to undo their own magic**. Cold drafts carried whispers that never resolved into words — the DM was explicit that **everyone hears them**, and that nobody admits it.
  Valerian claimed the cold-flame torch. Samothy turned the watching skull and opened the bypass. Too many alcoves had been disturbed, and **two Bonewraiths** formed out of swirling dust, releasing a **memory echo** — the sound of ancient workers arguing about the horrors below, moments before their deaths. Only Valerian failed the Constitution save against it.
  Kills: **[[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]** dual-wielding her fire-and-cold daggers took Wraith A (*"not today, you spooky bitch"*) and marked Wraith B with Slayer's Prey; **[[Vega Bloodroot]]** finished B with the Staff of Flames. The wraiths crumbled to dust and their clothes vanished.
  ⚑ **[[Flux]] cast `Rite of the Dawning Radiance` here**, granting the party **Sunward Blessing** — a ruling S14 later contradicted. See *Open Threads*.
  The session ended here, with the [[Lantern Spine]] ahead.

## Open Threads

- ⚑ **What else is stored in the alcoves?** Two Bonewraiths came out of "too many disturbed" alcoves. The corridor has rows of them and the party moved on.
- ⚑ **The memory echo** named *"the horrors below"* — ancient workers arguing about what is at the bottom, moments before dying. **Nobody followed up on what they were describing.** It is the earliest in-world testimony the party has about the veins.
- ⚑ **The scratched-out warding symbols** — someone tried to undo their own protective magic here. Never explained.
- ⚑ **The whispers in the cold drafts.** Everyone hears them; nobody has ever said so out loud in-fiction.
- 🛑 **`Rite of the Dawning Radiance` / Sunward Blessing.** S13 records Flux casting it here as a 1st-level ritual; S14's opening establishes it is a **5th-level** spell that she is not taking, and the blessing is never referenced again. **Neither note has been amended.** See [[DM Questions — Open]].
- ⚑ **Bonewraith vs bone wraith** — casing and spacing are inconsistent in the source. Pick one for [[Names & Terms]].
