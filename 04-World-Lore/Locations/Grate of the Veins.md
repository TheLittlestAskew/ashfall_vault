## Description

> "there's a massive iron grate at the bottom. It vibrates slightly, pulsing with a heartbeat-like glow."

That is the last image of [[Session 14 — An Angel with a Shotgun]], and it is deliberately the last thing the party saw before the DM stopped for the night.

## What lies beyond

**The veins** — the tunnels beneath the city. [[Marrowin Quill]] circled them in thick dark ink at the bottom of his map and called them:

> "the arteries of the vampire empire."

⚠️ **The DM has said the veins will take *"probably 2 sessions by itself,"*** and stopped S14 specifically so he could build the encounter properly. Treat this as a major arc threshold, not a corridor.

## Known Features

- 🚪 **Massive iron.** No stated mechanism for opening it.
- 💓 **It vibrates and pulses with a heartbeat-like glow.** ⚑ The [[Old Shrine]]'s relic chamber also ran on a "heartbeat light," which **went out** when [[Vega Bloodroot]] pulled the sword. **Whether the two are connected has not been raised.**
- 🔀 **It is one of two routes.** The DM, in S14: *"there are 2 ways to get to the veins. One of them you go down through the [[Watcher's Pit|Watcher's Pit]], and then the other one you go through the grate."* The party chose the grate.
- 🌊 The chamber before it holds a **fire pit burning for years** and **waist-deep water** — the water elemental's lair, now cleared.

## Events

- **S13 — named.** Marrowin drew the route and circled the veins beneath everything else.
- **S14 — reached.** The party crossed the [[Broken Descent]]'s 110-foot rope bridge, killed the water elemental in a single round, and stopped in front of the grate.
- **S15 — bypassed for now.** The party did not open it. They cleared the [[Old Shrine]]'s relic chamber and the mine instead, then **[[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] cast Teleportation Circle and returned everyone to base.**
  ⚠️ **The three-day clock stated in S14 has not been reconciled against this withdrawal.** The party believed they had three days to reach the bottom and get back out, or they would not be let back in.

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **It has not been opened.** Everything below is unknown.
- 🛑 **The three-day clock.** Stated in S14 as a hard limit on getting back out. The party has since left and returned to base. ⚑ **Does the clock still run? Can they get back in?** Nobody has asked.
- ⚑ **The heartbeat.** The grate pulses like one; the relic pedestal did too, and stopped when the sword came free. **Unraised connection.**
- ⚑ **How does it open?** No mechanism, keyhole, ward or lever is described.
- ⚑ **The [[Watcher's Pit]] is still the other option** — and the party now has no reason to go there, having taken the grate route, which means an entire mapped location may go unvisited.
