# Homebrew Abilities

> Abilities, spells, and features used at the table that have **no 2024 PHB match**. They are recorded here as **canon-in-play** — what was said, who used it, and what it did.
>
> 🛑 **Do not flag these as errors and do not hunt for sources.** Taylor is a player and does not have access to the DM's homebrew. An unmatched name is not a mistake; it's an ability she hasn't been shown the card for. If the DM volunteers the real wording later, update the row.
