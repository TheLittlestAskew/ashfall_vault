# Boilborn

> First encountered: [[Session 15 — Only Those Who Bleed Fire]] (08/27/2026), four of them, guarding the relic chamber below the [[Broken Descent]].
> 🛑 **Homebrew, and purpose-built.** The DM said so unprompted after the fight: *"I figured it was a good session to introduce the anti-tank. I figured Vega was causing too much damage to be left alone... **I made this enemy specifically to fight the barbarian.**"* Nothing here comes from a published book.
> ⚑ **Spelling is 60% — audio only**, never written down or spelled out. It is thematically anchored (they burst boils), so it is very likely right.
> ✅ **SPELLING INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED S18.** A single Boilborn returned in [[Session 18 — I Knew You'd Come]] and its **Rupture Burst DC of 16 matches this page exactly.** ⚠️ **The S18 spell-check capped three rows at 60% on a "first-seen term" basis — that rationale was WRONG**, because this page already existed. **The S18 corrections are better-supported than that table states.**
> 🛑 **STILL MISSING FROM [[Names & Terms]]** — a glossary gap, not a first sighting.

## Description

The party called them zombies for the entire fight and the DM let them, because **he could not get tokens made and used zombie art**. They are not zombies.

They move **jerkily and twitch constantly** — *"like they got a tick as they move"* — they are covered in **pustules**, and they carry a strong smell of decay.

🛑 **They are unidentifiable.** [[Valerian Hellebore]] rolled a **natural 20 on Nature (29)** and got nothing:

> "these creatures are like nothing you've ever seen or heard of before through rumors or anything."

For a party at level 10 who have catalogued [[Beast Thrall]]s, [[Feral Vampire Thrall]]s and the [[Unknown Great Cat]], a natural 20 returning *no information at all* is itself the datum: **the Boilborn are new to this world, or new to it recently.**

## Combat Capabilities (all observed S15)

- 🩸 **Boil burst — the signature, and the anti-tank mechanic.** A melee attacker takes **2d8 poison, halved**, plus a **Constitution save or be poisoned** until the end of the Boilborn's next turn, as *"a boil on their skin bursts and explodes all over you."* [[Vega Bloodroot]] triggered it on her first hit (failed the save on a 13, took 7).
  ⚑ **It did not recur on her second hit.** She asked for an Insight check first, was told she didn't think it would happen again, swung, and it didn't. **Whether that is once-per-creature, once-per-round, or a DM call is unresolved.**
- ⚡ **Spasm Step** (bonus action) — jerks **15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks**.
- 💥 **Rupture Burst** — an area effect, **DC 16 Constitution**. Dealt **30** on a failure and **15** on a success, hitting **three PCs at once** ([[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]], [[Flux]] and [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]). This is what dropped Lulu the first time and nearly killed Flux.
- 🤜 **Grapple** — on a hit, followed by **3d6 poison at the start of each of its turns** while held. Zelda was caught by this and escaped with **Misty Step** (see [[House Rules & Rulings]]).
- **To-hit** observed at +8. Damage observed at 12, 13, 17, 19 and 25.
- ⚑ **HP unstated.** [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s passive reads exact creature HP and he used it, but the number was not said aloud on the recording.

## Record

- **S15 — the relic chamber.** Four (A, B, C, D) guarding the sword in the stone. The fight ran roughly **thirteen rounds**, the longest of the recent run, against a party rolling **8 natural 1s across 118 d20s**.
  Killed by: **Vega** (two, including the last, with Reckless Attack, Eldritch Claw tattoo and Great Weapon Master), with the bulk of the attrition from **[[Valerian Hellebore]]'s Call Lightning at 5th level re-triggered every round**, **[[Barrett Grimmskar]]'s** fire-revolver crit for **42** plus Gut Shot, and **[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s** Slayer's Prey → Hunter's Mark grind.
  🛑 **[[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] was reduced to 0 HP twice** and revived both times by [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]. [[Flux]] confirmed she would have died without the **Tough** feat.
  **No PC died.**

- **S18 — ⚠️ ONE OF THEM RETURNED, SERVING [[The Necromancer]] AT [[The Cliffside Tower]].** 🛑 **This is the first time a Boilborn has appeared outside the relic chamber**, and it is the first evidence that they are **not native to that room** — they can be fielded, or they travel.
  💥 **Rupture Burst fired again, and the DM had been waiting to use it:** *"it's [[Boilborn]]'s turn, and he's got something I've been wanting to try."* **DC 16 Constitution, 34 damage** — hitting [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]], [[Flux]] and [[Vega Bloodroot]] at once. ✅ **DC 16 matches S15 exactly.** ⚠️ **Damage is up from S15's 30/15 to 34/17** — consistent with the DM's standing rule that **enemies level with the party.**
  Vega **saved on a 17** and took **17** (half); [[Flux]] **burned Heroic Inspiration on the save and took the full 34 anyway**; Lulu took **34**.
  🩸 **The boil burst ran all night.** [[Vega Bloodroot]] stood next to it for most of the fight and **triggered bursts repeatedly** — *"every time somebody hurt it, it burst, and I got a mouthful."* She failed **three Constitution saves** and was **poisoned three separate times**. ⚑ **This settles nothing about the trigger condition** (S15's open question) — but it does establish the burst **can recur many times in one fight**, which S15 could not confirm.
  ⚑ **The DM described it after the fact**, when [[Flux]] asked what had hit them: *"just imagine all of a sudden all these boils just started swelling on his skin and, and then they burst open and poison fluid shot everywhere."*
  ⚔️ **KILLED BY [[Flux]]** — bow (27 to hit, 10 damage) + **Sneak Attack (18)** + **Finger Guns** (20 to hit, 7). She had softened it earlier with **Witch Bolt at 3rd level for 24**, and [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]]'s **Draconic Spirit** breath weapon hit it in round 1 after it **failed a DEX save on a 12**.
  ⚠️ **THE ANTI-TANK FAILED THE SAME WAY TWICE.** Built to punish [[Vega Bloodroot]], it once again did its worst damage to **[[Flux]] and [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]]** — the same two characters it nearly killed in S15. **Vega, raging and resistant, took the least of the three.**

## The design intent, on the record

The DM built these to punish a specific player, said so, and then assessed the result honestly:

> "I made this enemy specifically to fight the barbarian. And the barbarian didn't take as much damage as I thought the barbarian was gonna take tonight."

> "the tank didn't take as much damage as I thought. **Lulu got demolished.** Flux almost died too."

⚠️ **The anti-tank did not beat the tank.** It nearly killed the healer and the archer instead — the two characters it was not aimed at. Worth remembering: **the boil-burst punishes melee, so it hurts whoever is *not* protected by the front line**, which is the opposite of what it was designed to do.
