## Description

> *"There are bugs near you that are not of the living sort. They are long like worms. Think, think centipedes… they seem to be sapping energy away as everyone is sleeping."*
> *"It looks like they're leeching your aura or your life force away from you."*

- **Long, worm- or centipede-like.**
- 🛑 **Invisible to ordinary senses.** They were found only because [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] cast **Detect Evil and Good** (30 ft) on watch — **nobody else could see them**, including the two PCs awake.
- They **feed on sleepers**, draining life force or aura. They were on **every sleeping member of the party** at once.

## Behaviour Observed (S16)

- Attach to sleeping creatures and **leech passively**. No attack was rolled and no damage was assigned on the record.
- **Fire drives them off.** [[The Doppelganger]] (as "[[Valerian Hellebore]]") cast **Produce Flame** at the ground — *"Just roll for the damage because you are just blasting at the ground."* **One was destroyed; the rest moved off.**
- They did not return once the party was inside [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]'s **Tiny Hut**.
- ⚠️ **They did not press an attack when discovered.** They scattered.

## Origin

⚑ **Unidentified.** [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]], who had never heard of undead insects feeding on sleepers, called it plainly: **there is a necromancer in the area.**

That inference is the *only* link to the **ridgeline figure** spotted at 300 feet the same night — *"hunched, moving quickly… moving incorrect for a human."* 🛑 **The connection is a player's read, not a DM statement.** The figure was never pursued, and [[The Doppelganger]] later admitted he had been **steering the party away from it**.

## Party Response

[[Vega Bloodroot]] wanted to hunt the source — *"It's kind of violating. He didn't even want to fight"* — and **was outvoted.**
