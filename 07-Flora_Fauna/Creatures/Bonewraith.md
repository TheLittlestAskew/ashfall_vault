# Bonewraith

> First and only encounter: [[Session 13 — The Cartographer of the Underkeep]] (08/02/2026), two of them, in the [[Ossuary Approach]].
> ⚑ **Casing and spacing are inconsistent in the source** — the transcript has both `Bonewraith` and `bone wraith`. **`Bonewraith` is the vault spelling.** Worth one line of confirmation from the DM.

## Description

They do not walk in. **They form** — out of the swirling dust of the ossuary's disturbed alcoves, in a corridor whose walls are lined with skulls that had been sitting undisturbed for a very long time.

⚠️ **They are a consequence, not a patrol.** Nothing summoned them and nothing was guarding anything. The party turned a marked skull and searched too many alcoves, and the corridor answered.

When killed they **crumble back to dust, and their clothes vanish with them** — which is the only real evidence the vault has about what they are: not corpses wearing grave goods, but something wearing the *shape* of the dead, cloth included.

## Combat Capabilities (observed S13)

- 🧠 **Memory echo.** On forming, they release a wave the DM describes as sound rather than force: **the voices of ancient workers arguing about the horrors below, moments before their deaths.** Resisted with a **Constitution save**; only [[Valerian Hellebore]] failed it.
  🛑 **This is the earliest in-world testimony the party has about the veins**, delivered by the dead who built the place — and **nobody followed up on what the workers were describing.**
- **They are not durable.** Both died inside a single round of the party's turns. Wraith A fell to [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] dual-wielding fire-and-cold daggers; Wraith B was softened by [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s **Ballistic Smite** (reported at 13 HP off his HP-reading passive) and finished by [[Vega Bloodroot]] with the **Staff of Flames** for 20.
- ⚑ **HP, AC, attack routine and damage type are all unrecorded.** They landed few enough hits that nothing was reconstructable. The archive holds **3 rolls for Wraith A and 3 for Wraith B**, best total 22.
- ⚑ **No radiant/necrotic vulnerability was tested or stated.** Given the party killed them with fire and cold, and given the [[Boilborn]] later proved immune to assumptions, do not assume undead resistances apply.

## Record

- **S13 — the [[Ossuary Approach]].** Two Bonewraiths, formed after the party disturbed too many skull alcoves while [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] worked the one turned skull that opened the bypass tunnel. **Killed in a single round. No PC downed, no death saves.**
  ⚑ This is the fight in which [[Flux]] is recorded casting **`Rite of the Dawning Radiance`**, granting the party **Sunward Blessing** — a ruling S14 later contradicted. See *Open Threads*.

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **What were the workers describing?** The memory echo is the only first-hand account of *"the horrors below"* the campaign has produced, and it came from people who died building the route the party is now walking. **Unfollowed.**
- ⚑ **Are there more?** The [[Ossuary Approach]] has rows of alcoves and the party disturbed only some of them. They walked on.
- ⚑ **What are they made of?** They form from dust and their **clothes vanish on death** — so the clothes were part of the manifestation, not loot.
- ⚑ **Statblock is effectively unknown.** If they recur, expect to be reconstructing from scratch.
- ⚑ **Relationship to the cursed bypass dust?** The tunnel beside this corridor turned out to be a burial ground for cursed people whose ground-up remains carry **seven fused curses** and killed [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] one session later. ⚠️ **Two different kinds of animate grave-dust, ten metres apart, never connected in play.**
- ⚑ **Spelling:** `Bonewraith` or `bone wraith`? See [[Names & Terms]].
