# Unknown Great Cat

> ⚑ **Descriptive title — the species is unnamed in-fiction.** First evidence: [[Session 10 — Never Truly Alone]]. **Never encountered alive** — the entire record is a single severed skull found in the restricted section of [[The Grand Library of the Veiled Dawn]].

## Description (from the skull, S10)

- **Stature** jaguar-like; **build/musculature** tiger-like. Not identified as either.
- **Dentition:** **4 fangs on the bottom, 2 on top.**
- **Severed at the neck** — DM: *"on the back of the skull where the neck would meet the spine, you can see where it was severed."* This was a deliberate cut, not scavenging.
- **Non-magical.** [[Flux]] cast **Identify** on it: no magic. *"It's just a plain skull."*
- A **jade ball (~1 lb)** rests in the mouth. Also non-magical. Placed, not swallowed — "You can't separate the set."

## Lore

DM, S10: *"you don't know what animal it is, but you do know that at one point this animal was abundant in the area."*

That is the whole of it. No living specimen has been seen, no name given, and no connection to the vampires, the thralls, or the [[Cult of the Pale World]] has been asserted by anyone at the table.
