# Feral Vampire Thrall

> First encountered: [[Session 01 — Graduation Day]] (15 slain). Run at-table on a reskinned "gnoll" statblock (table shorthand: "gnolls").

## Description (DM, verbatim-sourced)

"Humanoid shapes running on all fours, eyes glowing a dull hungry red. Their jaws are distended, teeth broken and jagged, nails long enough to scrape the cobblestone. They do not speak, they only scream." Blood and bits of flesh in their teeth. "Really big, huge, rabid dog monsters… built like the things from *A Quiet Place*."

## Known Capabilities & Behavior

- **Hunt by sound** — "their hearing is so fine-tuned they can literally hear your heartbeat"
- Multiattack: two claw/bite attacks per turn (2d6-class damage observed)
- Pack behavior; emerge from fog; climb and leap between buildings
- Fully obedient to [[The Beast Tamer]] — froze mid-charge at his "Halt"

## Weaknesses Observed (S01)

- **Radiant damage** — devastating (table rule: doubled vs. vampires); radiant kills immolate the body to ash/nothing
- **Loud sound** — their hypersensitive hearing cuts both ways: Zelda's Thunderwave (audible 300 ft) was ruled doubled and burst one thrall's skull
- No speech, no tactics beyond swarming observed

## S17 — The masterless ones (Tomb of Tharos Emberborn)

🛑 **These are the feral variety, and the DM gave them an origin for the first time.** Six of them (**A–F**) ambushed the party in the tomb the moment the **Gauntlets of Burning Grasp** were lifted.

> *"These vampire beast thralls are the feral ones. They don't have a master. They're the ones that turned and killed their beast master guy, you know, the gangly little sickly looking emo boys. Yeah, uh, they turned on theirs and killed him. Even the regular vampires avoid these thralls because they are just straight violent chump change."*

**New this session:**

- ✅ **They killed their own [[The Beast Master|Beast Master]]** — the first recorded instance of the thrall-bond being broken from below. ⚑ This bears directly on the S10 lore that *"a Beast Master standing alone is vulnerable."*
- ✅ **Even other vampires avoid them.**
- ✅ **Weak to radiant, thunder, and silvered weapons** — confirmed on the record when [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asked directly. **No immunities.**
- ✅ **They take extra damage every turn in sunlight** — the DM used **5d6** for [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie]]'s **Daylight** sphere.
- ⚠️ **Their CR scales with the party.** *"I'm adjusting the CR rating and stuff for the thralls because they level up with you guys, so their points have been upped a little bit, and I'm just testing them out on you."* ⚑ **This is a standing house rule for thralls, stated plainly for the first time.**
- ⚠️ **They attack the helpless.** Thrall B mauled the bound, paralysed [[The Doppelganger]] to 0 HP; the DM allowed auto-hits.

🛑 **SENSE DISCREPANCY — FLAGGED, NOT RECONCILED.** S17's DM says **they have no eyes and hunt by smell** — *"they don't have eyes, so no, they can't be blinded like vampires would be by the sun. They don't have eyes. They go by smell, remember?"* ⚠️ **This contradicts the S01 record above**, which states they *"hunt by sound"* with hearing fine-tuned enough to *"hear your heartbeat"* — the basis for the S01 Thunderwave ruling. ⚑ **Possibilities:** the feral masterless variety differs from the S01 master-bound ones; the DM revised it; or one of the two is a misremembering. **Neither entry was changed. This is a DM question.**
