# Beast Thrall

> First encountered: [[Session 02 — Ashes of the Living]] (3 slain — the blind variant). Distinct from the [[Feral Vampire Thrall]]: ferals are human-shaped and drain their kills; beast thralls are **massive** and crush/maul/eat them.

## Description

Large creatures ("much bigger than Zelda" — popping them with a toothpick, per the DM). The Hampshire specimen forced its way through walls: deep claw impressions in floorboards, furniture crushed inward, blood on the ceiling. Kills are **crushed, mauled, half-eaten** — not drained.

## Variants

- **Sighted / "eyed"** — destroyed Valerian's family home in [[Hampshire]] (estimated 3–5 ran with the feral pack). **Confirmed on the record in S11**, when [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] lit the field with Flame Blade and asked whether it hurt them — DM: ***"you do see that these ones have eyes. The normal ones did not."*** ⚑ **The sighted variant has no name.** It is the specimen [[Flux]] needs for the transformation project, per the DM: *"especially this one since this one has eyes and the normal ones do not."* All six thralls in the S11 Dead Zone ambush were this variant.
- **Blind** — hospital dwellers: eyeless, hunting by **sound and heat**. Immune to sight-based tricks, but **back away from strong heat/light** (Valerian's Daylight). Sunlight imposes disadvantage on vampires generally; the blind ones merely "feel the heat." S11 establishes this as **the normal/default variant**.
- **"A massive thrall"** — see [[The Massive Thrall]]. Appeared at [[The Cloaked Figure]]'s side in S11, larger than any of the six fought, and **never acted**. ⚑ Distinct type or just a big specimen? Unknown.

## Combat Capabilities

**S02, observed:**
- Multiattack (bite + claw); hits observed at 7–8, 14, 17, and 20 damage (2d8/2d10-class)
- One attack carries a **Strength save rider → knocked prone** (DC unstated; Valerian failed on an 11)
- Wall-jumping mobility (leapt off a wall at Barrett)
- Silver wounds **stop their self-healing** (implying they otherwise regenerate)

**S11, the DM's own statblock disclosures — the biggest single addition to this page:**
- **HP ~86.** [[Vega Bloodroot]] asked Thrall D's maximum and was told **86**. Consistent with [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s *"these things that, uh, have like 80 fucking health or some shit."*
- 🩸 **THE "BLOOD TRAIT" / FRENZY.** Below **50 HP** a beast thrall **enters a frenzy**. DM, triggering it live on Thrall C at 42 HP: *"it's cool because I've been wanting to try this out… When reduced below 50 HP, which he's at 42 now, he enters a frenzy… this is a blood trait that they have. It's basically like a Barbarian's Rage. It turns all— it gives advantage on hits."* ⚑ Confirm **"blood trait"** is the DM's actual term and not a mishearing.
- 🧱 **DOUBLE DAMAGE TO STRUCTURES.** DM: *"the Beast Thrall does deal double damage to structures, doors, and other fortifications."* Relevant to any siege or fortified-position scenario — including [[The Walled Base (Museum-Castle)]].
- ☀️ **RADIANT DAMAGE IS DOUBLED AGAINST THEM.** Applied consistently by the DM all session and **the decisive mechanic of the S11 fight** — [[Valerian Hellebore]] alone put **158 radiant** into it across three Moonbeam ticks (31→62, 34→68, 14→28). See [[House Rules & Rulings]].
- **They climb and fight from elevation** — wall-scaling confirmed again (one was grappled *on the side of a building* near the top), plus rooftop leaps and charges through doorways.
- 🧠 **They can be commanded to HERD.** DM: *"They don't attack like animals. They are moving with a purpose, circling, flanking, driving you into position."* Under a handler they execute tactics, not instinct.
- **Damage output (S11):** hits observed at 7, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18 — consistent with the S02 range.
- **Grapple escape** ran as a Strength **check** against the grappler's **save** ⚑ (contradicts the S09 house rule of a flat save vs DC 8+prof+Str).

## Handlers

Controlled by **Beast Masters** (light-blue magical auras; emerald-vulnerable). [[The Beast Tamer]] "trains and maintains all the vampire beasts" — relationship to the Beast Master class ⚑ pending DM.

## The Revolted Thralls (S16) — a feral population east

🛑 **A distinct, uncontrolled population of beast thralls exists east of [[The Dead Zone]], and they killed their own handler.**

[[Lt. Hargraven]]'s briefing before the party left: rumour holds that feral vampire beast thralls out east are ones that *"turned on their beast tamer, or their beast master, and ran east… tore him to shreds, and then vanished."*

**Confirmed in the field the same session:**

- **Massive claw marks on trees and boulders** the entire road east — a Survival check read them as thrall marks.
- A **half-eaten elk, torn apart with feral brutality.** ⚠️ A **Nature check ruled out vampires** — a *predator* did this. A **History check** then tied the marks and the kill to Hargraven's rumour: **these are the thralls that revolted.**
- Claw marks are also **gouged into the walls inside [[The Volcano Tomb]]**, alongside scattered bones.

⚠️ **They were never seen.** No encounter, no initiative. The party passed through their territory and reached the volcano unopposed.

**What this establishes:** beast thralls are **not permanently bound to a handler** — the control can break, and when it broke these ones killed the handler and left. That is a direct qualification of everything the vault records about [[The Beast Tamer]] and [[The Beast Master]]'s psychic bond. ⚑ **Whether the revolt was spontaneous, caused, or the handler's death came first is unstated.**

⚑ **They hunt by sound** — recorded in the session note; the DM's description of the vampire feeding method (*"it tears open a section of your body and then literally sucks all the blood out within 3 seconds, and you die a bloodless death"*) was likened at the table to *"a monster from A Quiet Place, but… for vampires."*
