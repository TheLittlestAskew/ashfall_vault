## Description

From the boxed text at 01:13:15:

> *"The necromancer is standing over him, robes swirling with dark power, chanting words that scrape against your ears like Broken glass. A massive ritual crystal floats behind him, glowing brighter with every drop of stolen magic."*

He turns on the party *"a smile carving across his face."*

⚑ **No face, species, age, height or voice is otherwise described.** The one physical word attached to him — *"towering"* — belongs to the opening boxed text, where the figure is named as the Beast Master instead.

## What He Was Doing

🛑 **He held [[Valerian Hellebore]] for three weeks and was draining him.** The ritual, per the boxed text:

> *"Val is hanging suspended in a glowing magic circle, chains of shimmering energy wrapped around his arms, his chest, his throat. **Each chain pulses as it siphons away his magic, his immortality, and his very essence.** His skin flickers, mortal one moment, immortal the next, as if it really can't decide what he is anymore."*

⚑ **This is the first on-record statement that [[Valerian Hellebore]] has immortality to lose.**

The ritual focus was **a massive crystal** — a **dark replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]]**, dedicated to **[[Lunara, the Nightbringer]]**, *"necrotic necromancy"* in nature.

## Motive — stated exactly once

> *"Ah, the rescuers. How touching. **But his power will be mine, and when I'm finished, the vampire lords will kneel before a new master.**"*

🛑 **This is the ONLY statement of his motive on the record, and it places him AGAINST the vampire hierarchy rather than inside it.** He intends the vampire lords to kneel to him.

⚑ **It is consistent with an earlier aside** that thrall movements are *"not exactly a sanctioned thing by all the vampires"* — but the two are not explicitly linked.

## 🛑 The Beast Master Problem — UNRESOLVED, HIGHEST-PRIORITY DM QUESTION

The opening boxed text (00:08:58) names the towering figure on the tower top:

> *"Behind them, a towering figure steps into view from the top of the tower. His voice slithers across the stones. **You're too late. His power is already flowing into the circle. The Beast Master raises his hand** and the thralls surge forward in a frenzy."*

**That is the only use of "the Beast Master" in the entire session.** From 00:20:38 onward the antagonist is *"the necromancer"* without exception, and **the necromancer is who the party fights, kills and loots.**

🛑 **[[The Beast Master]] died in S10** — killed by [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s Call Lightning, body reduced to ash.

**Three unresolved possibilities, none of them chosen:**

1. **Leftover wording** in the DM's prepared text, carried over from an earlier draft.
2. **A genuinely new figure** who also holds the Beast Master title.
3. **A return** — the S10 death was not as final as recorded.

⚠️ **The S18 spell-check normalised the spelling only** (`Beastmaster` → `Beast Master`, 70%) and **explicitly did not resolve the referent.** Nothing has been merged. See [[DM Questions — Open]].

## How He Died

**Not by damage — by a control stack.** Four characters, in sequence:

1. **[[Mr. Cat]]** leapt onto his face (Acrobatics **27**, with DM-granted advantage *"for being a cat"*) and clung there → **disadvantage for the rest of the fight.**
2. **[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]** hurled the necromancer's own 20-lb ritual crystal at him with **Catapult at 4th level** — his only 4th-level slot. The DM ruled a person is **not a surface**, which made it a saving throw instead of an automatic stop; the necromancer failed a **disadvantaged Dex save, 15 vs DC 17**. **36 bludgeoning, knocked prone.**
3. **[[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]** cast **Dispel Magic** on the ritual contraption, collapsing the circle.
4. She then ran, jumped, grabbed [[Valerian Hellebore]] (**Acrobatics 21**) and **Dimension Door**ed them both out.

🛑 **He was on 1 hit point when the DM called it** — *"the necromancer is gonna die anyways. He's got 1 HP left."*

⚑ **He rolled a natural 20 on a CON save** earlier in the fight, negating one of Samothy's effects — one of the session's four nat 20s.
⚑ **He does NOT take double radiant damage**, unlike the thralls — Samothy's 1d6 radiant rider was not doubled against him.

### The death text

> *"the necromancer's body hits the floor. The ritual collapses instantly. The runes flicker out. The crystal cracks, splitting light, spilling light across the chamber. The chains shatter, dissolving into drifting sparks. … And somewhere in the distance, far across the land, **something answers the dying pulse of the ritual. A deep resonant tone, a warning, a promise.** The necromancer may be dead, but the power he awakened is That's the end of it, of what I have written."*

🛑 **The final clause is unfinished.** The DM ran out of prepared text mid-sentence. **Whatever the ritual awakened is explicitly left open.**
