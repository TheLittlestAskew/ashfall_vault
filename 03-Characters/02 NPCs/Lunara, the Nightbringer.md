## How She Was Named

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asked whether the shattered ritual crystal's shards still held magic. On an **Arcana 30** the DM granted 8 enchantable shards — and, pulling up his note, named their source:

> *"this crystal is a replica of— oh yeah, it's a replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]], right? But it's the darker version… This crystal is dedicated to **Lunara**, Lunara, who's referred to as **the Nightbringer**."* … *"it's necrotic necromancy that it can be used for."*

⚠️ **She was named in a loot aside, not a story beat** — the DM was answering a mechanical question about shard salvage. ⚑ **He was reading from prepared material**, which means she predates the question.

## What Is Known

- **The ritual crystal was a replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]]** — *"but it's the darker version."*
- **The replica was dedicated to her.**
- **Its magic is necrotic necromancy.**
- She is *"referred to as the Nightbringer"* — ⚑ **"referred to as"** implies the epithet is how others speak of her, not necessarily a self-title.

🛑 **That is the complete record.** Nothing else about her exists in the vault.

## ⚠️ The Sun Shard Symmetry — recorded, not interpreted

The crystal is explicitly a **dark replica of the [[The Sun Shard|Sun Shard]]**. In the same session the Sun Shard was revealed to be **one of a set of three** (*"Because you have 2 of the set of 3"*) — a fact the table did not notice.

⚑ **Candidate reading, deliberately NOT adopted:** that Lunara stands to the dark what the Sun Shard's source stands to the light, and that the campaign is building a paired cosmology.

🛑 **Nothing on the record says this.** The Sun Shard has **never been attributed to a deity at all**. Do not merge, do not infer a pantheon, and do not make her [[Emberos]]'s opposite number without the DM.

## Thematic Note

⚑ Recorded because it is structural rather than speculative: the party spent this session **under a spell that turns darkness into light**, with **radiant damage on every weapon at the table**, and ended it by **shattering a dark replica of the Sun Shard dedicated to a goddess called the Nightbringer**. The campaign's "defiant hope" core stopped being subtext in the same scene that named her.
