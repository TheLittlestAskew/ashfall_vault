# Dr. Wilson

> First seen: [[Session 05 — The Bell of Saint Ardas]]. ⚑ Spelling provisional.

Castle surgeon — exhausted, dark-circled, blood-stained coat, hands trembling "not from age but stress." Received the hospital medical supplies with shaking relief: *"These will save lives."* Paid the party **300 gp + 5 healing potions** (in addition to a 300 gp + 5 greater-healing reward "last time" — the lost session ⚑).

**Location:** castle medical ward · **Status:** alive, overworked.

## S11 — "the doctor" ⚑ IDENTITY UNCONFIRMED

In [[Session 11 — A Delightful Chase]] the DM set the terms of [[Flux]]'s **vampire transformation project** and told the party they could **take a beast thrall body back to "the doctor"** for dissection — *"You can just take the body back with you to the doctor."*

⚑ **"The doctor" is never named on the record.** Dr. Wilson is the only doctor the vault has, and he works out of the castle the party was returning to, so this is the obvious referent — but it is an **inference, not a statement**. Do not treat him as confirmed party to the research until the DM names him.

If it is him, the project puts a **beast thrall corpse — the *eyed* variant specifically** — on his table, with [[Valerian Hellebore]] assisting. The DM's stated terms: dissect and learn the anatomy, *"especially this one since this one has eyes and the normal ones do not"*; it works *"similar to"* Druid Wild Shape; and **"you keep your normal stats."**
