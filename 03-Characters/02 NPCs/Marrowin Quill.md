# Marrowin Quill

> First and only appearance: [[Session 13 — The Cartographer of the Underkeep]] (08/02/2026) — the session is named for him.
> ✅ **The spelling is CONFIRMED, unusually so.** The transcript rendered his name six different ways (`Marvin the Mapmaker`, `Marwin Quill`, `Morrowind Quill`, `Marwen Quill`, `Marrowind`, `Marrowin`), and the DM **spelled it out letter by letter on air** — *"M-A-R-R-O-W-I-N"* — with four players repeating it back correctly.
> ⚑ **The surname `Quill` is NOT covered by that spelling.** It appears only in the earlier misheard renderings. Almost certainly right; worth one line of confirmation from the DM.

## Description

Wiry and pale, fingers stained black with ink, hair sticking out *"as though he's been arguing with it."* He lives behind a door reinforced with iron bands and barricaded from the inside, and he slides **three locks** home behind anyone he admits.

His challenge through the door, before he will open it:

> "If you're enthralled, you can't come in. If you're not enthralled, prove it."

The room beyond overflows with scrolls, quills and half-finished maps. The walls carry his work and, written over and over, a phrase that reads as either a working principle or a compulsion:

> *the walls lie, the veins speak true*

He is direct about wanting them gone, and equally direct that it isn't personal:

> "It's not that I don't like you, but I don't like strangers in my house for longer than they have to be in here."

## The savant problem

🛑 **He can map almost anything without ever having seen it.** This is the single most important thing about him and the campaign has not explained it. He has never been to the catacombs. He drew the party a working route through them anyway, complete with named stages, hazards and a hidden bypass — and **every part of it that has since been walked has been correct.**

⚑ He is also explicit that his maps are **incomplete by nature**, not by error:

> "Sometimes lines are older than others. Some symbols are warnings, some are apologies."

And that the catacombs resist mapping at all:

> "they change, they breathe, they remember"

**S14 vindicated him twice over.** The party found the [[Lantern Spine]] exactly as described, the bypass exactly where he drew it — and the one warning they half-remembered and ignored (*"don't touch the walls"*) turned out to matter.

## History

- **S13 — the consultation.** The party descended a narrow spiral staircase beyond the war room, past walls growing colder with each step and scraps of parchment jammed into the cracks *"as if someone tried to keep the darkness from seeping through."* After the enthralled challenge and a pause, a single ink-stained eye appeared at the door and they were let in.
  He knew why they had come before they said it: *"You're looking for the tunnels beneath the city, the veins. Dangerous business, foolish business, necessary business."*
  He then drew the route, narrating *"in a low rhythmic murmur, half explanation, half confession"* — the **[[Ossuary Approach]]** (*"old kings built this, lined the walls with skulls so their enemies would feel watched"*), the **[[Lantern Spine]]** (*"Vampires added that. Blue flame, cold flame. Don't trust it. It burns without heat, but it hungers"*), the **[[Old Shrine]]**, the **Flooded Gallery** (*"Blood run off from the upper floors. Don't fall in. Things float in there that shouldn't"*), the **[[Watcher's Pit]]**, and the **[[Broken Descent]]** (*"Beast thralls patrol it. They smell fear, they smell hope, they smell everything"*) — and, circled in thick dark ink beneath all of it, **the veins**, *"the arteries of the vampire empire."*
  He also marked the **narrow bypass** near the Ossuary Approach — *"hidden, narrow, useful if you're being hunted."*
  He accepted a potion from **Charlotte** ([[Charlottes-curatives]]), which visibly moved him, and promised [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] a map of the western mountains **within a day**.

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **HOW does he map places he has never been?** Never explained, never asked in play. The most interesting unanswered question attached to him.
- 🛑 **The western mountains map** — promised *"by the time you return,"* within a day. The party has since returned to base (S15) and **nobody has gone back for it.** Three sessions stale.
- ⚑ **`Underkeep` vs `Underdeep`.** He calls himself Cartographer of the **Underkeep**; the DM, out of character, twice called the destination the **Underdeep**, and S14 used **Underdeep** three more times. Two words, or one mishearing. **Unresolved since S13.** See [[DM Questions — Open]].
- ⚑ **What is he afraid of, specifically?** Agoraphobia is stated. The parchment stuffed into the cracks *"to keep the darkness from seeping through"* suggests something more particular, and nobody asked.
- ⚑ **Who is he to Charlotte?** A potion from her *"visibly moved him."* The relationship is unstated.
- ⚑ **He warned about the walls** — the party remembered this only vaguely in S14 (*"Didn't the map guy say not to touch— something about don't touch the walls?"*), and the exact warning is not in the S13 transcript. ⚑ Either it was off-record or it was paraphrased. It nearly mattered.
