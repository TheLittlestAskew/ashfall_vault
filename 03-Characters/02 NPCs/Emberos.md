## What Is Known

- **[[The Volcano Tomb]] was his sanctuary first and a tomb second** — repurposed, not built as a grave.
- The obsidian tablet describes the site as *a burial site sacred to an old fiery deity*. ⚑ The tablet does not name him; the mural does. **The vault treats these as the same deity because the DM's Religion result says "this was a sanctuary of a fire deity" about the same room — but that identification is inference, not a stated equivalence.**
- His tribe *"worshipped fire deities"* — **plural** on the record. ⚑ **Emberos may be one of several.**
- [[Tharos Emberborn]] is associated with him: the hero of the fire-greaves, entombed in the fire god's house.

## Possible Connection to the Pre-Vampire Fire Deity (S15)

⚠️ **Recorded as a candidate link, deliberately not merged.** S15 established *"a **pre-vampire** deity buried beneath the [[Old Shrine]], honoured by an Emberborn"* — currently logged on the [[Campaign Dashboard]] NPC directory as **"The fire deity (unnamed)."**

**S16's Emberos and S15's unnamed fire deity share:** fire, pre-vampire dating, an Emberborn connection, and a burial site.
🛑 **Nothing on the record equates them.** Two separate sites are involved — the [[Old Shrine]] beneath the castle, and a volcano two days east. **Ask the DM before merging.**
