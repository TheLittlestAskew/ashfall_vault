## Description

Emaciated and physically unimpressive. DM: *"Well, that's what the Beast Masters look like— very weak and frail."* The table's read on the portrait the DM posted to party chat was immediate and merciless: "Oh no, it's a sad vampire boy" ([[Barrett Grimmskar]]), "he looks emaciated as fuck" ([[Vega Bloodroot]]), "He just gets really ashy in the sunlight." DM: *"No, it's not Edward. He doesn't sparkle."*

## The Beast Master Lore (S10 — the session's one worldbuilding drop)

Read aloud to the table by [[Vega Bloodroot]] after a **natural-20 Perception**, with the DM confirming that both player *and* character possessed the knowledge:

- Beast Masters are **thin, fragile, physically unimpressive** — they are not muscle and not speed.
- They carry a **rare psychic mutation** that bonds them to **vampire beast thralls**.
- Through the bond they **see through a thrall's eyes**, **hear through its ears**, **issue commands instantly across great distances**, and **coordinate entire packs**.
- Over **decades** the link erodes them: predatory behavior, animal mannerisms, pack mentality, and eventually **no distinction between their own identity and their countless thralls**.
- *"A Beast Master standing alone is vulnerable. A Beast Master with their thralls is Truly Never Alone, or Never Truly Alone."* ⚑ The speaker gives both orderings in the same breath; the session takes its title from the second.

## History

- **S10:** Entered [[The Grand Library of the Veiled Dawn]] on the lower floor mid-battle. Spotted by [[Vega Bloodroot]]'s natural-20 Perception a full round before he could act. **He came alone** — no thralls were ever deployed. [[Valerian Hellebore]], the only PC who could reach him, descended as a spider and dropped Call Lightning on him: Dex save **12** against DC 16, failed. **He died without speaking a line, without taking a turn, and without using a single ability.** Body reduced to ash. Looted by Valerian for the party's **200 gold** total and a **folded note in an unreadable near-hieroglyphic script**.

## ⭐ The S19 Lore Drop — THE FULLEST DESCRIPTION OF THE ROLE IN THE CAMPAIGN

> ⚠️ **THIS DESCRIBES THE ROLE. IT DOES NOT IDENTIFY ANY INDIVIDUAL, AND IT DOES NOT CLOSE THE [[The Beast Tamer|`Beast Tamer`]] QUESTION.** 🛑 **DO NOT MERGE THE PAGES.**

[[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] asked whether a Beast Master is itself a **beast** (he has a spell that cares) and rolled **History 13**. ⭐ **[[Vega Bloodroot]]'s player asked to answer instead — *"Vega should know"* — and the DM agreed: *"Sounds fair."* So this is delivered as Vega's knowledge, to the table.** *(Second time after S10 that this creature's lore comes out of Vega's mouth.)*

> *"Vega would know the Beast Master isn't— it's just a **vampire that's sickly**, right? His— he's **not very strong**, can't, you know, lift a rock and throw it, isn't going to break someone's arm. They're **very frail and weak**, but they have a **telepathic connection with beast thralls**. **Each Beast Master gets 6 thralls to himself that he trains.** Now, if you kill the Beast Master and the thralls are still alive, **they will scatter and leave** because they don't know what to do anymore."*

**And the answer to the actual question asked:**

> *"No, **he's just a vampire**."*

**What is NEW here, against the S10 lore:**

| Fact | Status |
|---|---|
| 🆕 **A Beast Master is allotted SIX thralls, and he TRAINS them** | **First statement of a number and of training anywhere in the campaign** |
| 🆕 **Kill the master and surviving thralls SCATTER AND LEAVE** — *"they don't know what to do anymore"* | **First statement of what happens to the pack. ⚔️ This is directly actionable tactical intelligence and the party has not used it** |
| 🆕 **A Beast Master is NOT a beast — he is a vampire** | **Settles a mechanical question ([[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s spell) and a taxonomic one** |
| ✅ **Frail, weak, physically unimpressive; telepathically bonded to thralls** | **Confirms the S10 lore independently, eight sessions later** |

🛑 **THE ARITHMETIC NOBODY DID.** The lore says **six** thralls per master. [[Lt. Hargraven]]'s scouts counted **five** at the nest. ⚠️ **Nobody at the table reconciled it out loud.** Either one thrall was unaccounted for, the scouts were wrong, or there is no master at that nest.

⚑ **Note the friction with S16/S18.** S16 recorded thralls that **revolted against their handler and fled east** — so the bond can break. S18 recorded a necromancer whose thralls dispersed because *"their contract is over."* **S19 says they scatter because they cannot think for themselves.** 🛑 **Three different accounts of what unbinds a thrall, from three different sessions. NOT reconciled here.**

## 🛑 The S18 Reappearance — UNRESOLVED

**In S18's opening boxed text (00:08:58), the towering figure on top of [[The Cliffside Tower]] is named as the Beast Master:**

> *"Behind them, a towering figure steps into view from the top of the tower. His voice slithers across the stones. **You're too late. His power is already flowing into the circle. The Beast Master raises his hand** and the thralls surge forward in a frenzy."*

🛑 **That is the ONLY use of the name in the entire session.** From 00:20:38 onward the antagonist is *"the necromancer"* — roughly **thirty times**, including in **both** later boxed-text reads — and **[[The Necromancer]] is who the party fights, kills at 1 HP, and loots.**

**Three possibilities, none of them chosen:**

| Reading | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Leftover wording** in the DM's prepared text | The DM was reading prepared boxed text and **ran out of it entirely by the session's end** (*"That's the end of it, of what I have written"*) | — |
| **A genuinely new figure** holding the same title | *"Beast Master"* is a **role**, not a name (established S10) — there can be more than one; ⚑ **[[Hampshire]]'s Beast Master** (S02) is a separate, still-unaccounted-for individual | The figure who raises his hand is in the same position as the necromancer, one boxed text apart |
| **A return** | — | 🛑 **He died on-screen in S10**, body reduced to ash, and nothing in S18 hints at a resurrection |

⚠️ **The S18 spell-check normalised the spelling only** — `Beastmaster` → `Beast Master`, **70% confidence** — and **explicitly declined to resolve the referent.**

⚑ **One detail worth recording without interpreting it:** when the necromancer died, the DM said the surviving thralls fled because *"**Their contract is over**."* **"Contract" is not the psychic-bond language of the S10 Beast Master lore** — but it is one word, in one line, and nothing else in the session supports reading further into it.
