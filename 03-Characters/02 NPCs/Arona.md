## The Complete Record

Two lines. That is all of it.

> *"I'll be fine. I can call on **Arona**. Why are you staring at me?"* — [[Valerian Hellebore]]

> *"You mean calling in **Arona** and summoning Blast?"* — echoed back by another speaker

🛑 **No referent is ever given, and nobody asks.** The party is preoccupied with the fact that Val looks and sounds different; the name goes by without a follow-up question.

## Why It Matters

[[Valerian Hellebore]] came back from three weeks in [[The Cliffside Tower]] **casting Eldritch Blast — a Warlock cantrip — as a Circle of the Land druid.** Asked how, he credits *"calling on Arona."* Pressed on why his magic is different, he says: *"My magic's no different."*

🛑 **[[Vega Bloodroot]]'s natural 20 Insight clears him:** *"nothing is wrong with Val other than he looks and sounds different, but he is Val."* The DM adds that *"he looks like he's genuinely confused, and he's telling the truth, as far as he knows."*

⚠️ **So Arona is a name he uses fluently for something he does not know is new about him.** That is the whole shape of the problem.

## ⚠️ The `Book of Varona` Question — flagged, NOT resolved

S16's glossary records *"someone has the **Book of Varona**"*, and [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] claimed *"I have the book that, um, only I can read."*

**`Arona` and `Varona` are one leading consonant apart.**

🛑 **This is deliberately NOT being treated as a link.** Both stand verbatim in their own sessions. The arguments in each direction:

| For a link | Against a link |
|---|---|
| One consonant apart — a classic ASR ambiguity | The two are two sessions and one captivity apart, with no narrative bridge |
| Both are unexplained proper nouns attached to unexplained magic | ⚑ **Nobody in S18 mentions a book**, and nobody in S16 mentioned a patron |
| A book only one person can read is a plausible source for a borrowed cantrip | ⚠️ **`Book of Varona` is itself ⚑ unconfirmed** — linking two unconfirmed hearings compounds the error |
