## The Complete Record

Two lines. That is all of it.

> *"I'll be fine. I can call on **Arona**. Why are you staring at me?"* — [[Valerian Hellebore]]

> *"You mean calling in **Arona** and summoning Blast?"* — echoed back by another speaker

🛑 **No referent is ever given, and nobody asks.** The party is preoccupied with the fact that Val looks and sounds different; the name goes by without a follow-up question.

## Why It Matters

[[Valerian Hellebore]] came back from three weeks in [[The Cliffside Tower]] **casting Eldritch Blast — a Warlock cantrip — as a Circle of the Land druid.** Asked how, he credits *"calling on Arona."* Pressed on why his magic is different, he says: *"My magic's no different."*

🛑 **[[Vega Bloodroot]]'s natural 20 Insight clears him:** *"nothing is wrong with Val other than he looks and sounds different, but he is Val."* The DM adds that *"he looks like he's genuinely confused, and he's telling the truth, as far as he knows."*

⚠️ **So Arona is a name he uses fluently for something he does not know is new about him.** That is the whole shape of the problem.

## ⭐ S20 — Auriona (the DM names the patron)

In the heart room of [[The Ruined Neighborhood (Vampire Nest)]], with nobody able to work out how to kill the giant flesh heart, [[Valerian Hellebore]] cast **Contact Other Plane** (⚑ the DM's d20 result was never stated: *"Big sus"*). The DM:

> *"So the area lights up bright yellow sunlight. It warms your skin. The psychic pain of the heart itself has died down, so no one is doubled over in pain anymore. Um, And then before you stands one of the prettiest women you've ever seen. Her name is Aurorna— Aurorna— Auriona. Sorry, my bad. Auriona is her name. She is the goddess of sun for the area. She is the patron for, uh, Val. She's in a little bit of a bad mood because you caught her when she was doing her hair, so she's not pleased that you have contacted her but is willing to answer your questions anyways."*

**The exchange, verbatim:**

> *"Apologies, Lady Arona. Beautiful as ever, even with your hair. Not fully done."* — [[Valerian Hellebore]]
> *"Yes, flattery gets you everywhere. What do you need?"* — Auriona
> *"Like you would any heart. Stab your sword through it over and over till it starts— stops beating."* — Auriona
> *"Just, uh, be careful because it could summon vampires or other creatures. We— with these hearts, they're known to go feral."* — Auriona

⚠️ **The garbled middle line (*"I don't know how I feel about this, but contact the imp"*) is NOT attributed to her** (S20 flags §3).

**What S20 establishes:**

- ✅ **She is a goddess, *"the goddess of sun for the area"*, and [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s patron.** This answers S18's "what is Arona?" and S19's "who is his patron?"
- ✅ **Her presence suppressed the heart's psychic pressure** (*"no one is doubled over in pain anymore"*).
- ✅ **Personality on the record:** vain, annoyed at being caught *"doing her hair"*, responsive to flattery, helpful anyway. Val dismissed her politely to avoid her *"getting upset at me."*
- ⚑ The heart was then killed with light: Daylight, Blinding Radiance, and Val's **"Aurona's Blessing"** (⚑ name held, spell-check row 29) for **168 radiant**, followed by a natural-20 CON save and Val's *"I truly am Morona's chosen"* (⚑ row 30 held).
- 🛑 **How and when the pact formed is still unstated.**

## ⚠️ The `Book of Varona` Question — flagged, NOT resolved

S16's glossary records *"someone has the **Book of Varona**"*, and [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] claimed *"I have the book that, um, only I can read."*

**`Arona` and `Varona` are one leading consonant apart.**

🛑 **This is deliberately NOT being treated as a link.** Both stand verbatim in their own sessions. The arguments in each direction:

| For a link | Against a link |
|---|---|
| One consonant apart — a classic ASR ambiguity | The two are two sessions and one captivity apart, with no narrative bridge |
| Both are unexplained proper nouns attached to unexplained magic | ⚑ **Nobody in S18 mentions a book**, and nobody in S16 mentioned a patron |
| A book only one person can read is a plausible source for a borrowed cantrip | ⚠️ **`Book of Varona` is itself ⚑ unconfirmed** — linking two unconfirmed hearings compounds the error |
