## What Is Known

Everything below is from the S16 obsidian tablet, [[Lt. Hargraven]]'s decoding of it, and the murals inside [[The Volcano Tomb]]. **Nothing else is known.**

- He is a **pre-vampire hero** — [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] is the one who noticed what that implies: *"doesn't it also say in there that he's a pre-vampire hero? So what was there before the vampires that they needed heroes for?"* That question opened the **First Vampire War** thread.
- Hargraven: *"Tharos Emberborn, he was the wielder of the Grieveflame, the Flame Greaves. He was the legendary hero that helped with the Vampire War."*
- The tomb murals show him in sequence: **vampires descending on a city like a plague**, then *"Tharos Emberborn, greaves blazing, charging into battle"*, then **the Sunfire blade piercing the vampire lord** atop a stone altar with the blade left embedded **as a seal**, and finally **Emberborn leading the last stand inside this very mountain**.
- The tablet's hidden compartment carried a warning: ***"only those who bleed fire may walk the path of— of Emberborn."***

## The Warning, and [[Vega Bloodroot]]

🛑 **The DM tied the warning to Vega's sword unprompted** — *"this ties directly into the sword that Vega has."*

She pulled the burning longsword in S15 on three Strength saves after **her palm split open and her hand locked to the hilt**. She bled on it. The warning says only those who bleed fire may walk the path of Emberborn.

⚑ **She said nothing about this at the table.** The connection is stated by the DM, not tested in play. See thread 75 on the [[Campaign Dashboard]] — *is "Emberborn" an individual, or a title?* — which has been open since S15 and is **still not answered.**

## Open Threads

- 🛑 **His name.** Four renderings, two sessions, no glossary entry to arbitrate. **DM question.**
- 🛑 **The Grieveflame / the Flame Greaves** — the party's stated objective for all of S16. ⚑ **One name or two?** **Not retrieved** — the tomb was never entered.
- ⚠️ **Is "Emberborn" a name or a title?** Open since S15.
- ⚠️ **The Sunfire blade** — ⚑ possibly the same blade [[Vega Bloodroot]] pulled in S15, which was also unnamed and also embedded. **Ask the DM.**
- ⚠️ **Which Vampire War did he fight?** He is *pre-vampire* and fought "the Vampire War" — and S16 establishes the current one is the **Second**. The murals show him sealing a vampire lord, which reads as the **First**.
