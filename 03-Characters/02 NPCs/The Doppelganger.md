## Description / Appearance

- Identical to [[Valerian Hellebore]] *"to the T"*, **including clothing** (S16).
- **Reacts to silver on bare skin.** [[Barrett Grimmskar]] pressed a **silver bullet** to him — blocked first by the **Cloak of Many Fashions**, then, once it touched skin, **there was a reaction**. Barrett tested it by pressing rather than firing because *"he didn't want to shoot it into you and be wrong."* (S16)
- Wore the **Cloak of Many Fashions**. ⚠️ The vault records this item as *Cloak of Fashion* elsewhere — see [[Names & Terms]].
- **His pockets were empty.** Searched by [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]]: *"You go through his pockets and there's nothing in them."* (S16)

## Personality & Method

- **He never tells a deliberate lie under Zone of Truth and still gives up nothing.** He identified the loophole out loud within seconds — *"It just guards from deception… If everyone's lying, I don't have to answer shit."* — and then answered questions with questions for forty minutes.
- Best evasion of the session, and technically true: *"When did you see Val for the first time?"* → *"When I— the same day I looked into a mirror for the first time."*
- Openly contemptuous of the ambush: *"It's cute that you guys all planned this together."*
- **Actively steered the party away from the ridgeline figure** — by his own admission (S16).
- Under real pressure he broke into pleading — *"You're freaking me out. Can you just please get off of me?"* — which the party could not distinguish from Valerian.

## History

- **S15 (retroactive):** ⚑ **Already in place.** Per the S16 above-table debrief, everything "Valerian" said in character in S15 belongs to this impostor — including the hostility toward [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie]], confirmed as deliberate impostor characterisation. 🛑 **The S15 note has NOT been retro-edited and should not be** (`No Session Contamination`); a disambiguation note was appended instead.
- **S16 — suspicion:** [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] catches him twenty minutes in on a mundane tell — **elves don't sleep, and he slept** (*"he has this look in his eye where he actually looks sleepy"*). Two Insight checks agree. He blocks **Detect Thoughts** twice.
- **S16 — the ambush:** In the chain-room doorway the party springs a plan coordinated by private message all session. **Entangle** ([[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]]) → **grapple** ([[Barrett Grimmskar]]) → **[[Vega Bloodroot]] holds him single-handedly** → **Zone of Truth** ([[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]). He passes the save; **[[Flux]]'s Silvery Barbs forces a reroll and he fails.**
- **S16 — the interrogation:** Forty minutes of lawyerly evasion. [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] holds a **silver-tipped dagger** on him; [[Eiluned "Lulu" Denulie]] casts **Pins and Needles** and shuts down his left arm, then a leg, then **Inflict Wounds**; [[Barrett Grimmskar]] presses the **silver bullet** to his skin. [[Vega Bloodroot]] objects: *"I was told we were just gonna restrain you."*
- **S16 — the reveal:** [[Zelda "Z" Whipper]] lands **Detect Thoughts** on the fourth attempt. [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] uses **Hunter's Sense**: *"He's a changeling."* Zelda deep-dives his memory and finds **a tower and a tied-up Val** — the last two images.
- **S16 — the confession:** Weeks embedded, a master who made and pays him, and a goal of **chaos**. He does **not** give up the tower's location.
- **S16 — session end:** 🛑 **Grappled, kneecapped, and unresolved.** Kill / keep / bribe were all argued and nothing was decided. **S17 opens inside this standoff.**
