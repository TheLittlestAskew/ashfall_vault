## Build Notes

- **Radiant specialist** — and radiant is doubled vs. vampires (house rule): every Moonbeam/Starry Wisp hit this session killed.
- **Spiny Shield** (⚑ spelling/source unconfirmed): reaction; ethereal spike barrier; reduces incoming damage by 2d4 and reflects the same to the attacker.
- Won the boot-camp **Perma Inspiration** ("teacher's pet" — seated alone at graduation); spent it S01 on a DM-prompted d20 (rerolled a 2 into a 4 — "missed the vital organs").
- Already teasing **Call Lightning** at level 5: "Do you know what happens to a vampire when it's hit by lightning? The same thing that happens to everything else."

## Key Events

- **S15: CALL LIGHTNING, EVERY ROUND — and the worst he has treated anyone.** Rolled a **natural 20 on Nature (29)** to identify the [[Boilborn]] and learned **nothing** (*"like nothing you've ever seen or heard of before"*). Cast **Call Lightning at 5th level** and re-triggered it every single round for the rest of the fight, targeting pairs — the bulk of the party's damage. Also **Shillelagh**, **Stone Shape** (plotting to cut a gem door out of the mine wall and have [[Vega Bloodroot]] kick it off its hinges), and a **vomit attack** the DM allowed for 1d6 acid on the rule of gross.
  ⚠️ **His treatment of [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] escalated sharply.** He proposed testing the shrine sigils with **her** blood rather than his own; suggested she **walk into the unexplored corridor first** (*"maybe Lulu should go first"*), which Chase then called out at his own table; announced he would *"start picking over her corpse"* when she dropped; declined to let anyone spend a potion on her (*"it's fine, it's her time"*); and, when [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] healed her anyway, clarified he cast Cure Wounds *"with my foot on her."* He also rounded on Zelda at the session's end — *"Deanna died and you could have teleported us this whole time?"* — and apologised once [[Flux]] explained anchoring.
- **S14: DEPTH CHARGE, AND THE OFFER TO BRING HER BACK.** Cast **Depth Charge** twice — **25 thunder** killing Mimic A outright (*"That's the biggest fucking echo you've ever heard in an enclosed space"*), then **32 at 4th level** on the water elemental after getting the DM to formally rule that a creature made of water counts as submerged in one: *"Yeah, water is wet, as a matter of fact."* **Wild Shaped into a bat** to cross the rope bridge, and kept talking under 2024 rules.
  ⭐ **He is the one who offered a way back for [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]** — **Reincarnate**, 1,000gp of rare oils within 10 days, random ancestry. Still untaken.
  ⚠️ **He disliked [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] on sight**, and Chase played the reason out loud: Val feels **threatened at being replaced**, *"he doesn't like the idea of not being needed or wanted. Especially since he has no parents anymore."*
- **S13: he found the torch, and he was the only one the echo caught.** Spotted the hidden alcove (Perception 16) and pulled the **cold-flame torch** free on a **Constitution check of 19** as a door snapped shut on his arm — it sheds a 25-foot circle of light **through the vampires' magical darkness**. ⚠️ He was also the **only PC to fail the Constitution save** against the Bonewraiths' *memory echo* — the sound of ancient workers arguing about the horrors below, moments before their deaths.
- **S11: 158 RADIANT, AND THE LINE OF THE SESSION.** The single largest damage contribution of the night, and the fight's decisive mechanic. He dropped **Moonbeam at 5th level** on Thrall C — *"because I heard these things that, uh, have like 80 fucking health or some shit"* — and then walked it around the battlefield for three rounds: **31 radiant doubled to 62**, **34 doubled to 68** (**Thrall C dies**), and **14 doubled to 28** onto Thrall F. *"Haha, bitch! Radiant beams from my holy face, motherfucker."* / **"Fry, motherfucker! Put the fries in the bag, bitch!"** — the session's most-quoted line. Announced for the record that he had been **one turn away from polymorphing into a T-Rex** and talked himself out of it on action economy. Held concentration through two hits on **War Caster** advantage, and answered Thrall C's bite with a reaction **Spiny Shield** ⚑ — damage cut to **8** and **6 piercing reflected**: *"deal 6 back to the little bitch who bit me."* / *"Okay, damn, reactions are great."* Later **Barkskin** (AC 17). **Session profanity champion at 22**, his best night since the S08 record of 40, and **the first player at the table past 200 campaign-wide.** Two beats worth keeping: he **volunteered unprompted** to help [[Flux]] with the vampire-transformation research — *"If it speeds it up for her, I— Val would be more than willing to help"* — and when [[Vega Bloodroot]] stole [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]'s kill, he covered for her with *"That's fine, I was gonna steal it if you didn't."* The S10 kill-credit volatility has flipped into generosity in one session.
- **S10: KILLED THE BEAST MASTER — from inside a spider.** The session's decisive act, and it was his alone. In **Wild Shape as a spider** (small enough that nobody looked twice, and the only PC who could physically reach the lower floor), he called it out — *"I'm one of the few people that can reach him, so fuck it, we ball"* — and dropped **Call Lightning** on [[The Beast Master]]. Dex save **12** vs DC 16, **failed. Dead**, body reduced to ash, without ever speaking, acting, or deploying a thrall. Then descended three floors toward the ashes and zapped **Carl Senior and Shithead** on the way (both failed, "funnily enough, they both rolled the same") for **32 — Shithead dies too**. **Two kills, the most at the table.** His opening Call Lightning, by contrast, is the campaign's saddest roll: **6 damage out of a possible 40** (2, 2, 1, 1) — *"I'm crying. I didn't even know spiders could cry."* **Looted the ashes**: the party's **200 gold** and a **folded note in an unreadable near-hieroglyphic script**, which he pocketed to find someone who can read it. Immediate, public tactical regret: *"I feel like we probably should have interrogated him. You're just here for the bookshelf? Because 2024 rules, I can talk, bitches."* Volatile about kill-credit in both directions — took Shithead out from under [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] and then redirected her grievance at [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] ("does 5 damage and kills, huh?"), before conceding: "Girl, welcome to my fucking life."
- **S09 (⚠ partial recording): ⚡ CALL LIGHTNING, AT LAST.** Two archived casts (21, 16) in the library battle — the threat he's been making since S01 ("the same thing that happens to everything else"), finally executed. Indoors, no less ⚑ ruling unrecorded. Initiative 19 (2nd).
- **S08:** Read the **soul-trap seal** in five seconds flat (Arcana 18). Backed a monk off with a growl and a gold eye-flash (intimidation dirty 20). Survived an investigation rollercoaster — **two nat 1s** ("my dyslexia is really bad right now and I can't read"), saved once by Barrett's cough — then found the green book via a dust-based "nature check" workaround. Capped the session with a **NAT-20 STARRY WISP (28 crit)** that blew the restricted-section doors open. Also: **new single-session profanity record (40)**, set in a library that demanded silence. "Hi God, it's me Valerian. Can you help a bitch out?"
