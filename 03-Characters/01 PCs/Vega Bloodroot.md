## Backstory

> From Vega's character sheet (pre-campaign). Names are kept as plain text until/unless these people and places appear in play.

Born beneath the towering trees of the **Thornshade Forest**, in a moss-roofed wooden cottage with smoke forever curling from its stone chimney. Twin sister of **Varro**; the two were raised by their father **Harkon Bloodroot**, a quiet, steady man who bore his grief with stoic resolve, after their mother **Graza** — a fierce half-orc hunter — was devoured by a forest-dwelling monstrosity when the twins were barely old enough to walk.

Despite that shadow, her childhood in the small, tightly-knit village of **Mossmere** was a happy one: friends, scraped knees, warm fires, and Vega always the bold one — climbing trees, leading games, testing limits. Though small for a half-orc, she never lacked courage, and she learned early to turn her stature into a weapon, playing the underestimated "helpless" role to bait bandits, slavers, and raiders before showing them the fury they never saw coming.

When she came of age she joined the Mossmere militia — not for pride, but to protect: the children, the hearths, the land where her mother fell. In battle she fought like a storm, wild and unrelenting; the village elders said her love for her people was what fueled her strength.

**The Siege of Hollowbarrow.** A horde of aberrant creatures spilled from the rotted heart of the forest. Vega fought day and night beside Varro and the militia, and they won — but at great cost. She survived; the images did not leave her. Eyes like black pits, screams, the ease with which her axe sank into flesh, the smell of burning blood. The nightmares still come.

In the aftermath, blame circled. During a joint effort with an adventuring party, Vega made a call she believed would save more lives; it cost the life of a party member. The party's leader, **Kael Virethorn**, named her an enemy for it and still hunts her, convinced she is reckless and dangerous.

Around the same time, an estranged great-uncle from a distant clan died and bequeathed her the **greataxe** she still carries — worn but enchanted, it felt as though it had always belonged in her hands. It is her legacy, a tie to her ancestors and to a destiny beyond herself.

And then, in a glade where moonlight pooled like water, she met a **demigod**: a being of radiant presence and unknowable intent. It tested her in spirit, not in combat. She emerged changed, and never speaks of what passed between them.

**Personality:** small but mighty; a dry sense of humor and a deep love for underdogs and outcasts. **True Neutral**, raised to a modest life. **Bonds:** her ties to Mossmere and to Varro are unshakable; the greataxe binds her to her ancestors. **Flaws:** guilt from the siege makes her question her own judgment, and her pride can goad her into fights when she's underestimated.

- **S17 — cruelty, performed calmly, and not regretted.** The thousand-cuts interrogation is the coldest thing she has done, and she chose the second cut's placement *along the path of its tear*. ⚠️ **No hesitation in the moment and no remorse afterwards.** Read against S16, where she was the one voice objecting to violence against this same prisoner, **this is a real hardening**.

- **S17 — the line she would not cross was her friend's face.** She could not land the killing blow, and said why out of character: *"this character is tied to you and I don't want to kill you."* ⚑ She will torture a thing wearing [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s face but not kill a thing wearing Val's. **The distinction is the player's attachment, and she named it aloud.**

- **S17 — she noticed her own blank spaces.** Asked for something meaningful to offer the trial, she came up empty: *"I don't really have a very detailed backstory for Vega."* ⚑ She solved it by **making a promise about the future instead of drawing on a past** — which may be the most in-character answer available to her.

- **S16 — she wakes up tactical.** [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] shakes her awake on watch and before she is properly conscious she is asking *"to your left or to your right or towards like above… have they seen you?"* No grogginess, no "what?", straight to threat geometry. - **S16 — the job, redefined as care.** Nobody had to ask twice who walked the trap floor first. But the notable part is what she did *after* surviving it: she didn't just cross, she **mapped three-quarters of the pressure plates, walked back, and briefed the party on where to put their feet** — *"I'll explain to everybody what I see… or they could follow my path if they need to."* Going first has always been hers. **Turning it into information for other people is new.**

When it turned to needles and a bullet she said the only objection in the room — *"I was told we were just gonna restrain you"* — **and nobody engaged with it.** She is the physical instrument of an interrogation she never endorsed. This is the first real gap between Vega and her party.
- **S16 — she catches, and does not squeeze.** ⭐ The signature moment of her session is a **natural 20 to grab a fly out of the air blind**, and then **not crushing it**. Her own line: *"when I finally got proof, I still didn't squeeze."* Set against S10's spit on the corpse, this is the same instinct pointed the other way — she supplies her own witness, and this time what she wants witnessed is restraint.
- **S16 — "I am the fist," heard as a complaint for the first time.** S02's proud self-assessment comes back inverted: *"I am the fist. I have never once been the one who decides where it lands. I am starting to think that was the arrangement all along, and that nobody ever asked me if I agreed to it."* 🛑 **The party role she named for herself in session two is now the thing she is starting to resent.** Watch this.
- **S16 — her only session with zero attack rolls.** Zero weapon damage. Her entire contribution was walking a trap floor, mapping it, and holding a prisoner nobody else could hold. ⚠️ **Her role is shifting from damage to control**, and it happened in a session with no combat in it at all.
- **S16 — she keeps the sword to herself, again.** The DM tied the tomb's warning — *"only those who bleed fire may walk the path of Emberborn"* — **directly and unprompted to her sword**, in front of everyone. She said nothing: *"I did not say anything. I don't need to say everything."* Consistent with S15, where she sheathed the relic and told the party none of them could have it.
