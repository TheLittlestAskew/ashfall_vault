## Backstory

> From Vega's character sheet (pre-campaign). Names are kept as plain text until/unless these people and places appear in play.

Born beneath the towering trees of the **Thornshade Forest**, in a moss-roofed wooden cottage with smoke forever curling from its stone chimney. Twin sister of **Varro**; the two were raised by their father **Harkon Bloodroot**, a quiet, steady man who bore his grief with stoic resolve, after their mother **Graza** — a fierce half-orc hunter — was devoured by a forest-dwelling monstrosity when the twins were barely old enough to walk.

Despite that shadow, her childhood in the small, tightly-knit village of **Mossmere** was a happy one: friends, scraped knees, warm fires, and Vega always the bold one — climbing trees, leading games, testing limits. Though small for a half-orc, she never lacked courage, and she learned early to turn her stature into a weapon, playing the underestimated "helpless" role to bait bandits, slavers, and raiders before showing them the fury they never saw coming.

When she came of age she joined the Mossmere militia — not for pride, but to protect: the children, the hearths, the land where her mother fell. In battle she fought like a storm, wild and unrelenting; the village elders said her love for her people was what fueled her strength.

**The Siege of Hollowbarrow.** A horde of aberrant creatures spilled from the rotted heart of the forest. Vega fought day and night beside Varro and the militia, and they won — but at great cost. She survived; the images did not leave her. Eyes like black pits, screams, the ease with which her axe sank into flesh, the smell of burning blood. The nightmares still come.

In the aftermath, blame circled. During a joint effort with an adventuring party, Vega made a call she believed would save more lives; it cost the life of a party member. The party's leader, **Kael Virethorn**, named her an enemy for it and still hunts her, convinced she is reckless and dangerous.

Around the same time, an estranged great-uncle from a distant clan died and bequeathed her the **greataxe** she still carries — worn but enchanted, it felt as though it had always belonged in her hands. It is her legacy, a tie to her ancestors and to a destiny beyond herself.

And then, in a glade where moonlight pooled like water, she met a **demigod**: a being of radiant presence and unknowable intent. It tested her in spirit, not in combat. She emerged changed, and never speaks of what passed between them.

**Personality:** small but mighty; a dry sense of humor and a deep love for underdogs and outcasts. **True Neutral**, raised to a modest life. **Bonds:** her ties to Mossmere and to Varro are unshakable; the greataxe binds her to her ancestors. **Flaws:** guilt from the siege makes her question her own judgment, and her pride can goad her into fights when she's underestimated.

## Inner Life & Evolution

- **S01 — persona crystallized:** *"the Regina George of Barbarians"* — sarcastic alpha confidence, declared at the table after she flipped off [[The Beast Tamer]] with both hands (he flipped her off back). Player: "This is my personality now as a character. I hope you guys are ready."
- **S01 — line in the sand:** offered the "deal with the devil" house rule and refused: "I'm not dealing with the devil until it becomes entirely necessary." The DM is "so excited because someone's gonna have to at some point." Watch this.
- First blood taken in stride — quipped through her first kill ("Not today, Satan") but registered being drenched in vampire blood ("Oh gross, can't wait to take a shower").
- **S02 — the quiet caretaker under the sass:** took over Hampshire's body-disposal unprompted — staked the dead, piled them for the pyre, no speeches ("It needed doing"). Said nothing to Valerian in his parents' doorway; some things don't need the Regina George voice.
- **S02 — record swing:** 47 damage on a reckless nat-20 crit, narrated as full liquefaction ("there's somebody else still alive on this battlefield… lock eyes with him and just kind of smile"). Self-identified the party role: "That would be me for sure. **I am the fist.**"
- **S10 — the one who knows.** For the first time she is the party's *lore source*, not its blunt instrument: a natural-20 Perception surfaces [[The Beast Master]], the DM confirms both player and character have the knowledge, and she reads the whole of it aloud to the table — immediately and completely, holding nothing back for leverage. The Regina George voice doesn't show up for it.
- **S10 — witness as the point.** She kills the last enemy in the room and there is **nobody left alive to see it**, so she spits on the corpse. That's the whole gesture: supplying, herself, the witness the moment lacked. It rhymes directly with the S02 pyre detail — the unglamorous thing nobody's watching still gets done, and it still counts.
- **S10 — taunt, then own it.** "bring it on, whatever, you can't touch me" → immediate natural-20 crit to the face → "well, shit, you touched me. Goddamn, that was a lucky shot." No sulking, no retcon. The pride that goads her into fights (her sheet's stated flaw) is now openly a bit she's in on.
- **S10 — generosity in the kill economy.** When [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] loses a kill to [[Valerian Hellebore]] and says so, Vega defuses it by volunteering her own humiliation: she went **six sessions without a single kill**. She spent S03 furious about exactly that. She now spends it as currency to cool someone else down.
- **S11 — flow state, and she calls it dancing.** Asked by the DM to describe the axe kill on Thrall D, she gives the clearest interior moment the campaign has recorded for her: *"it would just look so fluid… she's hit flow state… like translating ballet to fighting. It's graceful. A continued movement, 1 slash, 2 slash, 3 slash"* — the last stroke bringing the axe over her head and straight down, *"splitting him open a little bit… She's dancing pretty much."* She reads the cause as being **outdoors and back in her element** after a session sealed inside a library, and as **familiarity** — she's fought these things enough now. This is the first time her competence is described as *beautiful* rather than as force.
- **S11 — she puts the weapon down.** Two rounds after the most technically perfect axe kill she has ever narrated, she **stows the greataxe mid-combat** and finishes the fight bare-knuckle, unprompted and with no mechanical advantage in it: *"She doesn't even want to use the axe anymore… She's given in fully to the beast."* The greataxe is her **inheritance** — the great-uncle's legacy weapon that "felt as though it had always belonged in her hands." She set it aside for her fists because it suited what she is now. **Both halves are hers: the discipline and the surrender.**
- **S11 — the bear, named out loud.** *"she's a bear, so she's just fucking handing it to him. With her fists."* Ten sessions in, the animal aspect behind Path of the Wild Heart finally gets stated. It reframes the S02 self-assessment — *"I am the fist"* — as something she's been circling the whole time.
- **S11 — she roars first.** Before Thrall F could swing at her she asked to **bear-roar him into not being able to hit her** — an Intimidation check spent pre-emptively, as a *reaction to an incoming attack*, which is not how the roll works and which the DM let her try anyway. She rolled **8** and ate 15 damage. *"It would have been beautiful."* The instinct is pure Vega: make the thing afraid of you before it touches you.
- **S11 — the apology.** She steals [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]]'s kill on Thrall A after Zelda had ground ~90 damage into it all night, and **says so immediately, unprompted, out loud**: *"God damn it, I didn't mean to steal a kill from somebody else. I just wanted to throw another punch."* S03 Vega was furious about having no kills; S10 Vega spent her own humiliation to cool Zelda down; S11 Vega owns the offence in the same breath she commits it. The kill economy has become something she has manners about.
- **S11 — softness, declared as a plan.** Her entire downtime declaration — the only one at the table not about equipment — is *"cultivate her relationship with [[Mr. Cat|Mr. Cat]],"* clarified as *"She just wants to pet him a lot."* [[Mr. Cat|Mr. Cat, Esquire]] keeps stepping in front of people and taking hits for them. She noticed, and her response is to give him an hour of not being a shield.

- **S16 — she wakes up tactical.** [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] shakes her awake on watch and before she is properly conscious she is asking *"to your left or to your right or towards like above… have they seen you?"* No grogginess, no "what?", straight to threat geometry. She notes it herself in her journal: *"That is not a thing I decided to become."* Ten sessions of front-lining have rewritten her waking reflex.
- **S16 — the job, redefined as care.** Nobody had to ask twice who walked the trap floor first. But the notable part is what she did *after* surviving it: she didn't just cross, she **mapped three-quarters of the pressure plates, walked back, and briefed the party on where to put their feet** — *"I'll explain to everybody what I see… or they could follow my path if they need to."* Going first has always been hers. **Turning it into information for other people is new.**
- **S16 — she dissents and complies at the same time.** She never believed "Val" was a problem, said so, and helped hold him anyway *because somebody had to be able to answer straight*. When it turned to needles and a bullet she said the only objection in the room — *"I was told we were just gonna restrain you"* — **and nobody engaged with it.** She is the physical instrument of an interrogation she never endorsed. This is the first real gap between Vega and her party.
- **S16 — she catches, and does not squeeze.** ⭐ The signature moment of her session is a **natural 20 to grab a fly out of the air blind**, and then **not crushing it**. Her own line: *"when I finally got proof, I still didn't squeeze."* Set against S10's spit on the corpse, this is the same instinct pointed the other way — she supplies her own witness, and this time what she wants witnessed is restraint.
- **S16 — "I am the fist," heard as a complaint for the first time.** S02's proud self-assessment comes back inverted: *"I am the fist. I have never once been the one who decides where it lands. I am starting to think that was the arrangement all along, and that nobody ever asked me if I agreed to it."* 🛑 **The party role she named for herself in session two is now the thing she is starting to resent.** Watch this.
- **S16 — her only session with zero attack rolls.** Zero weapon damage. Her entire contribution was walking a trap floor, mapping it, and holding a prisoner nobody else could hold. ⚠️ **Her role is shifting from damage to control**, and it happened in a session with no combat in it at all.
- **S16 — she keeps the sword to herself, again.** The DM tied the tomb's warning — *"only those who bleed fire may walk the path of Emberborn"* — **directly and unprompted to her sword**, in front of everyone. She said nothing: *"I did not say anything. I don't need to say everything."* Consistent with S15, where she sheathed the relic and told the party none of them could have it.
