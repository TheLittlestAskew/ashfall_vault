## Key Events

- **S16: SILVERY BARBS — the mechanical hinge of the session.** ⭐ When "[[Valerian Hellebore]]" **passed** his Charisma save against [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s **Zone of Truth**, Flux burned **Silvery Barbs** to force a reroll at the lower result — *"he has to reroll it and take the lower"* — **and he failed.** 🛑 **Everything the party learned about [[The Doppelganger]] this session flows from that one reaction.** Without it the impostor walks.
  Also: a **natural 20 Insight (→30)** in the mural corridor that established the party was being watched from somewhere unfindable and that *"the air around you feels heavy with anger… like violence"*; a **Perception 25** at camp that caught the ridgeline creature **moving away**; offered **Enlarge** during the standoff (**not cast**).
  ⚠️ **He opened the bag of holding to show a superior officer his dead colleague — and could not remember her name.** He argued for **killing** the prisoner. Level **10 → 12** ⚑ off-screen.

- **S15: THE EYES OF THE PARTY, AND SHE NEARLY DIED FOR IT.** Spotted the hidden chamber on **passive Perception** (*"one of the walls in the room looks off. It just— it's funky"*) and warned everyone — *"hopefully it's not another mimic."* Rolled the Insight (21) clearing [[Eiluned Lulu Denulie|Lulu]] of being a mimic, and asked to see her teeth. In the mine she used **Mold Earth** on every gem vein to strip the surrounding earth, **giving the entire party advantage** on the Strength checks — the single most efficient action of the session.
  🛑 **She almost died.** A [[Boilborn]] **Rupture Burst** hit her for **30**, and she said afterward: *"If I wouldn't have had the Tough feat, I would have been dead."* Two natural 1s on the night, one of them a Finger Guns that hit [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] for 5 instead.
- **S14: SHE DUG DEANNA OUT, AND SHE TOOK THE DAGGERS.** Used **Mold Earth** to pull back the collapsed tunnel and recover [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s body, then rolled the **Arcana (17, with Zelda's assist d6)** that explained why Revivify was failing — the cursed grave-dust. Her new bow put **10 feet of knockback and a 10-foot speed reduction** on Mimic D. **Misty Stepped** the rope bridge.
  ⚠️ **She claimed both of Deanna's daggers** — magical, goddess-blessed, undamaged by the collapse — including one she had originally given her. She was direct about it and about her reasoning: *"I don't know if she would want you to have her stuff, but I'm taking her stuff."* She was also the one who broke the news to the rest of the party, and who apologised to Lulu for the group's coldness.
- **S13: the ritual, and the contradiction.** 🛑 Recorded as casting **`Rite of the Dawning Radiance`** as a ritual, granting the party **Sunward Blessing** (necrotic resistance, advantage vs. charm/fear/vampiric domination, +1d6 radiant on weapon attacks). ⚠️ **S14's opening ten minutes contradict this** — the spell is established as **5th level**, only visible at 1st through a D&D Beyond shared-content glitch, and Flux says she is **not** taking it. Sunward Blessing is never referenced again. **Unresolved; neither note has been amended.** See [[DM Questions — Open]].
- **S11: THE SCOUT WHO SAW IT COMING — and a new project.** Opened the session with the only successful read of the pre-ambush ground: a **natural 20 Perception for 30**, pinning down **four sets of tracks, laid recently, heading the way the party had to go** — and told everyone immediately. Fought the whole encounter as chip damage and mobility (Witch Bolt kept up across rounds, bow, **Finger Guns**, a bonus-action disengage, and one **Sneak Attack for 15** — *"He's looking pretty fucked up"*), and finished with **no kills**. Also **took zero damage all session**: the only nat 20 aimed at him was answered with a reaction **Shield** (AC → 22). Answered a telepath in kind — *"And then if he can read our mind, I'm saying, or maybe a little stupid."* **The session's real development is the downtime project:** he confirmed the vampire plan is **transformation, not disguise** — *"Well, it's not disguising, it's— I would turn into one"* — and the DM set the terms on the record: **dissect and learn the anatomy** (*"especially this one since this one has eyes and the normal ones do not"*), the party may **take a body back to the doctor**, it works *"similar to"* Druid Wild Shape, and **"you keep your normal stats."** [[Valerian Hellebore]] volunteered to help. This is the S02 beast-thrall-corpse study, four sessions dormant, finally becoming a mechanic. ⚑ **The whispering that said his name in S10 was not mentioned once this session.** Still carrying the jade ball, the great-cat skull, and the entire unread restricted section.
- **S10: SOMEONE IS SAYING HIS NAME.** Fought from **on top of the bookcases** all night — missed Shithead with the bow (11), tagged him with **Finger Guns** (23→7), then **killed Shirley** with a 22-damage bow shot (19→7+15) and clipped Taint (14→7). ⚑ Asked "Are *they* within 5 feet of Zelda?" then "I hit the right one" — **Shirley may be a matched pair.** Looted **compulsively during combat** — "anything I can get my hands on" — spending a full turn on the bag of holding and walking out with **the entire restricted section's books and scrolls**, plus diamonds, rubies and emeralds. Took the **jade ball seated in a big-cat skull** *because* it sounded cursed: "the way that you said that, no, but I'm going to." **Identified** it — no magic, "just a plain skull," severed at the neck, from an animal once abundant in the area (see [[Unknown Great Cat]]). **Then the hook:** the DM asked for a Perception check "for no particular reason," Flux rolled **22 with advantage**, and heard whispering from an empty space to his left — then, turning back, **heard it again, clearer, saying his name.** He doesn't know where it's coming from. He asked [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]] if he heard it, got "Don't go into the light," and **chose to ignore it**: *"If it keeps happening, then I'll worry about it."* Also the only person at the table who swore **zero** times this session.
