## Death & Revival

**How it happened (S14).** While the rest of the party fought two mimics in the [[Lantern Spine]], Deanna was alone in the parallel **bypass tunnel** with [[Mr. Cat]], **firing her shotgun at the walls to widen it** so the others could follow — shouting encouragement down a corridor where nobody was standing: *"Don't worry guys, I almost got this open."* The DM called for a d20, then a Dex save. She rolled a **natural 1**.

> "All your blasting and hammering away at things trying to widen the tunnel have caused it to collapse on top of you. You try to reach out for the end of the tunnel but are unsuccessful because you slip on the dirt beneath you... and piece by piece a rock falls."

Her bones were crushed to dust inside intact skin — *"you go to lift her up and it's like—"* she folds. [[Mr. Cat]] made his own Dex save and escaped, *"but it cost him one of his 9 lives."*

**Why Revivify failed.** [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] held two 150gp diamonds against her chest like defibrillator paddles and cast **Revivify**. Nothing happened — no magic left her. She wiped her hands, wiped the diamonds, dragged Deanna clear of the passage and cast it again. Nothing. An Arcana check (17, with Zelda feeding [[Flux]] what the failed casting *felt* like as an assist d6) and [[Samothy Smith-Wesson]]'s Detect Magic gave the answer: **it is not magic, it is curse** — seven of them, one per body ground into that dust, fused into a single layered thing. *"Curse is like an onion is what you're saying?"* — *"Yep."*
