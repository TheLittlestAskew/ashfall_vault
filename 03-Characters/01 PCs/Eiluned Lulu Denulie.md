## Build Notes

- 🛑 **The subclass changed between her first and second sessions, out of character.** In S14 she introduces herself in-fiction as **"Circle of the Land."** In S15 she is a **Wildfire Druid**, summoning a Wildfire Spirit. Called out at the table by [[Valerian Hellebore]]'s player — *"didn't this bitch say she was a fucking Circle of the Land?"* — answered *"No, I changed it."* The DM: *"She didn't have access to all the stuff for whatever reason, so we refreshed it all and she had it all today."*
  ⚠️ **This is a sheet fix, not an in-fiction event.** Recommended treatment: **Wildfire is canon; the S14 line is a build artefact.** See [[DM Questions — Open]].
- **Wildfire Spirit** — summoned as a bonus action, takes its turn after hers, attacks with **Flame Seed**, and can **Fiery Teleportation** an ally out of danger. She used the teleport to pull [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] out of a grapple zone in S15.
- ⚑ **`Pins and Needles`** — a Constitution save (DC 15) that shuts down a limb and causes searing pain but deals **no damage**. **Not in any published book I can verify.** Homebrew or DM-granted.
- **Mass Cure Wounds** cast at both 5th level (32 HP, S14) and 5th level again (20 HP, S15).
- ⚠️ **She is the party's only real healer**, and she has hit 0 HP twice in two sessions.

## Items

## Key Events

- **S15: DEMOLISHED, TWICE — and the reason the party survived anyway.** Her second session and the DM's own summary was *"Lulu got demolished."* A **[[Boilborn]] Rupture Burst** (DC 16 Con) hit her, [[Flux]] and [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] for 30/30/15 and dropped her; a **natural 1** on the follow-up Dex save finished the job. She was revived by [[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda]] (Cure Wounds at 3rd + Bardic Inspiration), went down a **second** time, and was brought back again. In between she carried the party's entire healing load — **Mass Cure Wounds at 5th for 20 HP to everyone**, plus Flame Blade, **Burning Hands at 4th for 27**, Pins and Needles, and the Wildfire Spirit doing chip damage and hauling Zelda out of a grapple. **Two natural 20 Strength checks** in the mine afterwards, which the DM rewarded with gems and [[Vega Bloodroot]] with *"a nod from Vega. You see pride on her face."*
  ⚠️ **The table's reaction to her going down was a punchline, not a silence** — *"She's with Deanna now."* · *"She was only here for a few minutes."* · *"We hardly knew her."* [[Valerian Hellebore]] announced he'd *"start picking over her corpse,"* declined to let anyone spend a potion (*"it's fine, it's her time"*), and clarified that he cast Cure Wounds on her *"with my foot on her."* She was also volunteered, unprompted, to **walk into the unexplored corridor first**, and to **bleed on the sigils** — she cut her own palm without arguing either time.
- **S14: ARRIVED.** Encountered at the [[Old Shrine]] by [[Valerian Hellebore]] while he circled the statue. Cleared of being a mimic ([[Flux]], Insight 21) and of being a vampire — she checked her own arms for puncture marks, grinned to show her teeth, and offered *"I like garlic too, so I really don't think I could be a vampire."*
  She had been **abandoned in the catacombs by her previous party** — *"They turned down a hall and then took off, and I got lost, and they left without me"* — and could not find the way out. 🛑 She admitted her interest in the [[Watcher's Pit]] was to *"take a short nap off a long cliff,"* and that the party turning up changed her mind. [[Flux]] redirected her to come along through the [[Grate of the Veins]] instead.
  She offered **Revivify** for [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]] before learning about the seven curses. Rolled **Initiative 14** and closed her first session with **Mass Cure Wounds for 32** across the party in the water elemental fight.

## Relationships

- 🛑 **[[Valerian Hellebore]] — hostile, and it is entirely one-sided.** He disliked her on sight. Chase played the reason out loud in S14: Val is **threatened that she will replace him**, *"he doesn't like the idea of not being needed or wanted. Especially since he has no parents anymore."* In S15 he escalated — proposed using her blood on the sigils, suggested she walk into danger first, looted her while she was dying, and healed her with his foot on her. ⚑ **She has not retaliated once.**
- **[[Barrett Grimmskar]]** — the only PC to check on the friction. Offered Val two options for her: *"if she's not good at what she does, she probably needs somebody who can teach her to be better. Or we can use her as bait."*
- **[[Zelda Z Whipper|Zelda "Z" Whipper]]** — has now saved her life twice in one session. The closest thing to a bond she has.
- **[[Flux]]** — brought her into the party, gave her [[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]'s earpiece, and apologised for the group's coldness (*"we just had a friend get crushed"*).
- **[[Deanna Smith-Wesson]]** — never met. She replaced her, arrived carrying her absence, and inherited her earpiece.
