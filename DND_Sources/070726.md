1. Gadget 1 Static‑Step Insoles Thin leather insoles lined with crackling blue runes. Effect: Once per long rest, the wearer can activate them to gain +10 ft movement speed for 1 minute. Their footsteps become nearly silent. Downside: At the end of the effect, the wearer releases a loud pop of static energy, potentially alerting nearby creatures.

2. Gadget 2 Tinker’s Emergency Un-Jammer A spring-loaded multi-tool. Effect: Instantly un-jams a mechanical device, lock, or contraption. Works even on magical devices with mechanical components. Downside: Roll a d6 — on a 5–6, it un-jams something else nearby instead.
3. Gadget 3 Magnetic Misbehaver
	A palm-sized magnet with a switch.
	Effect: Activate to magnetize or demagnetize metal objects within 10 ft for 1 minute. Can disarm enemies, stick weapons to walls, or secure gear.
	Downside: Roll a d8 — on an 8, the magnet targets you instead, pulling small metal objects toward your body.
